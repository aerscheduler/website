Directives in one list, and the **AD library** searches every AD the Federal Register has

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Technicians</Roles>

## Fleet ADs

With **Find them for me** chosen under <Where app="web">Settings → Airworthiness
Directives</Where>, **Fleet ADs** shows the ADs that name each aircraft's airframe, engine or
propeller, the same list each aircraft's **ADs** tab shows, for the whole fleet at once. A shop's
customer aircraft are included.

Use **Group by** to switch between two ways of reading it:

- **Aircraft**: each aircraft, then its ADs under **To review**, **Applies** and **Not
  applicable**. Answers "what does this aircraft still need decided?"
- **Status**: each AD once, with the aircraft it names beneath it. Answers "which of our
  aircraft does this AD touch?"

Choose a row to read the AD beside the list. The **…** on a row, or **Decide** at the foot of
the AD, marks it **Applies** (track it as an inspection, or nothing to track) or **Not
applicable** with a reason, or sends it **Back to review**. See [Review the ADs for an
aircraft](/docs/maintenance/review-the-ads-for-an-aircraft) for what each answer means.

The search box searches AD numbers, titles, makers, subjects and tail numbers, and the
**Aircraft** filter narrows the list to the tails you pick.

## The AD library

**AD library** lists every AD published since 1995, newest first, and checks for new ones daily.
Type in the search box to find:

- **An AD by number**, whole or by its start: `2013-11-11`, or `2013-11` for every AD in that
  issue.
- **Words anywhere in it**: a maker, a model, a part. `lycoming crankshaft` finds the ADs that
  mention both.

The **Type** filter narrows it to airplanes, engines, propellers and so on. Choose an AD to read
it laid out as the Federal Register prints it. When the organization finds ADs for you, **On your
aircraft** lists the aircraft it names and where each one stands.

## When a new AD is published

AerScheduler checks the Federal Register every day. When a new AD names one of your aircraft,
owners, admins and technicians are told by email and push, and it appears in the inbox. The
message names the AD and the aircraft, and opens that aircraft's ADs (or **Fleet ADs** when it
names more than one).

To stop these, turn off **New ADs for our aircraft** on <Where app="web">Notification
settings</Where>, or **New ADs** under <Where app="mobile">Settings → Notifications</Where> in the
app. They come only while the organization has **Find them for me** on.

<Callout type="important">
An AD the library lists against an aircraft is one worth reading, not one that applies. ADs from
before 1995 are not in the library at all.
</Callout>
