<Summary>
An inspection carrying the **Grounds the aircraft** flag grounds its tail the moment it
comes due, with the reason "Maintenance". Signing off the last such inspection puts the
aircraft back on the line by itself. Nothing else in AerScheduler grounds an aircraft on
its own.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Technicians</Roles>

## One switch, one behavior

Every recurring and one-off inspection carries a **Grounds the aircraft** switch. With it
on, the day that inspection comes due:

- The aircraft is grounded with the reason **Maintenance**.
- Every booking type except maintenance is refused on it with "Resource is grounded."
- The tail sorts to the top of <Where app="web">Maintenance → By aircraft</Where> with a Grounded badge.
- The inspection row itself carries a red **Grounds** flag in the work queue.

With it off, the inspection still comes due, still shows as overdue, and still notifies your
admins and technicians. The aircraft simply stays bookable.

## Turn it on for an inspection

The switch is on the Recurring and One-off forms in
<Where app="web">Maintenance → Add inspections</Where>. Afterwards it shows as a red
**Grounds** badge on the rule's row under
<Where app="web">Maintenance → Set up</Where>.

## Which of the standard inspections ground

Of the seven AVIATES presets, exactly two: the **Annual inspection** and the **100-hour
inspection**.

VOR check, Airworthiness Directive review, transponder check, ELT, static system and
altimeter, oil change and ELT battery replacement all come due without grounding anything.

## What an automatic grounding looks like

The same as any other grounding, with one tell: the reason reads exactly "Maintenance"
rather than something a person typed.

<Screenshot id="aircraft-grounded-banner" />

<Callout type="warning">
Grounding does not cancel the bookings already on that aircraft. They stay on the dispatch
board looking perfectly normal, and nobody on them is notified. They refuse at ramp out,
which is a bad moment to find out. When a tail grounds itself, check the board for the rest
of the day and move those flights.
</Callout>

## An hour-based grounding waits for a close-out

Calendar inspections come due on their own, around the clock. Hour-based ones do not: the
countdown only moves when a flight is closed out on that aircraft, and the grounding happens
at the same moment.

So a 100-hour that is already past due on screen will not have grounded the aircraft yet if
nobody has closed out a flight on it since. Correcting the tach by hand under
<Where app="web">Aircraft → the tail → Edit</Where> does not trigger it either. More on
this in
[Troubleshooting inspections and squawks](/docs/maintenance/troubleshooting-inspections-and-squawks).

## Coming back off the ground

Sign the inspection off and the aircraft returns to service by itself, as long as nothing
else is still holding it. Every overdue inspection on the tail counts, not only the ones
that ground, so an overdue oil change will keep it on the ground until that is signed off
too.

Deleting the rule, or detaching that tail from it, clears the grounding the same way.

<Callout type="important">
This only works when the grounding reason is exactly "Maintenance", which only the automatic
grounding writes. If somebody grounded the aircraft by hand and typed a reason, no amount of
signing off will lift it. An admin has to click **Return to service**.
</Callout>

## Reminders created during setup all ground

The maintenance step offered while you are setting the school up creates every reminder as
a grounding one, including the oil change.

If you set your fleet up that way, open
<Where app="web">Maintenance → Set up</Where> afterwards and look at which rows carry the
red **Grounds** badge. An oil change taking a trainer off the line mid-morning is the
support email this causes.

## The switch defaults differently on web and on the phone

| Where you create it | **Grounds the aircraft** starts |
|---|---|
| Console, Add inspections, Recurring or One-off | Off |
| iOS app, Add inspection | On |
| The maintenance step during setup | On |

Check it before you save, wherever you are.
