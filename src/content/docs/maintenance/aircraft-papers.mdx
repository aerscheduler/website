## Put a POH or W&amp;B on a tail

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open the aircraft">
    <Where app="web">Aircraft</Where>, then the tail. On the phone, open the same aircraft
    from Resources.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Open Papers">
    On the console the Papers section is in the left rail. On the phone it is a section on
    the aircraft page. Staff always see it. Members only see it when there is something
    they can open.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Add the file">
    Category first. The form opens on whichever of **Airworthiness certificate** and
    **Registration certificate** is still missing, else on POH. On the phone, Category and Who
    can open it each open a picker in the same sheet. Attach a jpg, png, or pdf, up to
    25 MB. You can keep up to five files in each category. On the iPhone, use **Take photo**,
    **Choose from Photos**, or **Browse Files**. The photo shows on the aircraft once it
    is saved. Tap it to zoom. A PDF shows as a document card.
    Chrome on a Mac may not read a raw HEIC; export JPEG from Photos instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The certificate and the registration

Every US aircraft has to carry an airworthiness certificate and a registration certificate
(14 CFR 91.203). Until the aircraft has a copy of both on file, staff see a line at the top of
Papers saying which is missing: "No airworthiness certificate or registration on file", or just
the one. It is a reminder, not a block: nothing stops the aircraft being booked.

| Category | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Airworthiness certificate | A photo or scan of the certificate, FAA Form 8100-2 (standard) or 8130-7 (special). |
| Registration certificate | The current certificate of aircraft registration. Track when it expires with the [Registration renewal](/docs/maintenance/add-the-standard-airworthiness-inspections) inspection. |
| Operating limitations | The separate operating limitations some aircraft carry, such as an experimental or light-sport aircraft. On most aircraft they are in the POH. |
| POH, Weight and balance | The current ones, not a history. |
| Insurance, Form 337, Logbook, Other | Whatever else the shop keeps for the tail. |

## Who can open what

POH and weight and balance default to **Bookers**, so a student opening that aircraft (or
booking it) can read them. The certificates, operating limitations, insurance, 337s and
logbook scans default to **Staff**. Staff can flip any of them.

Owners, admins, dispatchers, and technicians can add or remove files. Instructors,
students, and renters can only open the booker-visible ones.

## On a customer's aircraft

Nobody books a customer's aircraft, so its papers ask **Keep to the shop** or **Show to the
owner** instead. A paper shown to the owner is on their aircraft's page when they sign in, and
**Keep to the shop** on the paper hides it again. Your own aircraft have no owners to show
papers to, so the choice is not offered there. See
[Work on a customer's aircraft](/docs/maintenance/work-on-a-customers-aircraft).

## Booking

When you pick an aircraft on <Where app="web">Book</Where> (or on the phone when you create
a reservation), a Papers control appears if that tail has files you can see. It does not
block the booking.

## What this is not

- These are the **current** papers, not a history vault. Replacing the W&amp;B means removing
  the old file and adding the new one.
- Inspection sign-off attachments stay on the compliance record. They are not copied here.
- Squawk photos stay on the squawk. They cannot be deleted. Papers can.

<Callout type="warning">
A wrong weight and balance left on the tail is worse than a missing one. If a file is
stale, remove it.
</Callout>
