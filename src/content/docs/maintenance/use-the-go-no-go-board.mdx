## Work the no-go list

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Go / No-Go">
    <Where app="web">Go / No-Go</Where> shows separate counts and lists for grounded
    aircraft and grounded members.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Open the item that needs attention">
    Click a count to open the filtered Aircraft or People list. Click a named aircraft or
    member to open that record directly. The card includes the grounding reason when one
    was recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Resolve it on the full record">
    The Go / No-Go board is a triage view. Return an aircraft to service from its Aircraft
    page. Reinstate a member from their People record. The available controls follow your
    role and permissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the board counts

**No-go items** is the total of grounded aircraft and grounded members. Grounding blocks
normal booking or participation until an authorized person clears it.

**Currencies tracked** counts the rules your school has configured, such as a medical,
flight review, or aircraft checkout. Click a rule to see every member on it, separated
into current, expiring, expired, and not signed off, with its document evidence.

<Callout type="note">
Open Reports for the Pilot currency and Documents reports when you need an exportable
school-wide view across every rule.
</Callout>

## Manage currency rules

Owners and admins can click **Manage currency rules** to open the Currency rules section in
Settings directly. Dispatchers can see the rules being tracked but cannot change school
settings.

A currency rule defines who it applies to, when it expires, and how it renews. Changing a
rule can change who is considered current, so review its groups and aircraft scope before
saving.

Dispatchers can open each rule's detail page and work its member roster. They only receive
the Sign off or Renew action when that rule allows dispatcher renewal.

## Why an item is not here

The board shows records that are explicitly grounded. An open squawk does not ground an
aircraft by itself, and a maintenance reminder only grounds a tail when its rule has
**Grounds the aircraft** turned on and the reminder becomes due.
