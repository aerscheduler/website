<Summary>
Open the aircraft, then **History**: every inspection signed off, squawk resolved and work order
completed on it, newest first, each opening its own record. **Invoices**, beside it, lists every
bill for the aircraft. Both work the same for your own aircraft and a customer's.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Technicians</Roles>

## The history

<Where app="web">Aircraft → the aircraft → History</Where>. One row per thing done to it:

| Row | Date | Signed or done by | Hobbs and tach |
|---|---|---|---|
| An inspection signed off | The compliance date | The mechanic who signed, with their certificate | As recorded at the sign-off |
| A squawk resolved | When it was resolved | Who resolved it | Not recorded on a squawk |
| A work order completed | When it was completed | The job's technicians | Out, or in when nobody recorded out |

Click a row to open the inspection, the squawk or the work order. Filter by kind with the filter
button. The list pages like every table in AerScheduler, so a tail with twenty years of
records is still quick to open.

A **one-time inspection** (a single AD compliance, say) leaves the Maintenance tab once it is
signed off, because nothing is coming due any more. Its sign-off is kept for the life of the
aircraft, so it is here.

<Callout type="note">
Work orders in the history show only to owners, admins and technicians, the people who work on
jobs. A dispatcher's History tab lists the sign-offs and squawks.
</Callout>

Instructors, students and renters do not see the History tab. They see the open squawks on an
aircraft of yours they can book.

## Invoices

<Where app="web">Aircraft → the aircraft → Invoices</Where>. Every bill tied to the aircraft:
for the bookings that flew it (each payer's share of a split booking is its own invoice), and
for the shop's work orders on it. Each row says who it is billed to, what it is for (the booking
or the job's WO number), the amount and whether it is paid, outstanding, overdue, refunded or
void. Click a column header to sort, and a row to open the invoice on Billing.

Only owners and admins see this tab, the same people who see Billing: it lists what your
members and customers paid.

## What the aircraft's owner sees

An owner who has signed in sees a shorter history on their aircraft's page: the inspections
signed off and the jobs the shop finished. Not squawks, not who on your side did the work, and
nothing you keep to the shop. See
[See your aircraft and approve work](/docs/maintenance/see-your-aircraft-and-approve-work).
