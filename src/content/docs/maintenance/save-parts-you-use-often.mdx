<Summary>
Save the oil, filters, gaskets, supplies and fees your shop uses every week, then pick them onto
a work order instead of typing them. The part number, vendor and cost fill in, and the price
follows the job's markup, so the same part is charged the same way every time.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Technicians</Roles>

## Where they are

<Where app="web">Maintenance → Saved parts</Where>, under Work orders. Parts are grouped by kind:
Parts, Shop supplies, Outside work, Freight and shipping, Fees and Other. Search by name, part
number or vendor. **Used** counts how many job lines were picked from each one.

## Start from what you have already used

The first time you open Saved parts, **Add from past jobs** offers every part already put on a
work order and not saved yet, one per part number, with the latest cost (or the price, when the
line was priced by hand) and how often it was used. Untick any you do not want and press
**Save**. A part number already saved is skipped.

This needs **Shop pricing**: owners and admins have it, and you can give it to a lead technician.

## Save a part

Press **Save a part**, or tick **Save to parts list** when you add a part to a job that is not
saved yet.

- **Name** becomes the line's description.
- **Part number**: one saved part per number, so "20W-50" cannot be saved twice by accident.
- **Cost each** is what the shop pays, for parts and outside work. When the part is picked it is
  marked up by **that job's** markup: the job's own, else the customer's, else the shop's
  markup in Shop rates. A customer you bill at cost still pays cost.
- **Fixed price each** (optional) charges a set price instead of cost plus markup, for example
  oil sold by the quart. Supplies, freight and fees have only a price.
- **Sales tax** follows your organization's rule unless you choose Taxable or Not taxable.

Any technician can save a part with its cost. Setting a fixed price on a part, choosing its tax,
changing a saved part or archiving one needs **Shop pricing**.

## Pick one onto a job

On a work order, add a part (or a supply or fee). **Saved part** at the top of the form lists the
shop's parts; type to search. Picking one fills the description, part number, vendor, cost,
price and tax. Change the quantity, serial number, status or anything else before adding it.
The phone app has the same **Saved part** row when you add a part.

A technician without Shop pricing can pick any saved part, including one with a fixed price or a
tax choice: those are the part's own values, not a price they set.

## Changing a saved part never changes a job

Each line on a job keeps its own copy of the part. Changing a saved part's cost or price, or
archiving it, changes what the **next** pick charges. Lines already on jobs, and invoices
already raised, stay exactly as they were.

**Archive** a part you no longer stock. It disappears from the picker and frees its part number;
**Archived** at the top of the list shows them, and you can bring one back.
