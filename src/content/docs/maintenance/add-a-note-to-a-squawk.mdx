<Summary>
Open the squawk and use the **Notes** card. On the console, type in the box and click **Add
note**. In the iOS app, tap **Add Note**. It records what is happening without closing the
squawk, and a note cannot be edited or deleted once it is written.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Technicians</Roles>

## Why this is not the same as resolving

A squawk sits open for days. The part is on order, the hangar is full, the fault would not
repeat on the ground. None of that means the defect is fixed, and none of it belongs in the
Resolve form, because resolving a squawk says the aircraft is right again.

Notes are where that goes. Each one carries who wrote it and when, and they sit in the
order they were written, so the squawk reads as an account of the work rather than a status
that jumped from open to closed.

## Add one

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open the squawk">
    <Where app="web">Maintenance → Squawks → Open</Where>, then click the row. The notes
    are on the panel that slides in, and on the squawk's own page.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Write it">
    Type into the box under the thread. Up to 2000 characters. On the console,
    <kbd>Cmd</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and <kbd>Enter</kbd> posts it; plain <kbd>Enter</kbd>
    is a new line, because these run long.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Click Add note">
    It appears at the bottom of the thread under your name and the time. Everyone looking
    at the squawk sees the same thread.

    <Screenshot id="squawk-notes-thread" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the app, the squawk's detail page has the same thread, with **Add Note** under it and in
the gear menu at the top.

## Notes cannot be edited or deleted

<Callout type="important">
Nobody can change or remove a note once it is written, including us. Correct a mistake by
adding another note saying what was wrong. This is the same rule as compliance records, and
for the same reason: a record of work on an aircraft that can be quietly rewritten
afterwards is worth less than no record at all.
</Callout>

## Who can write, and who can read

Adding a note takes the same permission as verifying or resolving one: admins, owners and
technicians. Everyone else who can open the squawk can read the thread but has no box to
type in.

That includes pilots. Somebody who reports a squawk on a tail and can open it again later
sees what maintenance has written about it, which is usually the point: "part ordered, ETA
Friday" is exactly what the person who reported the flickering light wants to know. Write
notes as though the pilot will read them, because they can.

## Notes do not notify anybody

Adding a note sends nothing. It is a record, not a message. If somebody needs to know
about it today, tell them, and let the note be the thing they read afterwards.

## Sign-off notes are a different box

The **Resolve** form has its own notes field, and that one is not part of this thread. It is
a single paragraph describing what was done to clear the squawk, there is only ever one of
it, and a later sign-off overwrites it. On the squawk's page it appears under **Sign-off
notes**, separately, and it is capped at 500 characters rather than 2000.

Use it for the closing statement. Use notes for everything on the way there. See
[Resolve a squawk](/docs/maintenance/resolve-a-squawk).
