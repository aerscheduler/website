## Mark an invoice paid after cash, check or bank transfer

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Find the invoice on Billing">
Use the date range and the Status filter to narrow the list, or open the invoice from the
Recent invoices panel on the Dashboard.
</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Click Mark paid">
It is in the row menu on the invoice table, and on the invoice detail panel itself.

<Screenshot id="invoice-detail-panel" />
</Step>
</Steps>

What that does:

- The invoice flips to Paid here, and in Stripe it is recorded as paid out of band.
- Who marked it paid is recorded on the invoice's audit trail.
- The member gets a Payment received email and push notification.
- If QuickBooks sync is on, a Sales Receipt is created. See
  [Send paid invoices to QuickBooks Online](/docs/billing/send-paid-invoices-to-quickbooks).
- If the member was grounded for unpaid invoices and this takes them under the threshold,
  they are released automatically.

Two things can stop you:

- **"You cannot pay an invoice that has already been voided."** A voided invoice is
  finished. Raise a new one instead.
- **Stripe could not be reached.** You get a warning rather than an error, saying the
  invoice was marked paid here but Stripe could not be updated. The invoice is correct in
  AerScheduler; go and mark it paid in Stripe too, or the two will not agree.

## Void an invoice

Voiding is for an invoice that should never have existed: a duplicate, a booking billed to
the wrong person, a flight that never happened.

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Open the invoice and click Void">
Also available from the row menu on the Billing table.
</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Confirm">
The dialog says this marks the invoice as void and that it cannot be undone. It means it.
</Step>
</Steps>

The invoice is voided here and in Stripe, who voided it is recorded, and from then on it
stops counting toward the Outstanding total, toward the grounding threshold, and toward
every financial report.

<Callout type="warning">
You cannot void an invoice that has already been paid: "You cannot void an invoice that has
already been paid." Voiding is not an undo for a payment. Once money has moved, the only
correction is a refund in Stripe.
</Callout>

## Voiding a past flight's only invoice

If the invoice you are voiding is the only one on a booking that already flew and was
never canceled, voiding it would leave that flight with no invoice at all. AerScheduler
asks first instead of doing that silently:

- **Void and leave unbilled** voids it and stops there. The flight shows up wherever your
  unbilled work is tracked, for you to bill later by hand.
- **Void and open close-out** voids it and takes you straight to the booking so you can
  correct the close-out and raise a new invoice in the same visit.
- **Cancel** backs out and leaves the invoice exactly as it was.

Voiding an invoice that is not a past flight's only invoice, such as a duplicate on a
booking with another live invoice, skips this and just voids.

## Refunds happen in your Stripe dashboard

There is no refund action in AerScheduler. Not in the web console, not in the iOS app, not
in the API.

To refund somebody, sign in to your school's own Stripe dashboard, find the payment, and
refund it there.

## What a refund does not change in AerScheduler

<Callout type="important">
A refund made in Stripe is not read back into AerScheduler. The invoice stays Paid, it
keeps counting as revenue in the Revenue and Payments received reports, and if QuickBooks
sync is on, the Sales Receipt stays in QuickBooks. Nothing here will tell you the money
went back.
</Callout>

Keep your own note of refunds and correct them on your own books. If you refund often
enough that this hurts, the practical habit is to record the refund in QuickBooks by hand
on the same day you issue it in Stripe.

## Voiding one share of a split booking

On a booking split between several people, each payer holds their own invoice.

Voiding one of them does not hand that share to the others. Their invoices are unchanged,
and the booking becomes partially billed rather than unbilled.

<Callout type="warning">
**Create invoice** on the booking will not fill that gap, on either surface. It is hidden
while anybody on the booking still holds a live invoice, which is exactly the state you are
in. Raise a [manual invoice](/docs/billing/create-a-manual-invoice) for that one payer
instead. It only comes back if you void every invoice on the booking.
</Callout>

## Who is allowed to do this

| Role | Mark paid | Void | Send reminder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Admin | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Dispatcher | No | No | No |
| Instructor | No | No | No |
| Student, renter | No | No | No |

Dispatchers cannot open the Billing page at all. Full detail in
[Who can see and do what in billing](/docs/billing/who-can-do-what-in-billing).
