<Summary>
Billing is an owner and admin area. Two things need the organization owner specifically:
saving <Where app="web">Settings → Billing</Where>, and connecting Stripe or QuickBooks.
Everyone else sees only their own invoices, or the invoice for a flight they were on.
</Summary>

## The short version

- **Owner**: everything, including the two owner-only areas below.
- **Admin**: everything else in billing. Every invoice, every report, all the rules.
- **Dispatcher**: can close a flight out and fill in who pays what. Cannot open Billing.
- **Instructor**: the same, plus billing a guest flight they instructed.
- **Student and renter**: their own invoices and their own cards.
- **Technician**: no money at all.

<Callout type="warning">
An admin who is not the organization owner can open <Where app="web">Settings → Billing</Where>,
edit every field, press **Save changes**, and be told they are not authorized to make the
request. Nothing on the screen says the page is owner-only. If you are an admin and the
save is refused, the owner has to make the change.
</Callout>

## Owner

The owner is an admin plus four things nobody else can do:

- Turn **Billing enabled** on or off, and switch How members pay (invoice vs ledger).
- Save the Rates and fees card: default instructor rate, service fee and its label,
  overnight minimum, and the grounding threshold.
- Connect, manage and reconnect the Stripe payout account.
- Connect, configure, backfill and disconnect QuickBooks Online, and retry a single
  invoice's sync.
- Set the two "can manually generate invoices" toggles, in the **Manual invoices** card on
  <Where app="web">Settings → Billing</Where>.

The owner still cannot edit a Stripe invoice after it has been raised. On Account ledger,
owners and admins can add credit, adjust, refund prepaid balance, and reassign a flight
charge from the member's Billing tab.

## Admin

An admin runs day to day billing: the whole Billing page, manual invoices, mark paid,
void, payment reminders, every financial report, cost-splitting rules, membership plans
and every member's dues and money. An admin also gets **Override payment** and **Create
invoice** on a reservation, both on the booking's panel in the console and on the app.

What an admin cannot do is turn Billing enabled on or off, switch How members pay, save
Rates and fees, connect Stripe or QuickBooks,
or change a Stripe invoice's amount once it exists. On Account ledger they can manage the
member ledger (credit, adjust, refund, reassign) from People.

## Dispatcher

A dispatcher runs the ramp, not the books. They can ramp any booking out and in, fill in
the Who pays what panel, and see the invoice raised for a reservation they are closing
out. On the booking's panel they also get **Override payment** and **Correct times**, both
on either surface. They do not get **Create invoice**: a dispatcher can close a flight out
and cannot bill it.

<Callout type="note">
A dispatcher who opens <Where app="web">Billing</Where> is sent back to their own home
page. The organization-wide invoice list is admin-only.
</Callout>

They cannot mark an invoice paid, void one, see any financial report, or see anyone's
membership. They can create a manual invoice only if the school has switched on
"Dispatchers can manually generate invoices". The owner sets that toggle in the console, on
<Where app="web">Settings → Billing</Where>. Raising the invoice itself is still an app
job: the console's **New invoice** button stays owner and admin only.

## Instructor

An instructor can ramp out and in and fill in Who pays what on bookings they are on, and
can [close out and bill a guest or discovery flight](/docs/billing/bill-a-discovery-or-guest-flight)
they instructed, including viewing that guest's invoice.

With "Instructors can override reservation prices" switched on, they can also override the
instruction and resource rate on a booking, from **Override payment** on the reservation
panel in the console or from the app. Note that adding an override clears every sign-off
already collected.

<Callout type="note">
An instructor's copy of that setting is read from their session. Switch it on and they see
**Override payment** the next time they sign in, not the moment you save.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
That toggle is not limited to an instructor's own bookings. Switched on, any instructor can
override the rates on any booking in the organization, not only the ones they are flying.
</Callout>

They cannot open Billing, see other members' invoices or balances, see any financial
report, or mark anything paid.

## Student and renter

Identical for billing purposes. Both can:

- See their own invoices, and their outstanding balance on their home page.
- Pay their own invoices by card, save cards, set a default, and turn on autopay. See
  [Pay your invoice and set up autopay](/docs/billing/pay-an-invoice-and-save-a-card).
- Confirm a review with their PIN, which is what triggers the invoice.
- Fill in Who pays what on a booking they are assigned to.
- Read their own membership on their profile.

They cannot see what anybody else pays, cannot see the cost-splitting rules (those are
admin-only to read), and cannot change or cancel their own membership.

## Technician

A technician sees fleet reports and books maintenance, which is deliberately never
invoiced. They see no money anywhere, including on the aircraft record.

## Settings that change what a role can do

| Setting | Where it lives | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatchers can manually generate invoices | <Where app="web">Settings → Billing → Manual invoices</Where> | Lets a dispatcher raise a manual invoice from the iOS app |
| Instructors can manually generate invoices | <Where app="web">Settings → Billing → Manual invoices</Where> | Lets an instructor raise a manual invoice from the iOS app |
| Instructors can override reservation prices | <Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences</Where> | Lets an instructor use Override payment on a booking, on either surface |

All three are owner-only to change. The first two say who may raise a manual invoice, not
where: the console's **New invoice** button stays owner and admin only, so a dispatcher or
instructor you grant it to raises theirs from the app.

## Actions that still only exist in the iOS app

Invoicing a guest by hand. Turn on Guest recipient in Billing → New invoice, or use the
same fields on the iOS Generate Invoice screen, and enter a name and an email address.
