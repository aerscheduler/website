## What you see

<Screenshot id="person-ledger" />

The ledger (on <Where app="web">People → Ledger</Where> and on your own
<Where app="web">Billing → Ledger</Where> page) shows credit on account (positive) or amount
owed (negative), then entries. Search and filter by type or date, the same way
Operations Billing, Accounts tab, is the school-wide roster; click a row to land here.
The table itself filters like any other list. Credits render in green. **Posted by** is who
put the row on the ledger (an admin for desk credit, refund, and adjustment; the
member for a card top-up; **System** when Stripe or a job posted it). Click a row
to open the same right-hand panel reservations use. Those posts also appear under
Audit logs → Ledger. Common types:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Top-up | Member paid by card to add funds |
| Cash / Check / Other credit | Desk credit posted by an admin |
| Adjustment | Admin plus or minus with a memo |
| Flight | Close-out charge for a booking |
| Fee | Join fee, dues, enrollment fee, or a monthly late fee |
| Charge | Desk custom charge (when posted as a ledger item) |
| Refund | Money returned from a prepaid balance |
| Reversal | Undoes an earlier entry (never an edit of the original) |

Nothing on the ledger is edited in place. Corrections are new rows that reverse or replace
what went wrong.

## Add funds (member)

On their own Billing page, or when looking at themselves on People, the member clicks
**Add funds**, picks an amount, and pays by card. The balance rises by the credit they
asked for. Home and Billing show the live balance as soon as the charge lands. If the school set a
[card fee on top-ups](/docs/billing/set-a-card-fee-on-account-top-ups), the card is charged
a little more than that credit.

## Add credit, adjustment, refund (admin)

On someone else's ledger, admins get:

- **Add credit** for cash, check, or other money received at the desk (memo required).
- **Adjustment** for a signed plus or minus that is not a flight and not a top-up.
- **Refund** when the balance is positive. See [Refund](#refund).

## Refund

Refund pays the member back from a positive balance, down to $0.00, not below.

Pick a method, then an amount. You are not unwinding a whole top-up row.

- **Check / cash:** you write the check or hand them cash. AerScheduler only
  debits the ledger.
- **Original card:** pick which past card charge the money should ride back on.
  Stripe already knows that card. You can only return what is still unused on
  **that charge**, even if the account balance is higher. The amount field
  refuses anything over that remainder. To return more, pick another charge and
  refund again, or use check/cash for the full balance.

If they added funds twice (for example $25 then $50), those are two charges.
Refunding the $25 charge cannot send the other $50 home.

### Card fees on a refund

A top-up memo like "includes $6.00 card fee on charge" means the member paid
$56 to receive $50 of credit. The $6 never sat on the ledger. A refund can
return at most the $50 credit. The $6 surcharge stays on the original charge.

Stripe also keeps its own processing fee on that payment. Neither the member
nor the school gets that back from this dialog.

See [Card fee on account top-ups](/docs/billing/set-a-card-fee-on-account-top-ups).

## Auto-refill

On <Where app="web">Billing → Payment methods</Where> (and on People → Ledger for
admins), a member can turn on auto-refill. It charges the **default saved card** and
credits the ledger, the same path as Add funds.

Three modes:

- **When balance drops under a floor.** If the balance is below $X, charge $Y.
- **Pay whatever I owe.** Charge the amount owing, if it is at least $1.
- **Charge a fixed amount.** Charge $Y on the cadence even at zero.

Cadence is every day, or once a month on day 1–28. The job runs in the school's time
zone, after 4am local, so a downtime catch-up still fires that day. It tries once, then
retries two days later, twice. After three failures it pauses and emails the member.
A successful charge also emails the new balance, **and the amount charged to the card**,
which is larger than the credit when the school adds a card fee on top-ups. Both success
and pause land as an in-app notification. The ledger row memo is **Auto-refill** (not a
generic card top-up). The card is not deleted. Turn it back on after updating the card.

**On a monthly cadence the first charge waits for the date you picked.** Set it up on the
20th and choose the 1st, and nothing happens until the 1st. After that, if the job misses
the date it catches up as soon as it can.

Auto-refill needs ledger mode, a school that has connected card payments, and a default
card. If any of those is missing, the switch refuses to turn on and says which one, rather
than saving and failing quietly later. It is not ACH; that is a later slice.

If auto-refill pauses, the reason says whose problem it is. "The saved card was declined"
means the card. "This school has not finished setting up card payments" and "card payments
were unavailable" are not about the member's card at all and need nothing from them.

## Late fees

Owners set these under <Where app="web">Settings → Billing</Where> when the school is
on an account ledger. A whole percent of the amount owing, plus an optional flat
amount, posts once per member per calendar month (school time zone) after they have
stayed owing past the grace period, counted in school calendar days (default 30 days;
0 means the first eligible morning). The memo is
`Late fee YYYY-MM`, so a second sweep in the same month is a no-op.

<Screenshot id="ledger-late-fees" />

Invoice-mode schools ignore these fields. The fee is a ledger `Fee` row, not a Stripe
invoice. Open **Receipt** on that row for the line item.

When grace is longer than 7 days, the member gets a reminder email and in-app notice
about a week before the fee would post (`Late fee reminder YYYY-MM`, once). When the
fee posts they get another email with the amount and new balance, plus an in-app
"Late fee posted" notice. Add funds from Billing to avoid the next month.

## Statements

**Statement** on the ledger (your Billing page, or an admin looking at People → Ledger)
opens a date range in the **school's time zone** (not your laptop's): opening balance
(everything before the start), each entry in the period with a running total, and closing.

Print uses the same isolated print view as a receipt. **Email statement** sends it to the
member's email with a link back to Billing. This is not a Stripe invoice and does not
sync to QuickBooks.

The period cannot be longer than two years, and a range with more than 5,000 entries
asks you to narrow the dates.

## Receipts

Live **Flight**, **Fee**, and **Charge** rows offer **Receipt**. That opens the same
right-hand panel as an invoice or ledger entry: line items, the member, the school, and
the booking when there is one, with Print on the panel.

You cannot open a receipt for a top-up, a reversal row, or a charge that has already been
reversed. Use the live charge (or the new charge after a reassign) instead.

## Reassign a flight charge

Admins only. On a live **Flight** row that has not been reversed, click **Reassign**, pick
another active member, and confirm.

AerScheduler reverses the original charge and posts the same amount on the new member.
Both rows keep a memo. The booking's payer stake moves with the money, so close-out does
not try to bill the first person again.

Reassign refuses:

- Yourself as the target
- A member who already has a live charge on that same booking
- Archived or deleted members
- Rows that are not flight charges (fees and desk charges stay where they are)

## Where members look

| Screen | Who |
|---|---|
| <Where app="web">People → person → Ledger</Where> | Admins managing that member (same searchable table, plus desk credit / refund / reassign). Invoices is leftover Stripe bills. |
| <Where app="web">Billing → Ledger</Where> under the personal section | The member themselves (balance, searchable history, Add funds, receipts; no Reassign) |
| <Where app="web">Billing → Payment methods</Where> | Auto-refill and saved cards |
| iOS app Account / ledger | Balance, history, Add funds, receipts |

## Guests

Walk-ins are not on this ledger. Guest visits stay on pay-this-visit invoices. Promote
someone to a member under People if they should hold a balance going forward.
