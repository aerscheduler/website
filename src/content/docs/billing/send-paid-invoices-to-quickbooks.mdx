## Before you connect

Two things have to be true.

**You must be the organization owner.** Every part of this page is owner only. An admin who
is not the owner opens it and sees an Owner required panel. On the iOS app, Accounting
Integrations tells you to manage QuickBooks on the web.

**You need an active Product/Service item in QuickBooks.** Every line of every Sales Receipt
posts to one income item that you pick here, and you cannot pick it if it does not exist
yet. Create it in QuickBooks first if you have not: most schools use a single Flight
Revenue or Sales item.

## Connect QuickBooks

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Open Settings → Integrations → QuickBooks Online">
The page has a three-step strip across the top: Connect, Map income, Sync on. It tells you
where you are.

<Screenshot id="quickbooks-setup" />
</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Click Connect QuickBooks">
Intuit's own sign-in opens. Authorize the company you want AerScheduler to post into. Pick
carefully if you keep more than one company file.
</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Check the company name">
Once connected, the page shows the connected company's name. A sandbox company is labelled
so.
</Step>

## Choose the income item

Pick one from Income item (from QuickBooks). The list is loaded live from your Products &amp;
Services, so what you see here is what is in QuickBooks right now.

Every line of every receipt posts to this one item. There is no per-line mapping.

## Turn sync on

Switch on Sync paid invoices. The toggle cannot be turned on until an income item has been
chosen, which is the point of the three-step strip.

From that moment, an invoice is pushed as soon as it becomes paid, whether that was a card
payment, autopay, or you clicking Mark paid.

## Backfill past paid invoices

Click Sync past paid invoices to send invoices that were already paid before you connected.

A run is capped at 25 invoices. Run it again for the next batch until the activity feed
stops finding work.

## What lands in QuickBooks

One Sales Receipt per paid invoice, against a customer matched by email address, once and
only once.

Each receipt carries a private note holding the AerScheduler and Stripe references, the
member, the instructor, the flight date and the tail number, so a receipt can always be
traced back to the flight that produced it.

In AerScheduler you see the result in two places: a green tick on the invoice row in the
Billing table, and a QuickBooks section in the invoice detail panel showing the Sales
Receipt id, how long ago it synced, and Open in QBO.

<Screenshot id="invoice-detail-panel" />

<Callout type="warning">
Only paid invoices sync. Nothing about an unpaid or a voided invoice ever reaches
QuickBooks. And because AerScheduler never learns about a refund made in Stripe, refunding
somebody does not remove or reverse the Sales Receipt. See
[Mark an invoice paid, void it, or refund it](/docs/billing/mark-an-invoice-paid-void-or-refund-it).
</Callout>

## When a sync fails

Syncing fails softly and never blocks the money. If QuickBooks is unreachable when a member
pays, the payment still succeeds and the invoice is still Paid here. Only the bookkeeping
step is outstanding.

A failed invoice shows a red Failed chip with the error on the Billing list, and the invoice
detail panel offers Retry.

The Activity feed at the bottom of the QuickBooks page records every attempt, success,
skipped or error, with the invoice number and what triggered it. The most recent error is
also shown at the top of the page.

The common one is **"Invoice has no customer email for QuickBooks."** A Sales Receipt is
matched to a QuickBooks customer by email, so an invoice with no email on it cannot be
posted. Add the email to the member's record. Guest invoices without an email hit the same
wall.

If Intuit's authorization has lapsed, the page offers Reauthorize or Reconnect QuickBooks.
Use it and then retry the failed invoices from their detail panels.

## Disconnecting

Disconnect QuickBooks revokes the tokens at Intuit and deletes the stored credentials here.
Sales Receipts that already went across stay in QuickBooks; disconnecting does not reach
back into your books.

Reconnecting later starts from the setup strip again, including picking the income item.
