<Summary>
<Where app="web">Billing → New invoice</Where>. Pick the member, add line items, click
Create invoice. It is raised in Stripe and the member is emailed a link to pay it. A manual
invoice is never charged to a saved card, even when the member has autopay switched on.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers (if enabled), Instructors (if enabled)</Roles>

Use this for anything that is not a flight: a headset rental, a checkride fee, ground
school books, a fuel charge on a dry rate, or a correction you are making by hand.

Your school needs Stripe connected before any invoice can be raised. See
[Turn on invoicing and card payments](/docs/billing/turn-on-invoicing-and-card-payments).

## Create one in the web console

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Open Billing and click New invoice">
<Where app="web">Billing</Where> is in the left rail, and is visible to owners and admins
only.
</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Pick who is billed">
Search the member list, or turn on <strong>Guest recipient</strong> and enter a name and an
email address for someone who is not a member.
</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Add line items">
Each line has a Description, a Qty and a unit price. Add as many lines as you need with the
running total shown underneath.

<Screenshot id="create-invoice-dialog" />
</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Add a memo and a due date if you want them">
Both are optional. A due date matters more than it looks: it is what the automatic reminder
sweep chases against. See [Chase an unpaid invoice](/docs/billing/chase-unpaid-invoices).
</Step>

<Step n={5} title="Click Create invoice">
The invoice appears on Billing and on the member's own Invoices page, and they are emailed
and pushed a link to pay it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Create one in the iOS app

Open Billing, then Generate Invoice.


<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Choose the recipient">
Either search for a member, or enter a guest recipient with a name and an email address.
</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Add invoice items">
Tap Add + for each line: a name, a quantity and a price.
</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Add a memo and a due date">
Optional, same as the web console.
</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Tap Send Invoice" />
</Steps>

## Invoicing a guest

Turn on <strong>Guest recipient</strong> in the New invoice dialog (or use the same fields on
the iOS Generate Invoice screen). Type a name and an email address. Stripe creates a guest
customer and emails them a pay link. You cannot pick both a member and a guest on one invoice.

## Limits on line items and memos

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| Line item name | 60 characters |
| Quantity | Between 1 and 99 |
| Memo | 100 characters |
| Invoice total | More than 50 cents |

The 50 cent floor is Stripe's, not ours. Stripe will not accept an invoice below it.

## Manual invoices are never autopaid

<Callout type="important">
A manual invoice is always sent to the member to pay, even if they have a default card
saved and autopay switched on. This is deliberate: autopay exists so routine flight
invoices settle themselves, and a hand-typed invoice with an extra zero in it should not
charge somebody's card before anyone has looked at it.
</Callout>

## What a manual invoice does not do

- **It is not linked to a reservation.** A flight that was never invoiced still shows in
  the Unbilled reservations list on Billing after you type up a manual invoice for it.
- **It carries no rate breakdown.** There are no resource hours or instruction figures on
  it, so it does not appear as revenue against a particular aircraft.
- **It cannot be edited once created.** The only things you can do to an invoice afterwards
  are mark it paid and void it. See
  [Mark an invoice paid, void it, or refund it](/docs/billing/mark-an-invoice-paid-void-or-refund-it).

<Callout type="warning">
The Bill button on a row in the Unbilled reservations list opens this same New invoice
dialog, prefilled with the member and the reservation title as the memo. It is a manual
invoice, not the flight's invoice, so the booking stays in the unbilled list afterwards. To
bill the flight itself, finish its close-out:
[Why a flight was not invoiced](/docs/billing/why-a-flight-was-not-invoiced).
</Callout>

## Letting dispatchers or instructors create invoices

By default only admins can create a manual invoice. Two settings widen that, in the
**Manual invoices** card on <Where app="web">Settings → Billing</Where>:

- Dispatchers can manually generate invoices
- Instructors can manually generate invoices

They are also on the app's Billing Settings screen. Either way, only the organization owner
can save them.


<Callout type="note">
The Billing page itself stays owner and admin only, because it lists every invoice in the
school. A dispatcher or instructor with the grant raises a manual invoice from the iOS app's
Generate Invoice screen; admins also use New invoice in the console, including for guests.
</Callout>
