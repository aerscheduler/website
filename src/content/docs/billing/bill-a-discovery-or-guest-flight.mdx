<Summary>
A guest has no account, so there is no PIN and nobody signs off. Ramp out and ramp in as
usual, then click Close out &amp; bill guest in the booking's Close-out section. Stripe
creates a customer for the guest and emails them the invoice with a pay link.
</Summary>

<Roles>Admins, Instructors</Roles>

## Before the flight

The booking has to be a Guest reservation, and the guest record on it needs a name and an
email address. That is what the invoice is addressed to. Nothing checks it for you, so make
sure both are filled in before you close out.

A guest booking holds one guest, and the guest is the payer. Both the aircraft time and
the instruction land on their invoice: instruction is charged whenever there is an
instructor plus somebody to instruct, and a guest counts.

## Close out and bill the guest

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Ramp out">
Open the booking on <Where app="web">Schedule</Where> and click Ramp out in the Close-out
section. Enter Hobbs out and Tach out.
</Step>

<Step n={2} title="Ramp in">
When the aircraft is back, click Ramp in and enter Hobbs in and Tach in, plus Instruction
time (hrs) if you are charging for the lesson.
</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Click Close out & bill guest">
On a member booking this is where the pilots would confirm with a PIN. On a guest booking
the Close-out section offers Close out &amp; bill guest instead.
</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Check the guest's details">
The dialog shows the guest's name, email and phone. Correct anything that is wrong before
you send, because this is where the invoice is going.

<Screenshot id="close-out-bill-guest" />
</Step>

<Step n={5} title="Confirm">
Click Close out &amp; bill. The review is finalized and the invoice goes out.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">
Guests never enter a PIN. There is no pilot sign-off step on a guest booking at all, which
is why this is a one-click close-out rather than the usual round of confirmations. The
instructor on the booking can do it, not just an admin.
</Callout>

## What the guest receives

Stripe creates a customer record for the guest and emails them the invoice with a payment
link. Guest invoices are given 7 days to pay by default.

The guest does not get an AerScheduler account. They cannot sign in, and they see nothing
about your school beyond their own invoice.

## Guest invoices in the Billing list

The invoice appears on <Where app="web">Billing</Where> alongside everything else, and the
instructor who flew it can open it from the reservation.

<Callout type="warning">
On a guest invoice the Customer column shows a dash, and the invoice detail panel reads
"No customer". The guest's name and email live on the booking, not on the invoice row.
Identify a guest invoice by the linked flight in the detail panel.
</Callout>

## Reminders do not apply to guests

Send payment reminder is refused on a guest invoice: "Guest invoices are emailed by
Stripe". Chasing a guest happens in your own Stripe dashboard, where the invoice was sent
from.

## If the guest record is incomplete

<Callout type="warning">
The close-out does not check the guest's details. With the name or the email missing,
**Close out &amp; bill** still succeeds, the booking locks as reviewed, and no invoice is
raised. Nothing on screen tells you.
</Callout>

You cannot run the close-out a second time. It answers "This reservation has already been
reviewed", and the guest's details can no longer be corrected on the booking. Bill the
guest by hand instead, from Billing → New invoice with Guest recipient on (or the iOS Generate Invoice screen), where you can type
their name and email in directly.

## Billing a guest for something that is not a flight

The web console's New invoice dialog can only pick a member. To invoice a guest for a
headset, a photo package or anything else, use Billing → New invoice with Guest recipient on,
which has a guest recipient field. See
[Create a manual invoice](/docs/billing/create-a-manual-invoice).
