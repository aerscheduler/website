<Summary>
On <Where app="web">Operations → Billing</Where>, with Account ledger on, the
**Accounts** tab is every member's balance. Filter who owes, click a row to open
their ledger, and leave guest invoices on the **Invoices** tab. Aging buckets and
export live under <Where app="web">Reports → Accounts receivable</Where>.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins</Roles>

This page is only for schools on
[Account ledger](/docs/billing/choose-invoice-or-account-ledger). Invoice-each-booking
schools still use Operations Billing as the invoice desk; their receivables report
ages unpaid invoices instead.

## What you see

<Screenshot id="ledger-accounts-table" />

Two tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Accounts** | Current members and their running balance. Green is credit on account. Amber is amount owed. **Days owing** is how long they have been in the hole. |
| **Invoices** | Guest pay-this-visit invoices, leftover invoices from before you switched, and unbilled reservations. |

Accounts is the day-to-day call list: who owes, who has prepaid. Click a row to open
that person on People → Ledger, where you add credit, refund, or read the entries.
See [Manage a member's account ledger](/docs/billing/manage-a-member-account-ledger).

The four numbers above the table are school-wide. They do not shrink when you search
or filter the list.

## Filter who owes

Use **Balance** to show Owes, Credit, or Zero. Search matches name or email. The table
opens with the most owing at the top.

**Days owing** starts the day the running balance last went negative and stayed there.
A later credit that brings them back to zero or above clears it. It is not "days since
their last flight."

## Invoices still exist

Guests never join the ledger, so a discovery flight still raises an invoice. Unpaid
invoices from before you switched also stay here until they are paid or voided. That is
why the Invoices tab does not disappear.

## Aging and export

For 30 / 60 / 90 day buckets, CSV, or a saved view, open
<Where app="web">Reports</Where> and run **Accounts receivable**.

- Ledger mode: one row per member who owes, aged the same way as Days owing on Billing.
- Invoice mode: unpaid invoices rolled up by customer, aged from the due date (or the
  date raised, if there is no due date).

The start of the report's date range is unused. It is a snapshot as of the end of the
range, who owes right now. Overview also flags receivables older than 90 days.
