Your organization bills in one currency: US, Canadian, Australian or New Zealand dollars, British pounds, or euros. It is set from your Stripe account when you connect payouts. The owner can change it under <Where app="web">Settings → Billing → Currency</Where> until anyone has been billed, and then it is fixed.

<Roles>Owners</Roles>

## Where it comes from

When you connect Stripe under <Where app="web">Settings → Billing → Payouts</Where>, AerScheduler starts billing in your Stripe account's own currency, as long as nobody has been billed yet and you have not already picked one. A school whose Stripe account pays out in Australian dollars bills in Australian dollars without doing anything else.

Before you connect, every new organization starts in US dollars.

## Change it

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Open Settings, then Billing">
The Currency card sits under Payouts.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Pick the currency">
The change saves straight away. Every price, rate, invoice and balance in the console and the app now reads in that currency.
</Step>
</Steps>

Only the organization owner can change it. Admins see the card but cannot edit it.

<Callout type="warning">
Your rates are numbers, not amounts in a particular currency. If you set an aircraft at 250.00 an hour and then switch from US dollars to Australian dollars, the rate becomes A$250.00, not the converted value. Set the currency first, then your rates.
</Callout>

## When it stops changing

The currency is fixed as soon as money moves: the first invoice, the first account ledger entry, or the first card payment a member starts. From then on the card is greyed out and says why. Every amount you have recorded is a number in that currency, so changing it later would reprice everything already billed and turn account balances into a mix of two currencies.

If it was set wrong, contact support before you bill anyone else.

## If Stripe pays out in a different currency

You can bill in a currency other than your Stripe account's. Settings shows a note when they differ. Stripe then converts every payment into your payout currency and charges you for the conversion, and your members may see a foreign transaction fee on their card. Billing in your payout currency avoids both.

## What uses it

- Invoices, receipts, statements and the emails about them
- Account ledger balances, top-ups, refunds, late fees and auto-refill
- Rates, fees, membership dues, course fees, package prices and work orders
- Reports and their CSV and PDF exports, which name the currency in each money column
- Your public booking pages
- QuickBooks: AerScheduler only posts to a QuickBooks company whose home currency matches

Amounts are always written with a point for the decimals and commas for thousands, for example £1,234.56.

Your AerScheduler plan itself is priced and billed in US dollars.
