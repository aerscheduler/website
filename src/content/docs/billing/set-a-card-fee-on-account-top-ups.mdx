## What it is for

When a member adds funds by card, Stripe charges a processing fee. This setting lets the
school recover that cost on the **card payment**, not by raising aircraft rates.

- The member chooses how much **credit** they want on account (for example $100).
- If you set a fee, they **pay** a little more (for example $103.20) and still receive
  **$100.00** credited.
- Cash or check recorded at the desk never uses this surcharge.

It is your school's fee. It is not AerScheduler's Connect application fee, and it is not
your **Service fee**. Those are two different things that both belong to you: the card fee
recovers card processing on money going **on** to an account, and the service fee is your
percentage of a **bill**. The service fee applies in both billing modes, so a flight
charged to a ledger carries it exactly as an invoice would.

## Set the fee

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Turn on Account ledger under How members pay">
The card fee card only appears when ledger mode is on. See
[Choose invoice or account ledger](/docs/billing/choose-invoice-or-account-ledger).
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Enter a Percent and/or Plus flat (USD)">
Percent uses the same units as the invoice service fee (2.9 means 2.9%). Flat is dollars
and cents (0.30 means thirty cents). Blank or 0.0 / 0.00 means no surcharge.
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Click Save card fee">
Only the organization owner can save. An admin can read the fields but the save is refused.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What members see

On Add funds, if a fee is set, the screen shows **Pay $X → $Y credited** before they
confirm. After a short Undo window, the card is charged the higher amount and the ledger
credits only the amount they asked for.

## Refunds

Refunding a card top-up returns the **credit**, not the surcharge. If they paid
$56 for $50 on account, a full refund sends $50 back to that card. The $6 card
fee stays charged. Stripe also keeps its processing fee on the original
payment.

Check or cash refunds never touch Stripe, so they cannot return a card fee
either. See [Refund](/docs/billing/manage-a-member-account-ledger#refund).

## Limits and notes

- Percent cannot be more than 100. Flat cannot be more than $100.00 per top-up.
- Clearing both fields (or saving zeros) turns the surcharge off again.
- ACH top-ups are not part of this setting yet.
