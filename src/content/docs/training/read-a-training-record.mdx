<Summary>
A student's record has four tabs. Overview is progress and blockers, Lessons is the syllabus
with each record underneath it, Requirements is the hours, and Ledger is every credit that
built those hours. Lessons and hours are two different measures and are meant to disagree.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Instructors, Students</Roles>

Open one from <Where app="web">Training → Students → the student</Where>, or from the person's
page under Training. A student opens their own from
[My training](/docs/training/my-training-for-students).

## The header

Student name, the course and version (for example "Private Pilot · Rev A"), a Part 61 or
Part 141 badge, and a status badge: In training, Graduated, Terminated or Transferred. A
Part 141 record that has been certified also carries a Record certified badge. A record that
is still In training may carry a pace badge as well; a finished one never does.

## Overview: progress, blockers and fees

<Screenshot id="enrollment-overview" />

- **Lessons complete**, as a bar. How far through the syllabus they are.
- **Not ready to graduate**, an amber card on Part 141 courses, naming the requirements still
  unmet. This is the only place the reason for a disabled Graduate button is written down.
- **Course fee**, if the course had one when they enrolled, with the button to bill it.
- **Endorsements**, the same card that appears on the person's page.

<Callout type="note">
The bar and the requirement bars move independently on purpose. Lessons say how far through
the syllabus a student is. Requirements say whether they have the flight time to test.
A student can be on lesson 19 of 21 and still be hours short, and that is exactly the
question this module exists to answer.
</Callout>

## Lessons: what each badge means

Every stage and lesson from the version they are enrolled on, with the records made against
each one underneath.

| On a lesson | Means |
|---|---|
| Complete | The lesson has a signed record graded with a mark the course's scale says completes it. |
| Next up | The first lesson that is not yet complete. It is a suggestion, not a gate. |

| On a record | Means |
|---|---|
| Grading | A draft. Editable, and crediting nothing yet. |
| Awaiting student | The instructor has signed it. Hours are already credited. The student has not countersigned. |
| Signed | Both signatures are on it. |
| Superseded | It has been amended. Struck through, kept on the record. |
| Correction | The replacement for a superseded record. It credits nothing until it is signed. |

<Screenshot id="enrollment-lessons" />

Two things surprise people here, and both are correct:

- A lesson graded with a failing mark is signed, is on the record, and does not move the
  progress bar. The student flies it again.
- A student who flew the same lesson three times before passing counts as one lesson done.
  Lessons are counted, not records.

## Requirements: credited, flown and discounted

One row per requirement, showing credited hours against required hours, with a bar. A row
sourced from your own school rather than from the regulation carries a School badge.

<Screenshot id="enrollment-requirements" />

When the number credited is lower than the hours the student actually flew, an amber line
underneath says why. There are three reasons:

1. Device time above the course's simulator limit for that requirement.
2. Transferred credit above the limit for previous training.
3. Training that has fallen outside the requirement's recency window.

<Callout type="important">
None of these lose anybody's flying. Every hour is still on the ledger. The requirement is
refusing to count more than the rule allows, and the raw figure is shown beside it as hours
flown.
</Callout>

A recency window is counted in whole calendar months from the 1st, which is the FAA's
wording. On 5 August with a two month window, training from 1 June still counts.

## Ledger: every credit and reversal

Append-only. One row per credit, with the amount, the requirement it fed, a chip saying where
it came from, and the date. Corrections appear as negative rows labelled as reversals,
carrying the reason somebody typed when they amended the record.

<Screenshot id="enrollment-ledger" />

Sources are: a signed lesson, simulator time, transferred training under Part 61,
transferred training under Part 141, a manual credit, and a reversal. Nothing here is ever
edited or deleted.

## The pace badge

An advisory badge in the header, based on how long it has been since the student's last
signed lesson. At risk is 30 days. Stalled is 60 days. Hover it for the reason.

A student with no signed lesson yet gets no badge until they have been enrolled 60 days,
because new is not the same as behind.

It never gates anything. It exists to prompt a phone call. For the same information across
the whole school, use the Student progress report.

## Where graduated students go

<Where app="web">Training → Students</Where> only ever lists people whose enrollment is In
training. Graduated, terminated and transferred students are not on that page and there is no
status filter to bring them back.

You will find them in three other places: the course's own Students tab, the person's page
under Training, and the Student progress report.

## Who is allowed to open a record

| Who | What they get |
|---|---|
| The student on the record | Their own record in full, the same numbers the school sees. |
| Instructors and admins | Any training record in the school, in full, with grading controls. |
| A holder of the Auditor grant | Every record, read only. |
| Everyone else, including dispatchers | Refused. |

Grading buttons only appear for people who can actually grade. The lifecycle buttons
(Certify record, Graduate, End enrollment) only appear for admins and holders of the Enroll
and graduate grant.
