<Summary>
Open the course and go to **Requirements**, then click **Add**. A requirement is what a
student must accumulate before graduating (40 hours total, 3 hours night, 10 night
landings), tracked separately from lessons. Set the amount, set **Comes from**, and set any
simulator, transfer or recency limit.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, holders of the Configure training grant</Roles>

Requirements belong to a syllabus **version**, and the version has to be a draft. Add them
before you build the lessons: the **Credits toward** box in the lesson dialog is empty until
requirements exist.

The requirement appears immediately on every enrolled student's Requirements tab, and
becomes tickable under Credits toward on any lesson in the same version.

## Hours or events, never both

Choose **Hours** for anything measured on a clock: total time, dual received, night flight
training. Choose **Events** for anything you count: 10 night landings, one long solo
cross-country.

An events requirement is fed by lessons that tick **per lesson** under Credits toward, so
one signed lesson posts one event.

## Comes from: Part 61, Part 141 or Our own

| Comes from | On the student's record | Blocks a Part 141 graduation? |
|---|---|---|
| Part 61 | Shows a Part 61 badge | Yes |
| Part 141 | Shows a Part 141 badge | Yes |
| Our own | Shows a **School** badge | No, never |

<Callout type="important">
"Our own" is not a category label, it is a switch. A requirement sourced Our own is shown to
the student, tracked exactly like the others, and can never stop them graduating however
short they are. Use it for a bar your school sets above the regulation. Use Part 61 or Part
141 for anything that has to hold the Graduate button down.

On a Part 61 course nothing blocks graduation regardless. See
[Part 61 vs Part 141](/docs/training/part-61-vs-part-141).
</Callout>

## Simulator and transfer limits

Both are percentages of that one requirement.

- **Simulator limit %** caps how much of the requirement may be met with device time.
  Appendix B allows 20% or 15% depending on the course.
- **Transfer limit %** caps how much may come from previous training. §141.77 allows 50%
  from an approved Part 141 or 142 course, and 25% otherwise.

Leave a box empty for no ceiling.

<Callout type="warning">
Ceilings are applied when the record is **read**, not when the credit is written. Every hour
the student flew stays on the ledger forever. The requirement simply refuses to count more
than the cap, so a row can legitimately read "10.0 of 40.0 hrs" with "Flown: 20.0 hrs"
beside it, and the record explains which cap did it in amber underneath.

That is not lost flying, and it is the single most common "the software has eaten my
student's hours" email. See
[Read a training record](/docs/training/read-a-training-record).
</Callout>

## Only count training from the last N months

Some requirements only count recent training, for example the test-preparation hours that
have to be flown close to the checkride. Set **Only count training from the last … months**
and older training stops counting toward that one requirement. It stays on the record and on
the ledger, and the student's Requirements row says so.

<Callout type="warning">
The window is in whole **calendar months, counted from the 1st**, which is the FAA's wording
and not the same as N × 30 days. On 5 August with a 2 month window, anything flown from
1 June onward counts, not from 6 June.

It is judged on when the flying **happened**, not when it was typed in. That matters when
you post prior training: leaving the flown date blank dates the hours to today and makes a
2019 logbook look current forever. See
[Credit prior training](/docs/training/credit-prior-training).
</Callout>

## Connect requirements to lessons

A requirement on its own accumulates nothing. Hours reach it in two ways:

1. **A signed lesson that credits it.** Open each lesson in the Syllabus tab and tick this
   requirement under **Credits toward**, choosing flight time, ground time or per lesson.
   [Build a syllabus](/docs/training/build-a-syllabus)
2. **A credit posted by hand**, for prior training or device time.
   [Credit prior training and simulator time](/docs/training/credit-prior-training)

<Callout type="tip">
After you add a requirement to an existing draft, go back through the lessons and tick it
where it belongs. A requirement added late is a requirement no lesson feeds, and its bar
will sit at zero all year.
</Callout>

## Editing and deleting

Edit or delete a requirement with the controls on its row, while the version is a draft.
Once the version is published, the whole requirement set is frozen with it, and the only way
to change it is a new version. See
[Publish a syllabus version](/docs/training/publish-a-syllabus-version).
