<Summary>
Owners and admins can do everything. Instructors can read and grade every training record and
sign endorsements, but cannot touch the course library or enroll anybody until they are given
a grant. Students see only their own record. Dispatchers see almost nothing, despite Training
appearing in their navigation.
</Summary>

## What each role can do

| | Owner / Admin | Instructor | Dispatcher | Student | Renter | Technician |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open the Training page | Yes | Yes | Yes, but it is empty | No | No | No |
| See and edit the course library | Yes | Only with Configure training | No | No | No | No |
| Enroll, graduate, terminate, certify | Yes | Only with Enroll and graduate | No | No | No | No |
| Read any student's training record | Yes | Yes | No | Own only | Own only | No |
| Grade, sign and amend lesson records | Yes | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Countersign a lesson | No | Own only | No | Yes | Yes | No |
| Sign and renew endorsements | Yes | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Post transfer or simulator credit | Yes | Only with Enroll and graduate | No | No | No | No |
| Run the three training reports | Yes | No | Yes | No | No | No |
| Hand out training grants | Yes | No | No | No | No | No |

Two rows in that table catch people out.

**Instructors cannot open Reports at all.** The Student progress, Training records and
Endorsement expirations reports are owner, admin and dispatcher only. An instructor works from
the student's record and from the endorsement digest instead.

**Nobody can countersign on a student's behalf**, including the owner. The student's signature
is the student's.

## The four training grants

<Where app="web">Training → Permissions</Where>

<Screenshot id="training-permissions-tab" />

| Grant | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Configure training | Build and publish syllabi, edit stages, lessons, tasks and requirements, set the grading scale, retire and un-retire versions. |
| Enroll and graduate | Enroll students, post transfer credit, certify a Part 141 record, graduate, terminate or transfer, and bill the enrollment fee. |
| Check instructor | Records that this instructor is your designated check instructor, either for one named course or for every course. |
| Auditor (read-only) | Read every training record in the school and change nothing. |

Only **Check instructor** can be scoped to a single course. The other three always apply
school-wide.

## Hand out or take back a grant

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Training → Permissions">
    The section only renders for owners and admins. A grant holder cannot hand their own grant
    to somebody else.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Click Add on the grant you want to give">
    Each grant carries a description of exactly what it adds.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Pick a person">
    Only owners, admins, instructors and dispatchers are offered. Students and renters cannot
    hold a training grant.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Choose which course, for Check instructor only">
    Or Every course.
  </Step>
  <Step n={5} title="Click Grant">
    It takes effect immediately. To take one back, click the trash icon on the person's row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Grants only ever add

Nothing on this screen can take a capability away from anybody.

Owners and admins already configure training and enroll students, so granting or revoking on
their rows changes nothing about what they can do. A school that never opens this page behaves
exactly as it always did: the grants exist so that a chief instructor or a training
administrator who is not an org admin can do the job without being made one.

## Read-only access for an FAA inspector

The **Auditor** grant reads every training record in the school and changes nothing, but it
can only be given to somebody who already holds a staff role. The person picker offers
owners, admins, instructors and dispatchers only, so an inspector added to your roster as an
ordinary member never appears in it and cannot be granted Auditor at all.

<Callout type="warning">
Making the inspector a dispatcher or an instructor so that the grant can be given also hands
them that role's own powers, which is a good deal more than read-only.
</Callout>

In practice the cleaner route is to export the record yourself. See
[Pull training records for an FAA inspection](/docs/training/training-records-for-an-faa-inspection).

## Where the button and the permission disagree

Two places in the console offer a dispatcher something the server will refuse. Both are worth
knowing before somebody emails you about a broken screen.

**Sign one, on the Endorsements card.** The button is shown to dispatchers, but only an
instructor or an administrator can sign an endorsement. A dispatcher who tries gets told so.

**A training record opened by link or by search.** Dispatchers can reach the address, but the
server only serves a record to the student on it, to somebody who can grade, or to an Auditor.
A dispatcher gets a refusal on everybody but themselves.

<Callout type="note">
There is a related oddity on a person's page. If you are staff but not an instructor, the
Training card on somebody else's page can show your own enrollments and your own endorsements
rather than theirs. It is your data being shown in the wrong frame, not two members' records
mixed together. Read other people's records from Reports instead.
</Callout>
