<Summary>
Open the course and go to **Requirements**. The **Grading scale** card at the top holds the
marks this course version uses and which of them mean the lesson is complete. It defaults to
S, U and I with S completing the lesson, and it freezes when the version is published.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, holders of the Configure training grant</Roles>

The scale belongs to the syllabus **version**, not to the school, so two courses can grade
differently and a revision can change the marks without touching students on the old
version.

## Edit the scale

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Course → Requirements">
The **Grading scale** card sits above the requirement list. The version has to be a draft.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Edit the codes">
One row per mark.
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Tick Completes the lesson">
On every mark that means the student may move on.
</Step>
<Step n={4} title="Add or remove marks">
**Add grade** for another row, the trash icon to drop one.
</Step>
<Step n={5} title="Click Save scale">
The button only appears once something has actually changed.
</Step>
</Steps>

Two rules are enforced on save:

- At least one mark has to complete the lesson, or the save is refused with "At least one
  grade has to mean the lesson is complete."
- Two marks cannot share a name.

## Which marks complete the lesson

"Complete" on a lesson means the record is **signed** and carries a mark this card says
completes the lesson. Both halves are required.

That is what fills the lessons-complete bar on a student's record, and what the syllabus
uses to work out which lesson is **Next up**. Distinct lessons are counted, never records,
so a student who flew the same lesson three times before passing counts as one lesson done.

## What a U does to the record

An unsatisfactory grade is a real record, not a failure to record anything.

- It is signed, frozen and permanently on the student's training record.
- It appears on the Training records report for an inspector to read.
- It never moves the lessons-complete bar, so the lesson stays outstanding and stays
  **Next up**.
- Its hours are still credited to every requirement the lesson credits. The student flew
  them.

<Callout type="warning">
The close-out lesson picker and the "Next up" strip on the booking form treat a lesson as
flown as soon as **any** signed record exists for it, including one graded U. So the same
student can read as "Flight 5 ✓, next up Flight 6" at the close-out while their training
record still shows Flight 5 incomplete and Next up. The training record is the one to
believe.
</Callout>

## Where a custom scale cannot be graded

The **Grade** dropdown in the close-out **Training record** section always offers S, U and I.
It does not read the course's own scale.

If your course grades 1 to 4, or Introduced / Practiced / Perfected, the signature from a
close-out is refused, with a message naming your course's actual marks, for example:
`"S" is not one of this course's grades (1, 2, 3, 4).`

<Callout type="important">
Grading from the flight close-out is the path the whole module is built around, so changing
the scale costs you the fastest way to grade. Unless you have a firm reason to change it,
keep the default S, U and I.

If you do need your own marks, plan on grading from the student's training record or the
iPhone app instead. See
[Grade a lesson from a training record](/docs/training/grade-a-lesson-from-a-training-record).
</Callout>

## The scale freezes when you publish

Publishing the version locks the grading scale along with the lessons, the tasks and the
requirements. There is no unpublish.

To change the marks on a published course, create a new version, edit its scale, publish
that, and retire the old one. Students already enrolled stay on their version and keep
grading against the marks they started with. See
[Publish a syllabus version](/docs/training/publish-a-syllabus-version).
