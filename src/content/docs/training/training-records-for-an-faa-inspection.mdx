<Where app="web">Reports → Compliance → Training records</Where> is the log to hand an
inspector: every graded lesson, both signatures, and the hours. Set the range, filter to the
student, add the columns you want, and export.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers</Roles>

Instructors cannot open Reports, so this is a front-desk or chief-instructor job.

## Open the Training records report

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Reports">
    From the left navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Choose Compliance → Training records">
    In the report rail.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Set the date range">
    It opens on the past 90 days. Widen it to the period being inspected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each row is one graded lesson. As the report opens that is the date, the student, the course,
the lesson, the grade, the flight and ground hours, the instructor, and the signature state.

<Screenshot id="report-training-records" />

<Callout type="note">
The range filters on the **signature date**, falling back to the day the draft was opened when
a record was never signed. So a lesson an instructor started in March and never finished still
appears in March, which is where an inspector would look for it.
</Callout>

## Set the range and the student

Filter on **Student** to produce one person's log, and on **Course** as well if they are
enrolled on more than one.

Useful optional columns, added from the report toolbar: **Stage**, **Type**, **Simulator**,
**Notes**, **Superseded**, **Correction**, and **Credits**, which counts the ledger entries
each signed lesson posted.

If you pull the same thing every year, [save the view](/docs/reports/save-a-report-view) so the
range, the filters and the columns come back next time.

## What each Signatures value means

| Value | Means |
|---|---|
| Not signed | A draft. The instructor opened it and never signed. It credits nothing. |
| Awaiting student | The instructor signed it, so the hours are credited, but the student has not countersigned. |
| Complete | Both signatures are on the record. |

Filter Signatures to **Not signed** before an inspection to find drafts an instructor left
open, and to **Awaiting student** to find records nobody countersigned. Neither is chased
automatically, so this report is how you find them.

## Superseded and Correction rows

A record that has been amended stays in the report, flagged **Superseded**, with its
replacement flagged **Correction**. Both are shown on purpose.

That is the whole reason AerScheduler amends rather than edits: the original is still there,
the correction is beside it, and the reason for the change is on the reversal in the student's
ledger. See [Correct a signed lesson record](/docs/training/correct-a-signed-lesson).

<Callout type="tip">
Do not try to tidy these out of the export. A log with the wrong figure quietly replaced is
worth less to an inspector than one that shows the correction being made.
</Callout>

## Export it

Use the export action on the report toolbar. The export carries the columns you have on
screen, the range you set, and the filters you applied, so get the view right first. See
[Export a report to CSV](/docs/reports/export-a-report-to-csv).

## The Student progress report

<Where app="web">Reports → People → Student progress</Where>

The other half of the picture. One row per **enrollment**, with lessons done against lessons
in the syllabus, percent complete, total hours, how many requirements are still short, the
next lesson, and days since the student last flew.

<Screenshot id="report-student-progress" />

<Callout type="warning">
This report is windowed on the **enrollment date**, not on activity, and it opens on year to
date. Anybody who enrolled last year is missing until you widen the range. That single
default is the usual reason a school thinks students have disappeared.
</Callout>

Somebody training for two certificates at once appears twice, once per enrollment. That is
correct: they are two records with two sets of requirements.

## Find students who have stopped flying

On the same report, sort by **Requirements short**, or filter **Days since flown** to 30 or
more. Read the **Next lesson** column to see what to book them for, then call them.

The same signal appears one student at a time as the pace badge on their training record: At
risk after 30 days without a signed lesson, Stalled after 60.
