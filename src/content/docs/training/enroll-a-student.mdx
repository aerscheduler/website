## Check the version first

An enrollment points at a **syllabus version**, not at the course. Whichever version the
course page has selected is what the student will be trained, graded and graduated against,
for the whole course. The page opens on the current published, non-retired version, and a
version dropdown appears in the header once the course has more than one.

They are never migrated to a later revision, and there is no supported way to move a student
from one version to another. If you pick the wrong one, the fix is to end the enrollment and
enroll them again on the right version.

## One active enrollment per version

A student may hold only one **active** enrollment per version. A second attempt is refused
with "That student is already enrolled on this version."

Re-enrolling after a termination is allowed, and is the normal way to restart somebody who
quit and came back. The old enrollment and everything on it stays exactly as it was.

Somebody doing a Private and an Instrument at the same time simply has two enrollments, on
two different courses, and appears twice in the reports.

## Messages that stop an enrollment

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "A Part 141 course must be published before anyone can be enrolled on it." | Publish the draft first. Part 141 does not allow enrollment against a draft. See [Publish a syllabus version](/docs/training/publish-a-syllabus-version). |
| "This version has been retired. Enroll on the current version instead; students already on this one finish on it." | Select the current version in the header dropdown and try again. |
| "That student is already enrolled on this version." | They have an active enrollment. Open their record instead. |
| "That member is archived. Restore them before enrolling them." | Restore them on the People page first. |

## Enrolling an instructor

The picker is deliberately not students-only. A CFI adding an instrument rating is a normal
thing, so instructors and other staff appear in the list with **(staff)** beside the name.

Their record works exactly like a student's: they countersign their own lessons from
<Where app="web">My training</Where>, and another instructor grades them.
