<Summary>
Most training questions come down to five rules: publishing is permanent, lessons only credit
hours they were told to credit, requirements apply their limits when you read them, signing
freezes a record, and a dispatcher is not a grader. Everything below is one of those five.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Instructors, Students</Roles>

## I published by mistake and cannot edit the syllabus

There is no unpublish. Publishing freezes a version forever: its stages, lessons, tasks,
requirements and grading scale are read-only from that moment, and the editor is replaced by
a plain read-only page rather than a greyed-out one. Nothing failed to load.

The route forward is a new version. Click **New version** in the course header, name it, click
**Create draft**, and you get an editable deep copy of everything. Fix it, publish it, then
**Retire** the old version so nobody new is enrolled on it.

Anybody already enrolled on the old version stays on it and finishes on it. That is the point
of versions, and there is no supported way to move a student across. See
[Publish a syllabus version](/docs/training/publish-a-syllabus-version).

## Hours are not counting toward anything

The lesson credits nothing. In the syllabus editor, every lesson has a **Credits toward** box
listing the version's requirements, and you tick which clock feeds which: flight time, ground
time, or per lesson for a count.

A lesson with nothing ticked is perfectly flyable, gradeable and signable, and adds zero
hours to every requirement, silently.

<Screenshot id="syllabus-lesson-dialog-credits" />

Expand the lesson row in the syllabus. A lesson that credits nothing says so in amber. If the
version is already published you cannot fix it there: make a new version, tick the boxes, and
post the missing hours on affected students as [a credit by
hand](/docs/training/credit-prior-training).

## A requirement shows fewer hours than the student flew

Working as intended, and the row says which rule bit.

<Screenshot id="enrollment-requirements" />

Three possible reasons: device time above the course's simulator limit for that requirement,
transferred credit above the limit for previous training, or training that has fallen outside
the requirement's recency window.

Limits are applied when the record is read, not when the credit is written, so every hour is
still on the ledger. The requirement is only refusing to count more than its share.

If a recency window looks off by a few days, note that it is counted in whole calendar months
from the 1st, which is the FAA's wording. On 5 August with a two month window, training from
1 June counts.

## The Training record section is missing from a close-out

It appears only when all four of these are true:

1. The booking type is dual, ground, sim or solo.
2. At least one student is on the booking.
3. That student is enrolled on a course that has a lesson of the matching type. Dual and solo
   look for flight lessons, ground for ground lessons, sim for sim lessons.
4. You are staff, or one of the instructors assigned to that booking.

A rental, a guest flight or a maintenance booking never shows it, and neither does a booking
whose student is on no course. See [Grade a lesson when you close out the
flight](/docs/training/grade-a-lesson-at-close-out).

## The grade dropdown will not accept our marks

The grade dropdown in the close-out Training record section offers S, U and I only. If your
course version uses its own marks, the server compares what was sent against the course's
scale and refuses the signature, naming your own grades back at you: `"S" is not one of this
course's grades (1, 2, 3, 4).`

Courses created from a template use S, U and I and grade from the close-out normally. Before
you change a course's scale, read [Set the grading scale for a
course](/docs/training/set-the-grading-scale), which covers where a custom scale can and
cannot be graded.

## The numbers dropped after I amended a record

Expected, and temporary. The correction is created unsigned, and only a signed record counts,
so the moment you click Amend the lesson stops counting as complete and every hour the
original credited is reversed out of the ledger.

Finish the correction and sign it, and the numbers come back. If somebody amended a record and
walked away, look for a row badged Correction that is not signed. See [Correct a signed lesson
record](/docs/training/correct-a-signed-lesson).

## Graduate is disabled and I cannot see why

The reason lives on the **Overview** tab of the training record, in an amber card naming what
is outstanding. From the Requirements or Lessons tab you see only a dead button.

On a Part 141 course, graduation is blocked while the record has not been certified, or while
any requirement sourced from Part 61 or Part 141 is unmet. Requirements you set yourself, with
Comes from set to "Our own", never block.

On a Part 61 course the button is never disabled. See [Graduate a student, or end an
enrollment](/docs/training/graduate-or-end-an-enrollment).

## Training is empty when a dispatcher opens it

Dispatchers can open the Training page, and then see one section saying nobody is in training.
The page is not broken. The course library is not served to a dispatcher, so that section is
hidden, and the student list is scoped to the dispatcher's own enrollments, which is normally
none.

Dispatchers do get the three training reports, which is the right place for them to work. See
[Who can see and do what in Training](/docs/training/who-can-do-what-in-training).

## Someone else's records appear on a person's page

If you are staff but not an instructor, the Training card on another member's page can show
**your own** enrollments and endorsements under their name.

Nothing is mixed up between two members, and no student is seeing another student's record.
It is your own data drawn in the wrong frame, because the request for someone else's records
is answered with your own for anyone who is not a grader. Read other people's records from
Reports, or ask an instructor or an admin.

## I cannot find our graduated students

<Where app="web">Training → Students</Where> only ever lists enrollments that are still In
training, and there is no status filter on it. Nothing has been deleted.

Graduated, terminated and transferred students are in three places: the course's own Students
tab, the person's page under Training, and the Student progress report. Widen that report's
range past year to date or you will still miss anybody who enrolled last year.

## Simulator hours and task grades are always blank

Both are entered wherever you grade, the console included, but each only appears when the
lesson calls for it.

**Simulator hours** appear on a lesson your syllabus marks as a **sim**. On a flight lesson
there is no field, because there is nothing to declare: everything flown was in the aircraft.
If a device session was recorded against a flight lesson, the hours went in as aircraft time
and your course's simulator allowance never saw them. Fix that by grading it against the sim
lesson.

**Task grades** appear when the lesson has tasks. A lesson with none shows no task list, so
if the grid is missing on every lesson, the tasks were never added to the syllabus. See
[Build a syllabus](/docs/training/build-a-syllabus).

Nothing is marked for you, and unmarked tasks are saved as unmarked rather than as a pass.

## A grade I entered offline did not sync

Check the banner at the top of Training on the phone. If it is red, the server refused the
grade and its own message is on the entry: usually that the record was already signed, or that
you no longer have permission to grade that student. The entry is kept, not thrown away.

Two other things worth knowing:

- Only one pending grade is held per lesson. Grading the same lesson twice offline replaces
  the first attempt.
- If the record saved but the signature was refused, the next sync can leave a second draft on
  that lesson. Open the student's record in the console, sign the one you want, and grade over
  the other.

## One screen says the lesson is done and another does not

The close-out lesson picker and the Next up strip on the booking form treat a lesson as done
once **any** signed record exists against it, including a failing grade. The student's training
record only counts a lesson complete when it is signed **and** graded with a mark the course's
scale says completes it.

So a student can read as "Flight 5 done, next up Flight 6" at the close-out while their record
still shows Flight 5 incomplete. The training record is the one that counts, and the one an
inspector reads.

## I cannot undo a credit I posted by hand

There is no reverse button in the console. Check the requirement, the amount and the date
before clicking Post credit.

The only undo the console offers is amending the lesson record that created a credit, which
reverses that lesson's credits. It does nothing for a transfer, simulator or manual credit
posted by hand.

## An endorsement went out with blanks still in it

The sign dialog counts the unfilled braces and warns you, but the Sign button stays enabled, and
the text is stored exactly as you signed it and never regenerated. So an endorsement signed
with blanks keeps them.

You cannot edit it. Sign a fresh, correct one. If the endorsement carries an expiry date, use
**Renew** on the row, which signs a replacement and removes the old one from the card, the
expirations report and the digest. See [Sign an
endorsement](/docs/training/sign-an-endorsement).

## I cannot add a lesson to a student who has graduated

Once an enrollment is graduated, terminated or transferred, the console refuses to add or edit
any lesson record on it, and there is no un-graduate.

Record everything before you graduate a student, including the ground lessons somebody has
been meaning to write up. A lesson that turns up afterwards has nowhere to go in AerScheduler,
so it stays in your own paperwork.
