Open the course with the draft selected and click **Publish**, then **Publish and lock**.
Publishing freezes that version forever and there is no unpublish. To change a published
syllabus you click **New version**, edit the copy, publish that, and **Retire** the old one.
Students already enrolled finish on the version they started.
</Summary>

## What publishing locks

Everything the version owns:

- every stage, and its stage settings
- every lesson, its hours, objectives and completion standards
- every task and ACS code
- every requirement, including the simulator, transfer and recency limits
- the grading scale

All of it becomes read-only, permanently. This is the one irreversible act in the training
module.

The enrollment fee is the exception, because it belongs to the course rather than to the
version. A price is a commercial decision, not part of the document you file, so you can
change it whenever you like. See
[Charge a course enrollment fee](/docs/training/course-enrollment-fee).

until they cut a new version.

## Why a published syllabus has no edit controls

After publishing, the header badge changes to **Locked** and the syllabus editor is replaced
by a plain read-only page: no pencils, no Add buttons, no trash icons anywhere.

That is deliberate. Thirty greyed-out pencils read as a broken page, so the controls are
removed rather than disabled. Nothing has failed to load.

The header actions change too: **Publish** is replaced by **New version** and **Retire**.

## Retire a version so nobody new joins it

Select the published version and click **Retire**. The confirmation says it plainly: no new
students can be enrolled on it, and anyone already on it finishes on it.

Retiring is fully reversible. The button becomes **Un-retire**.

Enrolling on a retired version is refused with "This version has been retired. Enroll on the
current version instead; students already on this one finish on it."

<Callout type="note">
Retiring applies to a version, not to a whole course. There is no control that removes a
course from the Training page, so a course you have stopped teaching stays in the list with
all its versions retired.
</Callout>

## What happens to students already enrolled

Nothing. That is the point of versions.

- A student enrolled on Rev A stays on Rev A, is graded against Rev A's lessons and Rev A's
  grading scale, and graduates against Rev A's requirements.
- They are never migrated to Rev B, and there is no supported way to move them across.
- Publishing Rev B, and retiring Rev A, changes nothing on their record.

<Callout type="tip">
If you need one student on the new syllabus, end their old enrollment and enroll them on the
new version. Everything already recorded on the old enrollment stays exactly where it is.
See [Graduate a student, or end an enrollment](/docs/training/graduate-or-end-an-enrollment)
and [Enroll a student](/docs/training/enroll-a-student).
</Callout>

## Part 141 has one extra rule

Under Part 141 you cannot enroll anybody until the version is published. Under Part 61 you
can enroll students against a draft, which is convenient while you are still writing the
syllabus and dangerous afterwards, because the draft can change under them. See
[Part 61 vs Part 141](/docs/training/part-61-vs-part-141).
