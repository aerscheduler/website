<Summary>
Open the student's enrollment once while you still have signal, then grade and sign as
normal at the aircraft. A grade recorded with no signal is stored on the phone, counted in
a banner at the top of Training, and sent up the next time you open Training or that
enrollment with signal.
</Summary>

<Roles>Instructors, Admins</Roles>

## Before you lose signal

The app draws the grading screen from a cached copy of that student's syllabus, and it only
caches what it has successfully read. So open the enrollment at least once while you have
signal.

<Callout type="warning">
Open the app cold, out of range, on a student you have never opened on that phone, and the
lesson will not load. There is nothing to fix at the aircraft: the syllabus is not on the
device. Open each student you plan to fly before you walk out.
</Callout>

<Where app="mobile">Training</Where> lists the people you can grade. As an instructor or an
admin the rows are headed by the student name with the course underneath. Tap a student to
open their enrollment, which is enough to cache it.


## Grade a lesson on the phone

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open the lesson">
    From the enrollment, tap the lesson on the Lessons tab, or tap the Next lesson card at
    the top.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Tap a grade">
    The grade buttons are drawn from the course's own grading scale, so they are whatever
    marks that course version uses.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Fill in the hours and notes">
    FLIGHT and GROUND are always there. SIM appears only when the lesson's type is
    Simulator. Add per-task grades on the task chips, each with its ACS code beside it, and
    type your notes.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Save draft, or Sign and complete">
    Save draft leaves the record open and editable. Sign and complete freezes it and credits
    the student's hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>


<Callout type="important">
Signing is final. A signed record can never be edited, on the phone or anywhere else. If it
turns out to be wrong, see [Correct a signed lesson record](/docs/training/correct-a-signed-lesson).
</Callout>

If the syllabus author ticked Notes required on that lesson, the app will not let you sign
it with an empty Notes box.

## Simulator hours and per-task grades

The phone is the only place these two can be entered. Neither the grading dialog on the
web training record nor the Training record section of a flight close-out has a SIM field
or a task grid.

That matters more than it sounds. A course with a simulator limit on a requirement can only
apply that limit to hours that were recorded as simulator time, so a school that grades
entirely in the console never populates it. If you teach in a device, grade those lessons
on the phone.

## What the pending banner means

When a grade cannot be sent, the app saves it on the device and tells you so, and a banner
appears above the list on Training and on the enrollment counting what is waiting.

Only one grade is ever held per lesson. Grading the same lesson twice while offline
replaces the first attempt rather than queuing both, which is deliberate: replaying two
grades for one flight would credit the hours twice.

## Sync a queued grade

Open Training or the enrollment and tap **Try now** on the banner. Opening either screen
also pushes the queue on its own, so in practice a queue clears when you get back to signal
and open the app.

<Callout type="warning">
Nothing sends in the background. Getting your signal back with the phone in your pocket
sends nothing: a queued grade sits there until somebody opens Training or that enrollment.
Before you finish for the day, open Training and check the banner has gone.
</Callout>

## When a queued grade is refused

If the server refuses a queued grade, the entry is kept rather than dropped, the server's
own message is shown on it, and the banner turns red reading how many grades could not be
saved. Read the message: it is usually that the record was already signed by somebody else,
or that you no longer have permission to grade that student.

A refusal that happens while you are online, such as trying to grade an already-signed
record, is never queued at all. You see the message straight away.

<Callout type="warning">
There is one case worth checking on the web afterwards. If the record saves but the
signature is refused, the entry stays in the queue and the next sync saves the draft again,
which can leave two rows reading Grading on the same lesson. Open the student's record in
the console, sign the one you want, and grade over the other.
</Callout>

## What the app cannot do

Amending a signed record is deliberately not offered on the phone. The screen tells you to
do it in the web console.

Billing a course enrollment fee is on the enrollment screen behind a confirmation naming the
amount, and it is not queued when you are offline. It waits for signal.

A record that has already been signed shows as a green Signed and final panel, not as a
disabled form, so there is nothing to tap by mistake.
