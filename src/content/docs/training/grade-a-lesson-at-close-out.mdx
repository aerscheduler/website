<Summary>
This is the intended path. Close the booking out the way you always do, then scroll to the
**Training record** section on the close-out sheet, click **Grade this lesson**, check the
lesson and the grade, and click **Sign lesson**. Flight and Ground are already filled in from
the billed meter (Hobbs or tach, whichever that aircraft invoices on) and the briefing time.
</Summary>

<Roles>Instructors at the school, Admins, Owners</Roles>

## Close out the flight as usual

Nothing about training changes the close-out. Enter the meter readings and the briefing time
exactly as you would on a rental. See
[Close out a flight and bill it](/docs/billing/close-out-a-flight-and-bill-it).

If the student is enrolled on a course, you will already have seen a grey **Next up** strip
on the booking form when the flight was made, naming the lesson the syllabus suggests and
how far through they are. If they have finished every matching lesson, the strip says
**Syllabus complete** instead of naming one. If they are on more than one course, the strip
starts as one line with a link to show the others. Nothing is committed there: you pick and
grade the lesson at close-out, once you know how it went.

## Find the Training record section

Scroll down the close-out sheet. There is one block per student per enrollment, headed with
the course name and the lesson the syllabus suggests next.

<Step n={2} title="Check the Lesson dropdown">
The first unfinished lesson is preselected. Lessons that have already been flown are prefixed

<Step n={3} title="Set the Grade">
The dropdown offers this course's own marks, the same scale the training record and the

<Step n={4} title="Check Flight and Ground">
**Flight** is prefilled from the billed meter delta (Hobbs, or tach if that is how the

<Step n={5} title="Type Notes">
What the student did, and what to work on. This is what an inspector reads. If you already
saved a draft on the phone, those notes, hours, the grade and any task marks are already in
the form. Do not wipe them: Sign writes whatever is on screen.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Sign the lesson

Click **Sign lesson**. In one action the record is saved, signed, frozen, linked to this
booking, and its hours fan out into a ledger credit for every requirement the lesson credits.
The section then reads "&lt;Lesson&gt; signed and credited to &lt;Course&gt;." Reopening the
booking later still says that, and the folded header says **signed** (or **1 of 2 graded** on
a two-student flight).

If every matching lesson on the course is already finished, the section says **Syllabus
complete** instead of offering a grade. There is nothing left to sign on that booking.

<Callout type="important">
Signing is final. A signed record can never be edited, only amended, which creates a
correction beside it and takes the hours back until the correction is signed. Read the hours
before you click. See
[Correct a signed lesson record](/docs/training/correct-a-signed-lesson).
</Callout>

The student's countersignature is a separate step they do themselves from
<Where app="web">My training</Where>. The record sits at **Awaiting student** until they do,
with its hours already counted.

## Two students on one booking

Every student on the booking gets their own block, with their own lesson, grade, hours and
notes. Two students in one aircraft are graded separately, and each signature credits only
that student's record.

## When the section does not appear at all

The Training record section is hidden unless all of this is true:

- the booking type is **dual**, **ground**, **sim** or **solo**
- at least one **student** is on the booking
- that student is **enrolled** on a course
- the course has a lesson whose type matches the booking (dual and solo offer flight
  lessons, ground offers ground lessons, sim offers sim lessons)
- you are an instructor or an admin at the school

A rental, a guest flight or a maintenance booking shows no trace of it rather than guessing.
So does a dual flight for somebody who is on no course. If you expected the section and it is
not there, work down that list: the usual answer is that the student was never enrolled, or
the booking was made as a rental.

<Callout type="note">
A lesson graded U is signed and stays on the record, but it is not ticked complete. The
close-out picker, the Next up strip, and the student's training record all use the same
rule: only a signed passing grade finishes the lesson. Fly it again to complete it. Hours
from the U still count toward the lesson's requirements.
</Callout>

## Simulator hours

On a lesson your syllabus marks as a sim, the grader adds **Of the flight hours, in a
simulator**.

It is a part of the flight figure beside it, not an extra. That is what makes your course's
simulator allowance apply to the time: hours typed only into Flight are credited as aircraft
time, and the ceiling never sees them.

## Per-task grades

If the lesson has tasks, each one gets a row with its ACS code and your course's own marks
as buttons, and a count of how many you have marked.

Marking them is optional and nothing is marked for you. A syllabus lesson routinely covers
tasks that were not attempted, so leave those alone: the record should say what you assessed,
not tick everything the lesson could have covered. Press a mark a second time to clear it.
Clearing every chip and signing writes that the tasks were unmarked, including on a draft
that already had marks from the phone.

## What close-out grading cannot do

| Not here | Where instead |
|---|---|
| **Save as a draft**. The close-out signs in one action. | [Grade from the student's training record](/docs/training/grade-a-lesson-from-a-training-record), which has Save draft. |
| **Grading offline.** The console needs a connection. | The [iPhone app](/docs/training/grade-lessons-offline-on-the-app) queues lessons graded with no signal. |

If the lesson already has an unsigned draft on another flight, Sign takes that draft over for this booking and fills hours from this close-out. If the lesson is already signed, you can still pick it and Sign again: that records another dual on the same lesson.

Your course's own marks are offered here, whatever scale you set. A lesson your syllabus
marks **Notes required** will not sign until the Notes box has something in it, on this
screen and on every other.

Under Part 141, grading out of syllabus order shows a warning and does not stop the
signature.
