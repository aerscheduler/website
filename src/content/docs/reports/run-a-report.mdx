## Open a report

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Go to Reports">
<Where app="web">Reports</Where> in the left navigation. On a narrow screen the rail becomes a "Choose a report"
select at the top of the page.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Pick a report from the rail">
Reports are grouped under Financial, Operations, Fleet, People and Compliance. You only see the categories your
roles grant, so the rail is shorter for a dispatcher and shorter again for a technician.
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Read the report's one-line description">
It sits under the report name at the top of the pane and says what a row is.
</Step>
</Steps>

Nothing you do here is saved. The report is recomputed on every change, and leaving the page discards the shaping.
If you want to keep it, [save it as a view](/docs/reports/save-a-report-view).

## Set the date range

The date range picker sits at the left of the toolbar. Presets are Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days and
Last 12 months, or you can set your own dates.

Each report opens on its own default window. Most look back 30 days. The three compliance reports (Document
expirations, Pilot currency, Endorsement expirations) open on the **next** 90 days instead, Student progress opens
on year to date, and Training records opens on the last 90 days.

## Read the date basis caption before you trust the numbers

Under the picker there is a small caption reading "by" and then a date, such as "by the date the invoice was
raised" or "by the date of the flight". That is the date your range applies to.

<Callout type="warning">
Two reports over exactly the same dates can legitimately disagree because they window on different dates. Revenue
counts an invoice on the day it was raised; Payments received counts it on the day it was paid. Read both captions
before concluding something is broken. See
[Report numbers that look wrong](/docs/reports/numbers-dont-match).
</Callout>

## Add a filter

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Click Filters">
The button sits beside the date picker. The menu behind it holds Group by and Columns as well as the filters, so
this is also where grouping lives.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Pick a field from the Filters section">
Each report offers its own list of fields.
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Choose or type a value">
Some fields give you a searchable list of values to tick. Others give you a box to type into.
</Step>
<Step n={4} title="Pick a condition if the field offers more than one">
The Condition list is pinned to the bottom of the field's submenu.
</Step>
<Step n={5} title="Close the menu">
The filter appears as a dismissible chip under the toolbar, and the badge on the Filters button goes up by one.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="report-filters-menu" />

<Callout type="note">
The badge on the Filters button counts active filters only. Grouping and hidden columns do not show up on it, which
is why the button can read "Filters" with no number while the table looks nothing like the default.
</Callout>

## Conditions: is, is not, over, under, is between

Depending on the field, the Condition list offers: is, is not, is more than, is at least, is less than, is at most,
contains, starts with, is any of, is none of, is empty, is not empty, and is between.

Two rules govern how filters combine:

- **One filter per field.** "Over 1 hour and under 5" is the "is between" condition, not two filters.
- **Filters are always ANDed.** To express an OR, use a multi-select condition ("is any of") on the field the
  question turns on.

<Callout type="warning">
Changing the condition clears the value you had already picked. That is deliberate: a single value, a multi-select
and an is-empty check are not interchangeable, and carrying a value across would silently match nothing. Pick the
condition first, then the value.
</Callout>

A filter you have added but not yet given a value to does nothing at all, so the table does not empty out while you
are mid-edit.

## Money is typed in dollars and hours in hours

Type money in dollars and hours as hours, so 2.5 means two and a half hours. A "$" or "hours" hint sits beside the
box, and the conversion is automatic.

<Callout type="note">
Aircraft and people filters match on the label, meaning the tail number or the person's name. Two members with
identical display names cannot be told apart by a filter, and a member with no name set does not appear in the
dropdown at all.
</Callout>

## Remove a filter or clear them all

Click the "x" on a filter chip to drop that one. To drop all of them at once, use "Clear all" beside the chips, or
"Clear all filters" inside the Filters menu.

## What the row count and the Totals row are telling you

Under the table you get the row count ("n rows", or "n groups" when the report is grouped), then "page x of y",
then the report's footnote. The table pages at 100 rows.

Sorting, filtering and grouping all apply across every matching row, not just the page you are looking at. Click
any column heading to sort by it.

The sticky row at the bottom of the table is the Totals row, and it aggregates every matching row across all pages
too. That is what you want when you are reconciling a window against a bank statement.

<Callout type="note">
A blank cell in the Totals row is not a fault. It means there is no honest way to add that column up. You cannot
sum a tail number.
</Callout>

On an ungrouped report, clicking a row opens the invoice or booking behind it in a detail panel.

## A dash is not a zero

A dash in a cell means no value was recorded. Zero means it really was zero, and the difference matters.

An aircraft booked for 13 hours whose flights were never closed out shows a blank Flown and a blank Efficiency, not
"0.0 h" and "0%". Printing zero would claim the aircraft sat idle, which is a different and wrong finding. If a
whole column is dashes, its total comes out blank as well.

The usual cause is a flight that was never closed out. See
[Ramp out and ramp in](/docs/scheduling/ramp-out-and-ramp-in).

## When nothing matches

The empty state reads "Nothing matched. Try a wider date range", and adds "or fewer filters" when you have filters
set. Both suggestions are worth taking in that order: widen the window first, because the window is what most often
excludes the rows you were expecting.

## Things that catch people out

<Callout type="warning">
- Grouping is inside the **Filters** menu, not next to it. So is the column picker.
- Cancelled bookings are excluded from Utilization and Flight log entirely. They have their own report,
  Cancellations &amp; no-shows.
- Voided invoices are excluded from every financial report, so a school that voids and reissues sees one figure,
  not two.
- Columns like "Days since last flight", "Days open" and "Days left" are measured to today, not to the end of the
  window you picked. They are chase numbers, so they deliberately disagree with the window heading above them.
</Callout>
