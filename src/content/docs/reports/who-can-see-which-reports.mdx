<Summary>
Reporting permissions come from one thing: the category a report sits in. Financial is owners and admins only.
Dispatchers get everything else. Technicians get Fleet. Instructors, students and renters do not get the Reports
page at all.
</Summary>

<Callout type="note">
The roles below are what each one carries **by default**. An owner or admin can also give
one person a single extra permission from that person's record, so somebody may be able to
do something here that their role alone would not allow. See [Give one person an extra
permission](/docs/getting-started/permissions-and-grants).
</Callout>


## Category decides access, nothing else does

There are five categories: Financial, Operations, Fleet, People, Compliance. Every report sits in exactly one of
them, and your roles decide which categories you get.

There is no per-report switch, no per-person override, and no setting to change any of this. If somebody needs a
report they cannot see, they need a different role.

| Category | Owner | Admin | Dispatcher | Technician | Instructor, student, renter |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financial | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Operations | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Fleet | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| People | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Compliance | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |

## Owners and admins

All five categories, all 18 reports, and everything built on them.

Owners and admins can also:

- Create schedules, and edit, pause, test-send or delete **any** schedule at the school.
- Add outside email addresses to a schedule, which nobody else can do.
- Reach <Where app="web">Audit Logs</Where>.
- See the money card and the Recent invoices panel on <Where app="web">Dashboard</Where>.
- See a member's spend and invoices, and an aircraft's Collected and Outstanding tiles.

Either of them can also set the school's time zone, which decides what every report window means.

## Dispatchers

Operations, Fleet, People and Compliance. That is Utilization, Flight log, Cancellations &amp; no-shows, Fleet,
Squawks &amp; downtime, Maintenance due, Instructor activity, Customer activity, Student progress, Document
expirations, Pilot currency, Endorsement expirations and Training records.

They can save, share, pin and schedule any of those exactly as an admin can.

They cannot see any Financial report. That also removes:

- The Billed, Collected and Outstanding tiles on the Overview, and the "Billed and collected" trend chart.
- The "Overdue invoices" item on the Needs attention card. "Flown not invoiced" stays, because it is built on the
  Flight log report, which a dispatcher can run.
- Any saved view or schedule built on a financial report, including the knowledge that one exists.
- <Where app="web">Audit Logs</Where>, which is admin only.
- A member's billing figures and an aircraft's revenue tiles.

## Technicians

Fleet only: Fleet, Squawks &amp; downtime and Maintenance due. They can export, save, pin and schedule those three.

On the Needs attention tile they get Grounded resources, Open squawks and Maintenance overdue. On an aircraft's
record they get the Utilization section, the activity chart and the open-squawk count, but not the revenue tiles.

<Callout type="warning">
A technician's default board is the two widgets, "Up next" and "Needs attention", and nothing else. That is
expected: every other default tile is built on Revenue, Utilization or Cancellations, none of which are in the
Fleet category, and a widget has no report behind it to fail. Add your own tiles from the three Fleet reports. See
[Build your dashboard](/docs/reports/build-your-overview-dashboard).
</Callout>

Technicians reach the board at Reports \u2192 Overview. The Dashboard page itself is for owners, admins and
dispatchers.

## Instructors, students, and renters

None of these roles can open <Where app="web">Reports</Where>. The route sends them to their own home page instead.
They cannot open <Where app="web">Dashboard</Where> or <Where app="web">Audit Logs</Where> either.

They are not left without numbers. Everyone can read their own record: their own hours, flights, invoices,
currencies, documents and training. An instructor can additionally read a student's flight list, training and
instruction sections. See
[See one student's hours or one aircraft's utilization](/docs/reports/member-and-aircraft-activity).

## Why the Reports page sent you to your own home page

Because your roles do not include owner, admin, dispatcher or technician. The Reports route only admits those four,
so an instructor or a student following a link to it lands on their own page rather than on an error.

If you hold one of those roles at a **different** school, check which school you are signed in to. Roles are per
school.

## Why whole headings are missing from the rail

Reports you cannot run are absent, not greyed out.

<Callout type="note">
People go looking for a padlock or an upgrade prompt, and there is not one. The list of reports is built for you
personally when the page loads, so a category you cannot run has no heading, no rows and nothing to click. A
shorter rail is the permission model working, not a fault.
</Callout>

If your roles grant nothing at all, the page reads "No reports available".

## Audit Logs is owner and admin only

<Where app="web">Audit Logs</Where> is hidden from the navigation and refused at the route for anybody below admin.
There is no partial view of that feed. The one trail below admin is the Activity list on a booking, which any
member who can open the booking can read. See [Find out who changed something](/docs/reports/audit-log).

## What a shared saved view can and cannot expose

Turning on "Share with the school" makes a view visible to everyone **who can already run that report**. It never
grants access.

So sharing a Revenue view does not show revenue to a dispatcher: they cannot run Revenue, so the view simply does
not appear in their list. And if your own roles change so you can no longer run a report, its views disappear from
your list too, shared ones included.

## Scheduled reports respect the same rules

Three things follow from the category model.

- The Scheduled reports page only lists schedules whose underlying report you can run. A dispatcher cannot learn
  that a revenue report goes out weekly.
- Recipients are re-checked on every send. Someone whose roles change stops receiving that report from the next
  send onwards, and the schedule's card says so.
- An outside email address keeps receiving a report only while the person who set the schedule up still has access
  to it.

See [Email a report on a schedule](/docs/reports/email-a-report-on-a-schedule) and
[A scheduled report did not arrive](/docs/reports/scheduled-report-not-arriving).
