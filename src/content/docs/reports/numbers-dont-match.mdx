<Summary>
Nearly every "these numbers are wrong" question comes down to one of three things: which date the window applies
to, a flight that was never closed out, or a column that is measured to today rather than to the end of your
window. Work down this list in order.
</Summary>

## Revenue and Payments received do not agree

They are not supposed to.

Revenue is windowed on the date an invoice was **raised**. Payments received is windowed on the date it was
**paid**. An invoice raised in June and paid in August is June's revenue and August's cash, so at any school where
somebody pays late the two reports will differ over the same window and both are correct.

<Screenshot id="revenue-vs-payments" />

Check the caption under each report's date picker before reconciling anything against anything else. See
[Date ranges, date basis, and your school's time zone](/docs/reports/date-ranges-and-time-zones).

## A dash is not a zero

A dash means no value was recorded. Zero means it really was zero.

An aircraft booked for 13 hours whose flights were never closed out shows a blank Flown and a blank Efficiency, not
"0.0 h" and "0%". Printing zero would claim the aircraft sat idle, which is a different and wrong finding.

The usual cause is a flight nobody closed out. See
[Ramp out and ramp in](/docs/scheduling/ramp-out-and-ramp-in).

## The Totals row has blank cells

Same rule, one level up. If a whole column is dashes, its total is blank too.

A total is also blank where there is no honest way to add the column up. You cannot sum a tail number or a status.

## Efficiency looks terrible on a fleet that is busy

Efficiency on Utilization is flown divided by booked. An overnight trip holds the aircraft for the whole trip, so
its booked hours are enormous next to its flown hours. The aircraft was away earning, not sitting idle.

<Callout type="tip">
Group by Overnight to read the two populations apart, and compare **Billed** rather than **Flown** on trips,
because an overnight minimum is charged on top of what was actually flown. See
[Charge a minimum for overnight trips](/docs/billing/charge-a-minimum-for-overnight-trips).
</Callout>

## Utilization is missing bookings you know happened

Cancelled bookings are excluded from Utilization and Flight log entirely. They held the aircraft, so people expect
them in the booked figure, and they are not there.

They have their own report: Cancellations &amp; no-shows.

## Maintenance due looks almost empty

On Maintenance due, the date range is when the reminder was **raised**, not when it comes due. A 30 day window
therefore hides most of what is outstanding.

Widen the range, or filter Status to Overdue, to see everything.

## A voided invoice changed the totals

Voided invoices are excluded from every financial report: Revenue, Payments received, Items sold and Tax collected.

So a school that voids and reissues sees one figure, not two, which is right but will not match a manual tally that
counted both. And an invoice voided without a replacement takes its money out of the reports entirely. See
[Mark an invoice paid, void it, or refund it](/docs/billing/mark-an-invoice-paid-void-or-refund-it).

## A saved view stopped matching anything

Check its date range first. It is almost always a fixed window that has aged out.

A saved view stores the exact dates that were on screen when you saved it, presets included. "Last 30 days" is
resolved to real dates at the moment you save.

Fix it by resetting the range and saving the view again. See
[Save a report as a view](/docs/reports/save-a-report-view).

## A filter dropdown is missing somebody

Aircraft and people filters match on the label, meaning the tail number or the person's display name, not on an
internal record id. Two consequences:

- A member with no name set does not appear in the dropdown at all.
- Two members with identical display names cannot be told apart by a filter.

If you need to isolate one of two identically named people, filter on something else they do not share, or group
the report by a different dimension.

## Two people see different Last 30 days totals

That happens when nobody has set the school's time zone. With no organization zone, each reader's window falls back
to their own browser's zone, so a colleague two time zones away genuinely sees a slightly different thirty days.

Fix it in <Where app="web">Settings → Organization</Where>. See
[Date ranges, date basis, and your school's time zone](/docs/reports/date-ranges-and-time-zones).

## Hours look ten times too big

On screen and in a CSV export, hours are always real hours. Internally AerScheduler stores every hour figure in
tenths, and both the screen and the export convert before you ever see them.

So a figure that is exactly ten times too large means you are reading a raw value from outside the console. If you
are seeing it inside the console, that is a fault and worth telling us about.

## Two exports of the same thing came out different

Two ordinary reasons.

- **Columns measured to today.** "Days since last flight", ", Days open" and "Days left" are counted to today, not to
  the end of the window you picked, so they move overnight even on a historic report.
- **New data landed.** A report is recomputed every time it runs. A flight closed out between your two exports
  changes both exports and both were right when they were taken.

## A dashboard tile and its report disagree

Check the tile's own date range. A tile defaults to inheriting the dashboard's window, but it can be given its own,
and an overridden tile is labelled "· pinned" under its title. That is how "Revenue this week" and "Revenue this
month" can sit side by side.

Then check the comparison select, which changes only the percentage, not the figure.

Nine counts sit outside this altogether: the Needs attention items each carry their own window and ignore the
dashboard's date range entirely. See [Work the Needs attention list](/docs/reports/needs-attention).

## The Dashboard page does not agree with Reports

<Where app="web">Dashboard</Where> is the console home page, and its stat cards are not built by the report engine.

"Outstanding" there is the sum of every unpaid invoice with no date window at all. "Outstanding" on the Reports
Overview is the report engine's figure over the window you picked. They answer different questions and will differ.
Use Reports when you want a number you can defend.

## A tile disappeared from your dashboard

A tile is dropped, with a warning toast naming it, when it points at a report you no longer have access to or at a
metric or dimension the report no longer offers. The rest of the board still renders, so the board is simply one
card shorter.

Rebuild it, or ask an admin about the role change. See
[Who can see which reports](/docs/reports/who-can-see-which-reports).

## A student's progress looks stuck after a lesson

Two things to check on Student progress.

- Only lessons the student **passed** count toward "% complete". A lesson graded unsatisfactory counts as signed
  but not done.
- Lessons done and hours accumulated move independently, because one flight can credit several requirements at
  once. Neither number predicts the other.

See [Read a training record](/docs/training/read-a-training-record).

## Requirements short reads zero when your syllabus says otherwise

"Requirements short" counts FAA-sourced requirements only.

If your school sets a stricter bar than the regulation does, a student who is short of your bar but clear of the
reg is not reported here as a deficiency. That is deliberate: the column answers "can this student be
recommended", not "has this student met our house minimums".

## An endorsement disappeared but an old document did not

The two compliance reports deliberately behave differently.

| Report | What happens when the record is superseded |
|---|---|
| Endorsement expirations | The old endorsement is excluded and disappears from the report |
| Document expirations | The archived document is kept, hidden behind the status filter |

So "show me what I replaced" is one click away for documents and not available for endorsements. See
[Endorsements about to expire](/docs/training/endorsements-about-to-expire).

## Somebody with no expiry date is missing from the expirations report

Documents, currencies and endorsements with no expiry date never appear on their reports at all. Those reports
window on the expiry date, so a blank expiry cannot match any window, and the row is missing rather than listed
with an empty cell.

<Callout type="warning">
If you are auditing coverage, do not read those reports as a complete roster. A missing person may have no expiry
date recorded rather than nothing outstanding.
</Callout>
