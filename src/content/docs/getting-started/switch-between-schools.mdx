## How one account holds two schools

Your account is global. Your **membership** of a school is what holds your roles, your
identifier, your membership plan and your notification preferences. Join a second school
and you get a second membership: two sets of roles, two rosters, two sets of switches.

Nothing is shared between schools. Not aircraft, not people, not bookings, not invoices.

## Switch

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Click the organization name and logo at the top of the left rail">
    With only one school it is a label, not a dropdown. It becomes a dropdown the moment you
    belong to two.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Pick the other school">
    You land on that school's home page: the Dashboard if you are staff there, your own home
    page otherwise.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Switching is a full reload, closer to signing out and back in than to changing a filter.
Anything you have half typed is lost, and you do not come back to the page you were on.
Save first.
</Callout>

## What changes when you switch

- Your roles. You can be an instructor at one school and a renter at the next, so buttons
  appear and disappear.
- Everything on screen: schedule, roster, fleet, invoices, reports.
- Your notification preferences, which are set per school.
- The order of your navigation rail, your pinned pages and your Recent list, which are all
  remembered separately per school.

<Callout type="note">
Rail order, pins and Recent are stored on the device you are using, per school. Arrange the
rail at the front desk and your laptop at home still shows the default order. That is not a
sync failure, it is where the setting lives.
</Callout>

## In the iOS app

The switcher is under <Where app="mobile">Settings → Switch organization</Where>, not in the
navigation menu. That is the first place people look and the one place it is not.

## Joining the second school

Exactly like the first: get its code, then
[join with a code](/docs/getting-started/join-a-school). If you are already signed in, go
straight to app.aerscheduler.com/join.

## Leaving one

<Where app="web">Profile & account</Where> at the foot of the page, or
<Where app="mobile">Settings → Leave organization</Where> in the iOS app. Type the school
name to confirm. You cannot leave a school where you are the only owner, or the only admin.
Your flight and billing history stays with the school.
