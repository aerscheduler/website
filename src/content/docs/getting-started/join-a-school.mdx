<Summary>
Create an account at app.aerscheduler.com, verify your email, then type your school's code
at <Where app="web">app.aerscheduler.com/join</Where>. Either you are in immediately, or you
see "Request sent" and an admin at the school lets you in.
</Summary>

## What you need

One of two things, both of which come from the school:

- A **join code**, a short code that identifies the school. In the iOS app the school will
  call it the Organization Invitation Code.
- An **invitation email**, sent to your address by an admin. The link in it carries the
  roles you will get.

If you have the invitation, you do not need the code.

## Join with a code

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Create your account">
    Go to app.aerscheduler.com and choose **Create your account**: name, email and a
    password, or **Sign up with Google** / **Sign up with Apple**.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Verify your email">
    Click the link we email you. Every page will send you back to **Verify your email**
    until you do. That page moves on by itself the moment you click the link, so leave it
    open.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Choose I'm joining an organization">
    That is the first card on the "What brings you to AerScheduler?" screen. If you are
    already signed in and past that screen, go straight to
    <Where app="web">app.aerscheduler.com/join</Where>, or in the app to
    <Where app="mobile">Settings → Join an organization</Where>.

    <Screenshot id="join-school-code" />
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Type the code and press Join school">
    Either you land on the booking page as a member, or you see **Request sent**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If you see "Request sent"

Your school approves people before they join. Your request is sitting in a panel above
their People roster, and an admin has to accept it and give you a role. Nothing else
happens until they do, so there is nothing to retry: ask the front desk to look at
**People**.

## If you were invited by email

Click the link in the invitation. You are put into the school immediately with the roles
the invitation carried, whether or not the school approves people before they join. If the
invitation also named a membership plan, that is applied as pending, and the school starts
it when they are ready.

## Common messages

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "You already belong to this organization" | You are in. Sign in and use the school switcher at the top of the left rail |
| "Request sent" | The school approves new members by hand. Wait for an admin |
| The code is not accepted | Check it with the school. Demo sandbox codes cannot be joined |

## Once you are in

Joining drops you on the booking page. Your home page, **Home** in the left rail, is where
you will spend most of your time: upcoming reservations, unpaid invoices, currencies that
need attention, and the school's announcements.

If the **Book** link and the **+** button are missing, you were accepted without a role.
That is easily fixed: ask an admin to give you student, renter, instructor or technician
from <Where app="web">People → ⋯ → Edit roles</Where>.

<Callout type="note">
Flying at two schools is normal and supported. Join the second one the same way, with its
own code, and switch between them from the top of the left rail. See
[Fly at more than one school](/docs/getting-started/switch-between-schools).
</Callout>
