## Where updates appear

| | The card | Every update |
|---|---|---|
| **Web console** | Bottom-left corner of the window. On a phone, at the foot of the menu | The menu under your organization's name at the top of the rail → **What's new** |
| **iOS and Android app** | Home, under the quick actions | Menu (top left) → **What's new** |

A blue dot marks new updates you have not opened yet. Older ones are listed without a dot.

## Read an update

Click or tap the card. The update opens with a picture, what changed and how to use it. The
button at the bottom takes you straight to the screen it is about, and **Read the guide**
opens the help article on aerscheduler.com. In the console, the arrow on the card opens that
article directly.

## Hide the card

Press the **×** on the card. Opening the update, or reading its guide, hides the card too.
Either way it stays hidden on every device you sign in on, so closing it in the console also
clears it from your phone. The update itself stays in the **What's new** list.

## Why you see some updates and not others

- **Updates are written for roles.** Owners and admins see changes to billing and settings,
  students and renters see changes to booking, technicians see maintenance. If you hold
  different roles in another organization, you see what fits the one you are signed in to.
- **One card at a time.** When several are waiting, the newest shows first, with the edge of
  the next one peeking out underneath in the console. The next appears on a later visit, never
  the moment you close one.
- **A card shows for 30 days.** After that the update is only in the list, without a dot. If you
  have been away a while, you get the recent ones one at a time and the rest are waiting in the
  list.
- **New accounts start clean.** Anything that shipped before you joined is in the list but not
  marked new, and never gets a card.

## Every update, for every role

The [changelog](/changelog) lists every update we have published, newest first, including the ones
written for roles you do not hold. **All updates on aerscheduler.com** at the bottom of the What's
new list opens it.
