## What is yours and what belongs to the school

Your account is global: name, email, password and photo follow you everywhere. Your roles,
your identifier, your membership plan and your notification preferences belong to your
membership of one school.

That is why changing your name changes it everywhere, and why turning off reservation email
at one school leaves it on at the other.

## The tabs

| Tab | What is on it |
|---|---|
| Profile | Name and photo, your membership plan, contact details, emergency contacts, and your time zone |
| Security | Your password, and the PIN you sign close-outs with |
| Calendar | Connect Google Calendar so your bookings appear in it |
| Availability | Your teaching hours. Instructors only |
| Payment methods | Saved cards, your default card, and autopay |

Old links to `/me/availability` and `/me/payment-methods` still work and land on the right
tab.

## Your PIN

Your PIN is how you sign off a flight at close-out. Set it, or change it, from the
**Security** tab. The button reads **Set PIN** until you have one, and **Update PIN**
afterwards.

<Screenshot id="profile-security-pin" />

An invoice is raised when the last person on a booking signs off, so a school where nobody
has set a PIN is a school where nothing gets billed. Full detail in
[Sign off a flight with your PIN](/docs/scheduling/sign-off-with-your-pin).

## Time zone: two settings, two owners

This is the part people write in about, so it is worth thirty seconds.

**The school's time zone** is set by an admin on
<Where app="web">Settings → School → Organization → Time zone</Where>, under "Where you fly
from". It decides what time a 9am lesson actually is, and it is the zone your booking emails
and push notifications are stamped in.

**Your time zone** is on your Profile tab, and it is two separate choices:

1. Which zone is yours: follow this device, or pin one.
2. Whether the schedule is drawn in **airport time** or in yours.

<Screenshot id="profile-time-zone-card" />

<Callout type="important">
Neither setting ever moves anybody's booking. They change the clock you read the schedule
against, nothing else. A 9am lesson is 9am at the airport whichever way you set this.
</Callout>

Airport time is the recommended option, and it is what you get by default once the school
has set a zone. It is the right answer for almost everybody: the aircraft is at the airport,
and so is the instructor.

Pin a zone instead if you are away and want the schedule to read in local time while you are
travelling.

<Callout type="note">
If the school has never set a zone, the schedule falls back to each person's own device
zone, which means two people can genuinely see different times for the same booking. It also
blocks multi-day bookings, which cannot be turned on until time zones are configured.
</Callout>

More on how times are drawn and reported in
[Airport time and time zones](/docs/scheduling/airport-time-and-time-zones).

## Google Calendar

The **Calendar** tab connects your own Google Calendar so your bookings show up there. It is
a personal connection, not a school-wide one, so each person connects their own.

Each event is titled with the aircraft (or simulator / room) and the booking type, for
example `N172SP · Solo`, so a glance on your phone is enough to know which plane you are
on. The event location is the airport address from the booking, which lets Apple Calendar
and Google Maps offer travel-time suggestions. The description lists who is on the
booking and any notes you saved.

## Availability, for instructors

The **Availability** tab only exists if you hold the instructor role. It is your teaching
hours, day by day, with the days you do not teach showing as Unavailable. See
[Set your weekly availability](/docs/scheduling/set-your-weekly-availability).

## Payment methods

Save a card, set a default, and turn on autopay so new invoices are charged without you
doing anything. See
[Pay an invoice and save a card](/docs/billing/pay-an-invoice-and-save-a-card).
