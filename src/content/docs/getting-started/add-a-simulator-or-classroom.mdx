## What counts as a resource

A resource is anything a booking can hold: an aircraft, a simulator, or a ground-school
room. Aircraft live under **Aircraft**. Simulators and rooms live under **Facilities**,
which is an admin-only page.

<Callout type="tip">
Only aircraft count toward your subscription. Simulators and ground-school rooms are free,
so there is no reason to leave them off the schedule.
</Callout>

## Add one

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Facilities">
    The page has a left rail with three sections, **Locations**, **Simulators** and
    **Rooms**. You can view simulators and rooms as a grid or as a list.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Pick the section, then press Add simulator or Add room">
    Simulators carry a name, a location, Hobbs and tach readings, an hourly rate, and a
    Grounded or Available state. Rooms carry a room number and a location.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Fill the form and save">
    It appears on the dispatch board straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Both need a location, the same as an aircraft. If none exists yet, the form shows an **Add a
location** button that takes you to <Where app="web">Facilities → Locations</Where> with the
create form open.

## What you can book them for

- A **Sim** reservation takes a simulator.
- A **Ground** reservation takes a room, or no resource at all.

Which roles may book each type is in
[Which reservation type should I use?](/docs/scheduling/reservation-types).

<Callout type="note">
Renters and technicians never see simulator or classroom lanes on the dispatch board. That
is by role, not by anything you have configured. If a renter says the sim is not there,
that is why.
</Callout>

## Finding one again

Simulators and rooms have no record page of their own, unlike an aircraft. Searching for
one takes you back to **Facilities** with the search already filled in and the right
section selected.

Filters on the page: **Location** for both, plus **Status** for simulators.

## A grounded member can still use them

Grounding stops a member booking aircraft. It does not stop them being put on a simulator
session, a classroom ground lesson, or any booking with no resource. See
[Ground, archive or remove a member](/docs/getting-started/ground-archive-or-remove-a-member).
