<Summary>
The iPhone app is the same account as the console, narrowed to what a pilot needs away from
a desk: your schedule, booking, ramping out and in, closing out, squawks and lesson grading.
Anything an admin can do at a desk is in the console. The app is better on the ramp, and it
is the only place push notifications actually arrive.
</Summary>

## Which one should I use?

Use the console at app.aerscheduler.com for anything that involves the whole operation:
settings, billing, reports, the roster, the fleet, facilities, audit logs, posting
announcements, and leaving or deleting a school.

Use the app for the flight in front of you. It is built for the ramp and the aircraft, not
for the office.

## What the app is genuinely better at

- **Grading at the aircraft.** The app holds a grade written with no signal and syncs it
  later. See [Grade lessons offline on the iPhone app](/docs/training/grade-lessons-offline-on-the-app).
- **Ramping in and out.** It is a phone in your pocket on the ramp, and the console is not.
- **Push delivery.** A cancelled booking reaches you in a minute, wherever you are. You can
  choose which push categories you get from either surface; the alerts themselves only land
  on a phone with the app installed.

## What both do

- See the schedule and your own bookings.
- Book, edit and cancel a reservation.
- Ramp out, ramp in, close out, and sign off with your PIN.
- Read the roster and the fleet, and open a person or a tail.
- Log a squawk, verify one, resolve one, and read the maintenance boards.
- Grade a lesson and read a training record.
- Post, edit and delete announcements (admins).
- Read announcements and your notifications.
- Join or switch schools, leave a school, and delete one (owners).
- Invoice a member or a guest by hand.
- Choose email and push notification categories.
- Correct recorded times, override a booking's rates, and raise the invoice for a flight
  that closed out without one.
- Update an aircraft's home base on ramp in, when the school has that setting on.

## What the console can do that the app cannot

Everything school-wide, essentially:

- **Settings**, all twelve sections.
- **Facilities**, for locations, simulators and rooms. The app can add a location; only the
  console can edit or remove one.
- **Reports**, saved views, scheduled reports and the overview dashboard.
- The **Billing** page list: every invoice, marking paid, voiding, payment reminders.
- **Audit Logs**, **Cancellations**, **Go / No-Go**, and the **Dashboard** with its setup
  checklist.

## Signing in

The same email and password, or Sign in with Google or Apple. If you belong to more than one
school, switch between them from the org switcher in the console rail, or from
<Where app="mobile">Settings → Switch organization</Where> in the app.

## Android

There is no Android app today. Android users run the console in the phone's browser, which
works, and get email rather than push.
