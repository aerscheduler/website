## Two places, two different jobs

| | Where | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** (the bell) | Topbar in the console, the Inbox bubble in the app | Everything that has happened to you, with an unread count. Paged and searchable |
| **Notification settings** | Account menu → Notification settings | Which of those things also reach you by email or push |

In the iOS app, tapping a notification takes you to the thing it is about.

## Choose which emails and push alerts you get

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open the account menu at the foot of the left rail and choose Notification settings">
    Or go straight to app.aerscheduler.com/me/notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Leave Email notifications and Push notifications on">
    Each channel has its own master switch at the top of its card. Everything below is a category.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Turn off the categories you do not want">
    Each one saves immediately. There is no Save button. Email and push are independent: turning
    reservation email off does not turn reservation push off.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## The categories

| Group | Covers | Who sees the group |
|---|---|---|
| Organization | A member joining your school | Admins |
| Reservations | A booking created, updated, cancelled or completed | Everyone |
| Billing | An invoice received, paid or declined, and payment reminders | Everyone |
| Documents | A document about to expire | Everyone |
| Currency | A currency about to expire | Everyone |
| Endorsements | An endorsement about to expire | Instructors and admins |
| Maintenance | Inspection reminders | Admins and technicians |
| Status | Being grounded | Everyone |
| Announcements | School-wide notices from admins | Everyone |

Groups you would never receive anything from are hidden rather than shown greyed out, which
is why two people at the same school see different pages.

<Callout type="note">
Every category starts **on**. A switch you have never touched behaves as on, so a quiet
category means nothing is happening, not that it is off.
</Callout>

## Push delivery still needs the phone

You can choose push categories from the console. The alerts themselves only land on a device
with the AerScheduler iOS app signed in. Android users get email instead.

## What the master switch does not stop

<Callout type="warning">
Turning **Email notifications** off does not stop all email. Account email still sends:
invitations, email verification and password resets. Those are how you get back into your
account, so they are not optional.
</Callout>

## Preferences are per school

If you fly at two schools you have two sets of switches. Switch schools first, then set
them. See [Fly at more than one school](/docs/getting-started/switch-between-schools).
