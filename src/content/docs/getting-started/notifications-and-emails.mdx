## Two places, two different jobs

| | Where | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Notifications** (the bell) | Topbar in the console, the Inbox bubble in the app | Everything that has happened to you, with an unread count. Paged and searchable |
| **Notification settings** | Your organization's name → Notification settings | Which of those things also reach you by email or push |

In the iOS or Android app, tapping a notification takes you to the thing it is about. The
maintenance shop's notices (requests, jobs, findings, an owner's aircraft) open in the web
console; the app does not have those pages yet.

In the web console, the bell and unread count update live when something new lands,
without refreshing the page.

<Steps>

## The categories

| Group | Covers | Who sees the group |
|---|---|---|
| Organization | A member joining your school | Admins |
| Reservations | A booking created, updated, cancelled, completed, a reminder to ramp in or close one out, review reminders, and standby / slot offers | Everyone |
| Billing | An invoice received, paid or declined; payment reminders; ledger top-ups, auto-refill, late-fee reminders and statements when Account ledger is on | Everyone |
| Documents | A document about to expire | Everyone |
| Currency | A currency about to expire | Everyone |
| Endorsements | An endorsement about to expire | Instructors and admins |
| Maintenance | Inspection reminders | Admins and technicians |
| Maintenance shop | **New requests** from aircraft owners, and **Activity on my jobs** (put on a job; its owner answers, adds photos or updates the times). A **Customer aircraft inspections** setting above the cards picks which customers' aircraft you hear about; until you choose, it shows what you get by default | Technicians and admins, where your organization runs a maintenance shop |
| Your aircraft | **Work on my aircraft** (what the shop sends you, booked in, arrived, ready, the date moved) and **Inspection reminders** | Anyone who owns an aircraft the organization looks after |
| Status | Being grounded | Everyone |
| Announcements | School-wide notices from admins | Everyone |

Groups you would never receive anything from are hidden rather than shown greyed out, which
is why two people at the same school see different pages. An aircraft owner from outside the
organization sees only Billing and Your aircraft.

<Callout type="note">
Every category starts **on**, with one exception: **New requests** starts on for technicians
and off for admins, who turn it on if they want it. In an organization with no technicians signed
in, it starts on for admins too, so a request always reaches somebody; the switch says so. The
**Customer aircraft inspections** setting works the same way: **Aircraft I've worked on** for
technicians, **Off** for admins until they pick one. A switch you have never touched behaves as
its default, so a quiet category means nothing is happening, not that it is off.

Notices from the maintenance shop land in the bell whatever the email and push switches say,
with two exceptions: **New requests**, and inspections coming due on customer aircraft, reach
you (bell included) only while the email or push switch for them is on.
</Callout>

## Push delivery still needs the phone

You can choose push categories from the console. The alerts themselves only land on a device
with the AerScheduler app signed in. Email still arrives if you have no phone app.

## What the master switch does not stop

<Callout type="warning">
Turning **Email notifications** off does not stop all email. Account email still sends:
invitations, email verification and password resets. Those are how you get back into your
account, so they are not optional.
</Callout>

## Preferences are per school

If you fly at two schools you have two sets of switches. Switch schools first, then set
the preferences for that school.
