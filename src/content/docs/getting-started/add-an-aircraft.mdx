## Before you start

At least one location has to exist. Without one the **Home base** picker is disabled and
the form will not save.

If you meet "Add a home base location first, every aircraft needs one before it can be
scheduled"press **Add a location** beside it. That opens
<Where app="web">Facilities → Locations</Where> with the create form already up. Save the
location, come back, and the picker works. You only do this once per airport.

## Add the tail

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Aircraft and press Add aircraft">
    Admins and owners only. Everybody else can view the fleet but not change it.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Enter the tail number">
    This is what appears on the dispatch board, on invoices and in every report.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Optionally pick a Type template">
    Cessna 172, Cessna 152, Piper PA-28, Diamond DA40, Cirrus SR20, or Other. It prefills
    make, model, category and class, fuel capacity and a suggested rate. Everything it
    fills in stays editable.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Fill in make, model, year, and category and class">
    Single or multi-engine, land or sea.
  </Step>
  <Step n={5} title="Enter the current Hobbs and the current tach">
    Type the readings exactly as they show on the meters, for example 1234.5. These are the
    starting point for billing and for every hour-based maintenance inspection, so get them
    off the actual aircraft rather than off a spreadsheet.
  </Step>
  <Step n={6} title="Enter fuel capacity and its unit">
    Gallons or liters.
  </Step>
  <Step n={7} title="Set the hourly rate and the rate basis">
    **Wet** means the rate includes fuel. **Dry** means it does not.
  </Step>
  <Step n={8} title="Decide Bill by Hobbs time">
    On bills from Hobbs time. Off bills from tach time.

    <Screenshot id="aircraft-rate-fields" />
  </Step>
  <Step n={9} title="Choose the Home base and press Add aircraft">
    The home base is the airport the aircraft lives at, and it is what the schedule's time
    zone resolves against.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rate basis and Bill by Hobbs are two different questions

They sit next to each other and both sound like "how is this billed", which is why they get
confused.

| Control | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| Rate basis (wet or dry) | Does the hourly rate include fuel? |
| Bill by Hobbs time | Which meter do the billed hours come from, Hobbs or tach? |

A wet rate billed on tach time is a perfectly normal combination, and so is a dry rate
billed on Hobbs. Pick each one on its own merits.

More on what happens to those numbers at close-out in
[How billing works](/docs/billing/how-billing-works).

## What adding a tail changes

- It appears as a lane on the dispatch board and in every booking form.
- It becomes billable at the rate you set.
- It counts toward your subscription, which is $20 per month per aircraft. Simulators and
  ground-school rooms are free.

## Editing an aircraft later

Open the tail and press **Edit**. It is the same form, except **Type template** is fixed to
**Other**, so a template cannot silently overwrite the real details of an aircraft you have
already been flying.
