<Summary>
Seven roles: **owner, admin, dispatcher, instructor, student, renter, technician**. They
are additive and there is no ladder, so a person can hold any combination or none. Set
them from <Where app="web">People → ⋯ → Edit roles</Where>.
</Summary>

## Roles hang off the school, not the person

Your account (name, email, password, photo) is global. Your roles belong to your
membership of one organization. That is why editing somebody's roles at one school changes
nothing at another, and why a person can be an instructor at one club and a renter at the
next.

## The seven roles in one line each

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Owner | Full control. Always carries admin with it |
| Admin | Runs the school: people, aircraft, money, settings |
| Dispatcher | Runs the board. No money, no settings |
| Instructor | Teaches, signs off flights and lessons |
| Student | Books training flights for themselves |
| Renter | Rents aircraft solo |
| Technician | Logs maintenance and squawks |

Two words appear throughout the product and are worth learning:

- **Staff** means owner, admin and dispatcher together: the people who run the operation
  day to day. Staff land on the Dashboard, see the whole board, and can book on other
  people's behalf.
- Staff is **not** the set that can see money. Money is admin and owner only.

<Callout type="tip">
Roles add up. A student who is also a renter can book everything a student can, plus
rentals. If somebody is missing a button, the usual fix is one more role, not a different
one.
</Callout>

## Owner

Everything an admin can do, plus:

- Edit another owner's roles.
- Change billing settings and connect Stripe.
- Manage API keys and the QuickBooks connection.
- Schedule deletion of the organization, from <Where app="web">Settings → School</Where> or
  from the app. That starts a 30-day countdown (not an immediate wipe). You type the
  school's name to confirm, and it is refused while any invoice is unpaid. See
  [Delete your school](/docs/getting-started/delete-your-school).

An owner cannot be removed by anybody. The last owner cannot give up the role or leave the
school.

## Admin

Reaches every page in the console. Invites, removes, edits roles, grounds, ungrounds and
archives members. Accepts and declines join requests and instruction pairing requests. Adds,
edits, grounds and deletes aircraft, simulators and rooms, and approves renters on a tail.
Sees and manages all money. Changes every organization setting. Reads anyone's documents,
currencies, training records and endorsements. Books every reservation type on anyone's
behalf.

An admin cannot change an owner's roles, remove an owner, or remove or demote the last
admin (including themselves). An admin who is not the owner cannot connect Stripe or change
billing settings, and cannot schedule deletion of the organization. An admin can cancel a
deletion that is already scheduled.

## Dispatcher

A dispatcher runs the ramp. They create, edit, cancel, ramp out, ramp in and close out every
reservation type for anybody, ground and reinstate a member, read fleet and operations
reports including utilization, read another member's currencies and documents, read the
maintenance boards and open squawks, and see guests.

They cannot see any money at all. No Billing page, no invoices, no outstanding balances, no
revenue, no per-person spend.

<Callout type="note">
A dispatcher opening the Dashboard sees three stat cards, not four, and no Recent invoices
panel. That is deliberate, not a loading failure.
</Callout>

They also cannot open Settings, Facilities or Audit Logs, sign a squawk off as resolved or
verified, or read a student's training record or endorsements. Grounding is theirs, but
archiving and removing a member are not: those stay owner and admin.

## Instructor

Reaches the Calendar, People, Aircraft and Training, plus their own pages. Books dual,
solo, shared, ground, sim and guest reservations. Sees their students' flights, training
records and endorsements. Closes out and signs off their own flights. Sets their own
availability, which is a tab that exists only for instructors.

An instructor cannot open the Dashboard, Billing, Reports, Facilities, Settings,
Maintenance, Go / No-Go, Cancellations or Audit Logs. They cannot book a rental or a
maintenance block, cannot read another member's documents, and cannot invite, ground,
archive or remove anybody. Designing a syllabus needs a separate training permission: see
[Who can do what in training](/docs/training/who-can-do-what-in-training).

With **Instructors can override reservation prices** switched on, they get **Override
payment** on a booking, on either surface. It is not limited to their own reservations.

## Student

Views the school schedule, the roster and the fleet. Books dual, solo, shared, ground and
sim reservations for themselves. Sees their own training record, endorsements, currencies,
documents and invoices (or their ledger balance when Account ledger is on), and pays their
own invoices or adds funds.

A student cannot book a rental, see anyone else's money, currencies, documents or training
records, see open squawks, or open any staff page. With **Members can only book approved
resources** on, they can book only the aircraft they have been checked out on. See
[Check someone out on an aircraft](/docs/getting-started/approve-a-member-on-an-aircraft).

## Renter

Views the schedule, the roster and the fleet. Books rentals, and shared bookings (two
renters splitting a cross-country). Sees and pays their own invoices (or uses their ledger
when Account ledger is on), and sees their own currencies and documents.

A renter cannot book dual, solo, ground, sim, guest or maintenance reservations, and never
sees simulator or classroom lanes on the board. With **Members can only book approved
resources** on, they can book only the aircraft they have been checked out on. See
[Check someone out on an aircraft](/docs/getting-started/approve-a-member-on-an-aircraft).

## Technician

Reaches Maintenance and the fleet reports. Books maintenance blocks, which take an aircraft
off the line. Logs squawks and signs them off as resolved or verified. Sees maintenance
reminders and inspections across the fleet, and can ground an aircraft.

A technician sees no money anywhere, cannot book any flying reservation, never sees
simulator or classroom lanes, and cannot open the Dashboard, Billing, Settings, Facilities,
Training, Go / No-Go or Audit Logs.

## Somebody with no role

A member who joined and was never given a role can sign in, see the schedule, the roster
and the fleet, and see their own profile, documents, currencies and invoices. They cannot
book anything: the **+** button and the Book link simply do not appear. They show up under
the **No role yet** filter on People.

## Change somebody's roles

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open People, or the person's record">
    From the roster, use the row's **⋯** menu. From their page, press **Edit roles** in the
    header.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Tick the roles they should hold">
    Ticking **Owner** force-ticks **Admin** and locks it, because an owner is always an
    admin.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Press Save roles">
    Access changes immediately, everywhere: console, phone app and API.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Three refusals you may meet:

- "There must be at least one owner in your organization."
- You cannot remove the last admin, including your own admin grant.
- "You do not have permission to update the owner's roles." Only another owner can edit an
  owner.

## Two places roles behave unexpectedly

Reports are open to dispatchers and technicians, but each sees only the categories their
role grants. A dispatcher gets operations, fleet, people and compliance reports and no
financial section at all. A technician gets fleet reports only. See
[Who can see which reports](/docs/reports/who-can-see-which-reports).

Search is scoped by role too, and training is scoped differently from everything else. A
dispatcher can search another member's documents and currencies but not their training
records. An instructor is the exact opposite. See
[Find anything with search](/docs/getting-started/find-anything).
