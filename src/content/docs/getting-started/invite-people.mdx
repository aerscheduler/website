## Invitation or join code?

| | Invitation | Join code |
|---|---|---|
| How it reaches them | An email to a specific address | You tell them the code |
| Roles | Attached in advance, applied on acceptance | Chosen by you when you accept the request, or none at all |
| Approval | None needed. An invited person joins immediately | Depends on whether your school approves people before they join |
| Best for | Your instructors and your existing roster | A poster on the wall, a link in a welcome pack |

An outstanding invitation beats the approval setting. An invited person joins straight
away, code or not.

## Send invitations

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open People and press Invite">
    The button only appears for admins and owners.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Type one email, or paste a whole roster">
    The **Email** box takes one address. The **Bulk invite** box takes a list, one address
    per line, and commas and semicolons split too. The hint underneath tells you how many
    valid addresses it found.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Tick the roles for this batch">
    Admin, Instructor, Student, Renter, Dispatcher, Technician.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Optionally choose a membership plan">
    Only if your school runs membership plans. It is applied as pending when they accept,
    and nothing is charged until you start it from their record. See
    [Add a member to a plan](/docs/billing/add-a-member-to-a-plan).
  </Step>
  <Step n={5} title="Press Send invite">
    Each address gets an email with a link that puts them into your school with exactly
    those roles. They appear on People once they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Everyone in one batch gets the **same** roles. Two role sets means two invites. Send the
instructors, then send the students.
</Callout>

Duplicate addresses are removed and invalid ones are dropped without stopping the send. If
part of a batch fails you are told: "Invited 4 people. 1 couldn't be sent."

<Callout type="note">
**Owner** is not offered at invite time. Invite the person as an admin, then grant owner
afterwards from <Where app="web">People → ⋯ → Edit roles</Where>.
</Callout>

## The shorter invite flow

While you are still setting the school up, the dashboard offers a shorter version of this
that asks who you are inviting (Instructor, Student, Renter, Technician, Office staff) and
sets the roles for you. It reaches the same place.

**Office staff** means dispatcher: runs the board, no settings and no billing.

## Or hand out your join code

Your code is on <Where app="web">Settings → School → Organization → Identity</Where> with a
copy button, and in the iOS app under
<Where app="mobile">Organization settings → Organization Invitation Code</Where>. People
enter it at app.aerscheduler.com/join or in the app.

What happens next depends on one setting:

- **Approve people before they join is off** (the default): the code alone lets them in.
- **It is on**: the code creates a join request, and nothing happens until an admin acts.

That switch is set during setup, from the booking rules step on your dashboard, or in the
iOS app under
<Where app="mobile">Organization settings → Joining Organization Restriction</Where>.

## Accept or decline a join request

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open People">
    A yellow **Join requests (N)** panel sits above the roster whenever anybody is waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Choose a starting role for each person">
    Student, Renter, Instructor, Technician, Dispatcher, Admin, or **No role yet**.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Press Accept, or the × to decline">
    Accepting makes them a member with that role and lets them sign in to your school.
    Declining removes the request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Accepting with **No role yet** creates a member who can see the schedule, the roster and
the fleet but cannot book anything at all. If a new student says the Book button is
missing, look for them under the **No role yet** role filter and give them a role.
</Callout>

Two things about that panel worth knowing:

- It is invisible when the queue is empty, so a school that has never turned approval on
  will never see it. If somebody says "I entered the code and nothing happened", this panel
  is where they are.
- The count in the heading is the whole queue, not the page. Forty people waiting reads as
  40 even though 25 fit on screen.
