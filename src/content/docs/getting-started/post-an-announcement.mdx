<Summary>
<Where app="web">Operations → Announcements → New announcement</Where>. Enter a title and a
message, optionally set an expiry, and post. Members see it on the board and on home; those
with the iOS app can get a push.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins</Roles>

Use this for anything the whole school needs to know: a runway closure, a schedule change, a
new checklist, or a reminder about paperwork.

## Post one

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Operations → Announcements">
    Owners and admins see a <strong>New announcement</strong> button. Everybody else can still
    read the board.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Enter a title and a message">
    Title is required and capped at 60 characters. Message is optional, up to 600 characters.

    <Screenshot id="announcement-form-dialog" />
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Set an expiry if you want one">
    Leave Expires as Never to keep the notice until you delete it. Expired notices leave the
    live board.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Click Post announcement">
    The notice appears on the board immediately. Members with push enabled and the iOS app
    installed get an alert.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit or delete

Open the menu on a notice and choose Edit or Delete. Deleting removes it for everyone.
