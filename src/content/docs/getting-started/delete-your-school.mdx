</Summary>

## What happens when you delete

Deleting a school is not instant. It starts a **30-day countdown**:

1. An owner confirms from <Where app="web">Settings → Security</Where> (or Organization
   Settings in the iOS app).
2. AerScheduler emails and notifies every admin and owner with the deletion date.
3. Until that date, the school keeps working as usual: bookings, invoices, training,
   everything.
4. Any admin or owner can cancel from the same Settings place.
5. If nobody cancels, the school and everything in it is permanently removed on the date.

<Callout type="important">
After the countdown ends there is no undo. Bookings, invoices, training records,
documents and every member's access go with the school.
</Callout>

## Schedule deletion

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Settings → Security">
    Only an owner can schedule deletion. Settle every unpaid invoice first: the server
    refuses while anything is still owed.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Click Delete organization">
    Type the school's exact name to confirm. That cannot be done by accident.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Confirm Schedule deletion">
    The card switches to Deletion scheduled with the date. Every admin and owner gets an
    email and an in-app notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the iOS app the same flow is under Organization Settings → Delete Organization.

## Cancel a scheduled deletion

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Settings → Security">
    While a countdown is running, the destructive card is replaced by Deletion scheduled
    and a Cancel deletion button. Admins see this too, not only owners.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Click Cancel deletion">
    The countdown clears immediately. Nothing has been removed. Admins and owners get a
    short notice that it was cancelled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the iOS app: Organization Settings → Cancel Deletion.

## Gotchas

- **Unpaid invoices block scheduling**, not cancelling. Pay or void them first.
- **Only an owner can start the countdown.** An admin who is not an owner cannot.
- **Cancelling is admin or owner.** If you received the email, you can stop it.
- **The demo sandbox cannot be deleted.** That control is turned off there on purpose.
- **Leaving a school is different.** Leave removes only your membership. Delete removes
  the whole school after 30 days. See
  [Switch between schools](/docs/getting-started/switch-between-schools).
