<Summary>
All three live on <Where app="web">People → the row's ⋯ menu</Where>. **Ground** stops
somebody flying and emails them the reason. **Archive** takes them off the roster silently.
**Remove** ends their access to your school. Only removing is permanent, and none of the
three deletes any history.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers (ground and unground only)</Roles>

## Which one do I want?

| | Ground | Archive | Remove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is the member told? | Yes, emailed and pushed the reason | No | They lose access |
| What it blocks | Aircraft bookings only | Every booking type | All access |
| Still on the roster? | Yes, with a red Grounded badge | No, filed under Archived | No |
| Still gets your email? | Yes | No, nothing at all | No |
| Undo | Unground | Return to roster | No |
| Use it for | An expired medical, an unpaid balance, a safety review | A student who stopped coming | Somebody who should not have access |

<Callout type="tip">
If you are tidying up dormant students, **archive** them. Grounding was the only tool for
that job before archiving existed, and it emails everybody you touch.
</Callout>

## Ground a member

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="People, find the person, ⋯ menu, Ground">
    Grounding is not offered for somebody who is already archived.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Pick a reason">
    Missing or expired documents, Missing TSA documentation, Rental agreement not on file,
    Medical certificate expired, Outstanding balance, Currency lapsed, Pending safety
    review, or **Something else...** for free text.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Press Ground member">
    Their row and their record show a red Grounded badge and a banner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="important">
The reason is not a private note. It is emailed and pushed to the member word for word, as
the entire body of the notice, and free text is capped at 60 characters. Write it for them:
"Medical expired 1 Aug, send us the new one" rather than "chase re medical, again".
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
Grounding blocks **aircraft** bookings. A grounded member can still be put on a simulator
session, a classroom ground lesson, or any booking with no resource at all. If you need to
stop somebody completely, archive them.
</Callout>

Ungrounding asks for confirmation and clears the old reason.

### Grounding from the front desk

A dispatcher can ground and unground too, from the same ⋯ menu in the console, from the
member's record page, or from the app. Archiving and removing stay owner and admin, so a
dispatcher's menu offers Ground and Reinstate and nothing else.

### Automatic grounding for unpaid invoices

<Where app="web">Settings → Billing → Billing</Where> has **Ground members with unpaid
invoices**, which grounds somebody once they reach a number of unpaid invoices you set. It
starts off. The refusal a member then sees says they are grounded for unpaid invoices and
that paying them lifts it.

## Archive a member

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="People, find the person, ⋯ menu, Archive">
    You cannot archive your own membership.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Read the confirmation and press Archive member">
    They come off the roster, cannot be booked at all, and stop receiving any email or
    notification from you. Their flights, invoices and history are kept.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="To find them later, set the Roster filter to Archived">
    **Return to roster** on their ⋯ menu brings them back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

They are **not** told, and that silence is the point. It is the difference between
archiving and grounding.

<Callout type="warning">
The Roster filter's neutral position is **Current**, not "Any". Leaving it alone never
shows archived people, so an archived member will look as though they have vanished from
your roster entirely.
</Callout>

A member who is both grounded and archived shows as **Archived**. Archiving wins in the
interface, and it is the stronger stop of the two.

## Remove a member

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="People, ⋯ menu, Remove">
    Then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="They lose access to your school">
    Their flight and billing history is preserved, so your reports and invoices do not
    change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Three things to know:

- An owner cannot be removed at all. Demote them first from **Edit roles**.
- You cannot remove the last admin.
- If you do it from the person's own record page, you are sent back to People, because the
  page you were on no longer exists.

A member can also leave voluntarily, from
<Where app="web">Profile & account</Where>, or <Where app="mobile">Settings → Leave organization</Where> in the iOS app, under the same
last-admin and last-owner rules.
