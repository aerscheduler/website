<Summary>
Open **Messages** (the chat button in the bottom-right corner of every console page, or the
bell in the console's top bar or on Home in the app), then the pencil for a new message. Pick one person for a direct conversation, several for a group, or a people group for the conversation that follows it. On a
booking, use its **Conversation** card to message everyone on it. Type **@** to mention a
person, an aircraft, a booking or a work order.
</Summary>

## Three kinds of conversation

| Kind | Who is in it | Who can read it |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct** | You and one other person | Only the two of you |
| **Group** | The people you picked | Only the people in it |
| **People group** | Everyone in one of your organization's people groups, joining and leaving with it | Only the people in it |
| **Booking** | Everyone on the booking | Everyone on the booking, plus owners, admins and dispatchers |

Direct messages and groups are private, admins included. A booking conversation follows the
booking: the people flying it hear about new messages, and the desk can read and join in.
A dispatcher who writes on a booking's conversation becomes one of its participants.

## Start a conversation

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Open Messages">
In the console, click the chat button in the bottom-right corner of any page, or the bell in
the top bar, then **Messages**. It opens over the page you are on, so you can keep working
beside it, and the chat button shows a count of unread messages while it is closed. Click the
expand arrows to make the panel bigger, with your conversations and the open one side by side;
click them again to bring it back to size. On a phone, Messages already fills the screen. In
the app, tap the bell on Home, then **Messages**, or **+** on Home, then **New Message**.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Choose who it is with">
Click the pencil beside search (or **New message** in the **+** menu), then type names in **To** and pick people from the list. One
person opens your direct conversation with them (there is only ever one). Two or more start a
group, and you can give it a name. People groups you are in are listed above the people
(owners, admins and dispatchers see every people group): pick one to open its conversation.
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Write and send">
Press **Enter** to send in the console, and **Shift+Enter** for a new line. In the app, tap
the blue arrow.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Talk about one booking

Open the booking's page in the console (from the calendar, double-click it) and use the
**Conversation** card. In the app, open the booking and tap **Conversation**. Nothing exists
until somebody writes, so an untouched booking shows only the box to type in.

## Send a photo or a file

In the console, click **+** under the message box and choose **Add photos or files** (or press
**Cmd+U**, **Ctrl+U** on Windows), drop files on the box, or paste a screenshot. Each file starts uploading straight away and waits above the box with a preview;
click its X to take it back out. Press **Enter** when they are up, with or without words.

- Photos (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC), PDFs, and Word, Excel, CSV and text files.
- Up to 10 files on one message, each up to 25 MB.
- Photos show in the conversation; click one to open it full size. Other files show as a card
  that opens the file.
- Only the people in the conversation can open what was sent. Deleting the message removes its
  files for everyone.

## Mention a record with @

Type **@** (or choose **Mention someone** from the **+** menu) and start typing a name, a tail
number or a booking. Pick one from the list and
it becomes a chip in the message. Anyone who can open that record can click or tap the chip
to go straight to it.

<Callout type="note">
Mentions respect each reader's role. If somebody cannot open a work order, they see the words
"a work order" instead of a link. The record never leaks to a person who could not see it
anyway.
</Callout>

Mentioning a person in the conversation always notifies them, even in a burst of messages.

A booking you mention also shows as a card under the message: its name, the date and time in
the booking's own time zone, the aircraft, the people on it, and where it stands (**Upcoming**,
**Now**, **Out now**, **Past** or **Cancelled**). Click the card to open the booking.

An aircraft you mention shows a card too: its tail number, whether it is **Available**, **Out
now** or **Grounded** (and why), its Hobbs and tach, and how many squawks are open. Owners,
admins, dispatchers, instructors and technicians also see its next inspection, in red when it
is overdue. A work order you mention shows its number, where it stands, the aircraft, what was
asked for and whether it has been invoiced or paid; only the people who can open work orders
see it. An aircraft or a work order comes up again and again in a conversation, so its card
sits under the newest message that mentions it, showing where it stands now; earlier mentions
are just the link.

## Message a people group

People groups are the ones in Settings → Groups (the same groups that carry currency rules
and invitations). Type a group's name in **To** and pick it, and Messages opens the
conversation for that group, starting it the first time. There is only ever one per group.

It follows the group:

- **People:** whoever an admin adds to the group in Settings joins the conversation, and whoever
  is taken out leaves it. One line in the conversation says who joined and who left. The person
  who started it, and owners, admins and dispatchers who joined in, stay. As in any group,
  somebody who joins reads what is said from then on.
- **Name:** it is named after the group, and renaming the group renames it.
- **No Leave and no Add people:** change the group in Settings instead, or **Mute** the
  conversation to stop hearing about it.
- **Deleting the group** turns the conversation into an ordinary group with the same people and
  name, which you can then edit like any other.

## Name a group, change its photo and its people

Anybody in a group can change it. Click the group's name at the top of the conversation:

- **Name:** click the name, type a new one, press **Enter**. Clear it to go back to the names of
  the people in it.
- **Photo:** click the picture to choose a photo, or to take it away.
- **People:** **Add people** to search and add someone. Hover over a person and click the X to
  take them out; they keep what was said while they were in it, and can be added back.

Each change shows in the conversation as a short line, such as "Ann added Ben" or "Ann named
the group Saturday ramp". These lines never count as unread and never send a notification.

## React and reply

Hover over any message in the console for its bar: the smile to react, the arrow to reply, and
**More** for **Copy text** (and, on your own, **Edit** and **Delete**).

- **React:** pick one of six reactions. It shows under the message, with a count when more
  than one person picked it; hover it to see who. Click your own to take it back, or another
  to switch. A reaction never sends a notification, so a thumbs-up does not buzz anybody's
  phone the way an "ok" message would.
- **Reply:** the box shows **Replying to** and the line you are answering. Send as usual, and
  your message goes out under a quote of that one; click the quote to jump back to it. Press
  **Escape** or the X to stop replying. The person you answered is notified like a mention.

## See who has read it

Under your newest message, **Seen** appears once the other person has read it. In a group it
says who: **Seen by Ann and Ben**, or **Seen by everyone**. It moves live as people read.

Open a conversation with messages you have not read and a **New messages** line sits above
the first of them, under what you had already read. When there are more than fit on the screen,
the conversation opens at that line instead of the end; the arrow at the bottom takes you to the
newest. The line goes once you send.

While somebody is writing to you, three moving dots show at the bottom of the conversation
(in a group, with their name). They go when the person sends, clears the box or stops for a
few seconds.

## Find a message

Type in the search box above your conversations. People and conversation names match first;
from two letters on, **Messages** lists what was said that matches, newest first, with the
words you typed in bold. Click one to open its conversation scrolled to that message, which
flashes so you can spot it. Search only looks in conversations you are in, and in a group only
at what was said after you joined.

## Pin a conversation or mark it unread

Right-click a conversation in the list (or click its name at the top when it is open):

- **Pin** keeps it at the top of your list, with a pin beside its name, however long ago it
  was last used. You can pin up to 9; the one you pinned first stays first. **Unpin** puts it
  back in order.
- **Mark as unread** gives it a blue dot and counts it as unread, as a reminder to come back
  to it. Opening it clears the mark. **Mark as read** does the same without opening it.

Both are yours alone: nobody else in the conversation sees what you pinned or marked.

## Notifications

New messages arrive live while Messages is open. When it is not, you get a push to the app.
To keep a busy conversation from buzzing your phone, several messages in a row send one
push, and a mention always gets through. While you are using the web console, your phone
stays quiet: you can already see the message there.

Email is never one message at a time. Turn it on and you get one email with what you have
not read 15 minutes after it arrived, across all your conversations. If you read it in the
console or the app first, no email goes out.

- **Choose how you hear:** your notification settings have a **Messages** row under Push and
  under Email. Push is on and email is off to start. In the app, it is under Settings →
  Notifications.
- **Quiet one conversation:** open it, click its name at the top, then **Mute**. In the app,
  use the gear. Muted conversations still count as unread, they just never push or email.
- **Leave a group:** click its name at the top, then **Leave**. Somebody still in it can add
  you back. A people group's conversation has no Leave: mute it, or ask an admin to take you
  out of the group.

Clicking a conversation's name also shows who is in it, a link to the booking or the person's
profile, and every record mentioned in it. In the console you can also right-click a
conversation in the list to mute it, leave it, or open the person or booking.

## Change or remove what you sent

In the console, hover over your own message and open **More**. **Edit** is there for 15
minutes after sending: the message turns into a text box where it sits. Press **Enter** to
save or **Escape** to cancel, and the message then shows **Edited**. **Delete** removes the
message for everyone straight away. In the app, press and hold your message: **Edit** (also
within 15 minutes) puts it back in the message field and the check mark saves it, and
**Delete message** removes it.
