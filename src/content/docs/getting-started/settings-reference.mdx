<Summary>
<Where app="web">Settings</Where> is one page with a left rail of twelve sections in three
groups: **School**, **Billing** and **Connections**. The gear icon in the topbar only
appears for admins and owners, and nobody else can open the page.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins</Roles>

## The twelve sections

| Group | Section | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| School | Organization | Name, contact details, logo, join code, time zone |
| School | Booking preferences | Four rules about who can book what |
| School | Groups | Named sets of aircraft and of people |
| School | Document types | The categories members file documents under |
| Billing | Plan | What you pay AerScheduler |
| Billing | Billing | What your members pay you, through Stripe |
| Billing | Memberships | Club and FBO membership tiers and dues |
| Billing | Instruction rates | Named instruction types and their hourly rate |
| Billing | Cost splitting | How a shared booking divides between payers |
| Billing | Currency rules | Medicals, flight reviews, checkouts and their expiry |
| Connections | Integrations | QuickBooks Online |
| Connections | API keys | Keys for your own software |

The section you are on lives in the page's address, so a link or a bookmark to one section
works.

<Callout type="note">
**Plan** and **Billing** sit two rows apart and are easy to mix up. Plan is AerScheduler
charging your school. Billing is your school charging its members. They have nothing to do
with each other.
</Callout>

## School → Organization

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Organization profile (name, phone, email) | Your school's name and contact details, used on invoices, emails and the join page | Name from the setup wizard, contact details blank |
| Logo | Replaces the AerScheduler mark in the school switcher, and appears to members across the app | None. PNG, JPG or SVG up to 5 MB |
| Join code | The code people enter to join your school. Read-only, with a copy button | Generated by AerScheduler, and it cannot be changed from the console |
| Time zone ("Where you fly from") | What time a 9am lesson actually is for everybody, and the zone your booking emails and push notifications are stamped in | Unset, which makes the schedule fall back to each person's device zone |
| Delete this school | Owner only. Schedules permanent deletion in 30 days. Admins and owners can cancel from the same card while the countdown runs. See [Delete your school](/docs/getting-started/delete-your-school) | Off |

<Callout type="warning">
Set the time zone early. Until it is set, two people can see different times for the same
booking, and **Allow multi-day bookings** cannot be switched on at all: the toggle is
disabled and says what is missing.
</Callout>

## School → Booking preferences

School-wide booking behaviour. Most switches are off by default.

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Instructors can override reservation prices | Gives instructors **Override payment** on a booking, on either surface. Not limited to their own bookings. Admins, owners and dispatchers can always override |
| Members can only book approved resources | Students and renters can book only the aircraft they are approved on. Approve them from the aircraft (**Approve members**) or from their profile (**Compliance → Approved aircraft**). Instructors are not restricted, admins and dispatchers can still assign anybody to any resource, and classrooms are unaffected. Simulators are checked, the same as aircraft |
| Require payment method before self-book | Students and renters need a card on file before booking themselves. Owners, admins and dispatchers can still book anyone, instructor-led bookings are unaffected, and it only applies when Stripe billing is on |
| Flying day | Local hours when same-day bookings are allowed (default 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM). Per-plane override on the aircraft edit screen |
| Allow multi-day bookings | Lets a booking keep a resource overnight, overriding flying day hours until it is back |
| Cancel and edit lock | Hours before start when members cannot cancel or edit. Front desk, instructors, and technicians can still override |
| Late-cancel fee | Optional fee (Stripe) so a member can still cancel inside the lock by agreeing to pay. Staff cancels never charge |
| Max upcoming bookings | Caps how many future bookings one student or renter may hold, including every date in a repeating series |
| Max reservation length | Caps how long a single booking may run |

Currency checks at book (medical, flight review, checkouts) are always enforced when you
configure currencies under Compliance. They are not a toggle on this tab.

See [Booking rules and settings](/docs/scheduling/booking-rules-and-settings) for the full
story.

## School → Groups

Named sets of aircraft (Trainers, Complex, IFR-capable) and named sets of people. Currency
rules are written against them, and reports can filter by fleet group. None exist until you
make them.

<Callout type="warning">
Deleting a group un-scopes every currency rule that pointed at it, leaving a rule that
matches nobody and enforces nothing. The warning appears when you delete, and nothing flags
the inert rule afterwards. Check your currency rules after deleting a group.
</Callout>

## School → Document types

The categories members file documents under, each with **Admin upload only**, **Has an
expiry date**, **Warn this many days ahead** (30 by default) and **Active**. Covered in
[Track medicals, certificates and other documents](/docs/getting-started/member-documents),
including the two edits with a bigger blast radius than they look.

## Billing → Plan

Your AerScheduler subscription: $20 per month per aircraft, the aircraft count, the monthly
total, the status (Trial, Starts on a date, Active, Paused or Legacy plan) and the
**Subscribe** button. A 14-day free trial starts at signup with no card. Simulators and
rooms are free.

<Callout type="note">
Schools that were already billing through Stripe Connect before per-aircraft pricing
launched stay on their existing plan. Their Plan tab reads **Legacy plan** and shows no
prices, so two admins comparing notes will see different screens.
</Callout>

## Billing → Billing

What your members pay you. Owner only: an admin who is not the owner can edit these fields
and be refused on save.

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Stripe Connect (payouts) | Connects your own Stripe account, so close-outs become invoices payable by card or ACH, with payouts to your bank | Not connected |
| Default instructor rate | The hourly instruction rate used when a rating has none of its own | $0 |
| Service fee percent and label | An optional percentage added to invoices, with the label the payer sees | No fee, labelled "Service Fee" |
| Overnight minimum | Least billable time per night an aircraft is kept away. Out Friday, back Sunday is two nights. A booking back the same day is never affected | Blank, so nothing extra is charged |
| Ground members with unpaid invoices | Grounds a member automatically once they reach this many unpaid invoices | Off |
| Dispatchers can manually generate invoices | Lets a dispatcher raise a one-off invoice outside a reservation, from the app | Off |
| Instructors can manually generate invoices | The same for instructors, independently of the dispatcher grant | Off |

See [Turn on invoicing and card payments](/docs/billing/turn-on-invoicing-and-card-payments)
and [Who can do what in billing](/docs/billing/who-can-do-what-in-billing).

## Billing → Memberships

Club and FBO membership tiers with join fees and monthly dues, and optionally a booking
window per tier. A plan can be attached when you invite somebody and when you accept a join
request. Every membership control disappears at a school with no plans, which is most
schools. See [Set up membership dues](/docs/billing/set-up-membership-dues).

## Billing → Instruction rates

Named instruction types (Private, Instrument, Commercial) with a default hourly instructor
rate.

<Callout type="warning">
A lesson cannot be priced until its instruction type has a rate. There is no per-instructor
rate: every CFI teaching a given rating bills at the same figure.
</Callout>

## Billing → Cost splitting

How a shared booking's cost divides between the people on it, with each person getting their
own invoice. With no rules, one person is billed for the whole booking. See
[Set up cost splitting](/docs/billing/set-up-cost-splitting).

## Billing → Currency rules

Requirements like a medical, a flight review or an aircraft checkout: what expires, how it
expires (a fixed date, a number of days or months after sign-off, or with the documents),
which aircraft groups it covers and which member groups it applies to.

<Callout type="warning">
A rule with no aircraft group or no member group enforces nothing. It looks configured and
gates nobody. Create the groups under **Settings → Groups** first.
</Callout>

## Connections → Integrations

School-wide connections. Today that means QuickBooks Online, which syncs paid invoices as
Sales Receipts. Owner only. Personal connections such as Google Calendar live on your own
profile instead. See [Send paid invoices to QuickBooks](/docs/billing/send-paid-invoices-to-quickbooks).

## Connections → API keys

Keys for your own software to talk to AerScheduler, each scoped to one role: admin,
dispatcher, instructor, student, renter or technician.

<Callout type="important">
This screen is the only way to manage keys. An API key can never issue itself a replacement,
so if you lose the last one you come back here to make another.
</Callout>

## Settings that are not in Settings

Four things an admin goes looking for on this page and does not find.

| Setting | Where it actually lives |
|---|---|
| Approve people before they join | <Where app="web">Settings → School → Joining & fleet</Where>, or the booking rules step on the dashboard during setup |
| Locations (an airport or site) | <Where app="web">Facilities → Locations</Where>, first in the rail. Add, edit and remove. The app can only add one |
| Update an aircraft's home base on ramp in | <Where app="web">Settings → School → Joining & fleet</Where> |
