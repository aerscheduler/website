## Check somebody out from the aircraft

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open Aircraft">
    Or open the tail's own record. **Approve members** is on both.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Open the ⋯ menu on the tail and choose Approve members">
    A sheet lists every student and renter. Instructors are not on this list because they
    are never held to it.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Flip the switch beside each person">
    There is no Save button. Each switch writes immediately and confirms with a toast.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check somebody out from their profile

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open People and the person">
    Anyone with the student or renter role can be approved. Instructors can still have a
    checkout recorded, it just does not restrict what they book.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Open Compliance">
    The **Approved aircraft** card lists every tail and simulator.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Flip the switch beside each aircraft">
    Same write as the aircraft sheet. Use this when you are already on the person's record
    after a checkout flight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">
People groups (Settings → School → Groups) scope currency rules such as medicals and
flight reviews. They do not grant or refuse a checkout. An empty Groups tab is normal
and unrelated.
</Callout>

## What the approval actually restricts

That depends on one organization setting, **Members can only book approved resources**, on
<Where app="web">Settings → School → Booking preferences</Where>.

| Setting | What students and renters can book |
|---|---|
| Off (the default) | Any aircraft. The approvals are a record of who is checked out, and nothing is blocked |
| On | Only the aircraft they are approved on, on every surface, checked when the booking is saved |

With the setting on:

- **Students and renters** are held to the aircraft they are approved on.
- **Instructors are not.** A CFI is never checked against the approved list, so turning
  this on cannot ground your dual bookings.
- Owners, admins and dispatchers can still assign anybody to any resource. The restriction
  applies to a student or renter booking for themselves.
- Guest flights and classrooms are unaffected. Simulators are not: a simulator is
  checked against the approved list just like an aircraft.

<Callout type="note">
Turning the rule on at a school that has never used it will stop students and renters
booking. Work through the fleet (or through people's profiles) and approve them first,
then switch it on.
</Callout>

## If somebody cannot book a tail

Approvals are one of several reasons a booking is refused. The others include grounding,
the aircraft itself being grounded, a required card on file, and student-instructor
pairing. See [Why was my booking refused?](/docs/scheduling/why-was-my-booking-refused).
