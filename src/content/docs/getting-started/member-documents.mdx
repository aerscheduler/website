## Set up your document types first

Nothing can be uploaded until at least one type exists. A type is the category a document
is filed under: Medical Certificate, Photo ID, Renter Agreement, and whatever else your
school insists on.

<Roles>Owners, Admins</Roles>

Each type carries four settings:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Admin upload only | Members do not see this type in their own upload picker. An admin files it for them |
| Has an expiry date | Makes the expiry field appear when the document is uploaded |
| Warn this many days ahead | How much notice you want before it expires. The form starts at 30 days |
| Active | Turn a type off to retire it without deleting anything |

<Callout type="note">
**Admin upload only** is about uploading, not reading. It does not hide a filed document
from the member. Use it for the paperwork you want to control the copy of, such as a signed
rental agreement.
</Callout>

## Upload on somebody's behalf

Open the person from <Where app="web">People</Where>, go to the **Compliance** section of
their record, and upload there. Admins can file any type, including the ones marked admin
upload only.

## Who can see whose documents

| Role | Whose documents they can read |
|---|---|
| Owner, Admin | Anybody's |
| Dispatcher | Anybody's |
| Instructor | Their own |
| Student, Renter, Technician | Their own |

An instructor being unable to see a student's medical surprises people, since instructors
can see that student's training record and endorsements. The two are scoped differently on
purpose.

## Expiry warnings

A type with an expiry date and a warning period generates a **Document expirations**
notification the set number of days ahead, by email and by push. Members can turn that
category off for themselves under
[notification settings](/docs/getting-started/notifications-and-emails).

If a document lapses anyway, **Missing or expired documents** and **Medical certificate
expired** are both standard grounding reasons. See
[Ground, archive or remove a member](/docs/getting-started/ground-archive-or-remove-a-member).

## Two things that catch people out when editing a type

<Callout type="warning">
Turning **Has an expiry date** off on a type clears the expiry date on every document
already filed under it. Those dates do not come back if you turn the setting on again.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
Deleting a document type does **not** delete the documents filed under it. The confirm
dialog says so, and it is easy to read past. If you want a category to stop being used, set
it to inactive rather than deleting it.
</Callout>

## Documents are per school

Documents belong to your membership of one organization, the same as your roles. If you fly
at two schools you file your medical at each of them.
