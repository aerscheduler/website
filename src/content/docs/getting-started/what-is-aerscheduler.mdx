<Summary>
AerScheduler runs the day-to-day of a flight school, flying club, or FBO: who is flying
what, when, on which aircraft, what it costs, and whether the aircraft is legal to go.
</Summary>

## The shape of the product

There is one schedule, one roster of people, one fleet, and everything else hangs off
those three. A booking on the schedule pulls an aircraft out of the fleet and a person
out of the roster. When the flight comes back, the same booking produces the invoice,
moves the aircraft meters forward, and ticks off the lesson.

<Callout type="tip">
If you only remember one thing: the reservation is the unit of work. Almost every number
in AerScheduler is derived from reservations, so a booking that was never closed out is
the usual reason a report looks wrong.
</Callout>
