<Summary>
Under Settings → Booking offerings, Calendar visibility sets how much of the schedule
each audience sees when they pick a time on a public /book page or an offering. It does
not change the staff Calendar. Owners, admins, and dispatchers always see the full board.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins</Roles>

## This is not the dispatch board

<Where app="web">Calendar</Where> is still the shared school board. Members who can open
it still see bookings with names, as
[Who can book, edit and cancel](/docs/scheduling/who-can-do-what-on-the-schedule)
describes.

Calendar visibility applies when someone is **choosing a start time**:

- a guest on a public `/book/{slug}/{offering}` page
- a signed-in member looking up times on an offering

Owners, admins, and dispatchers are not in this list. Desk staff always get full detail.

<Where app="web">Settings → Booking offerings</Where>, in **Calendar visibility**. Each
audience has its own dropdown. A change saves as soon as you pick an option.

## The four options

**Open slots only.** They see times they can book. Occupied times are simply missing, and
nobody else's name is shown. If the offering lets them pick an aircraft, those free times
still include the tail so they can choose it. This is the usual setting for guests.

**Blocked, no names.** Busy times stay anonymous: no who, no tail. On the public guest
page those times are omitted rather than drawn as a named flight, so this often looks the
same as Open slots only for guests.

**Own bookings named.** They can see their own flights by name. Everyone else stays
anonymous. If the offering lets them pick an aircraft, a free time can include the tail.

**Full schedule.** Names and tails, closest to what the front desk sees. Use this only for
people you trust with the whole board. Guests almost never need this.

## Who each dropdown is for

**Guests (public booking).** People with no account on a public /book link. Leave this on
Open slots only unless an offering lets them choose a specific tail and you want that
tail on the free times.

**Students, Renters, Instructors.** Signed-in members with that role, when they pick
times on an offering. This does not change what they see when they open Calendar.

**Other members.** Signed-in people who are not student, renter, or instructor. Desk
staff are never this audience.

## What guests actually see today

The public page lists **open start times**. It does not paint other people's bookings
with names, even on Full schedule. Occupied times are left off the list.

If the offering lets the requester pick an aircraft, each free time includes the tail
even on Open slots only. Without that toggle, Open slots only and Blocked, no names keep
tails hidden.

<Callout type="note">
A request still does not hold the slot. Another booking can take the time while the
guest confirms email and you approve. Calendar visibility only changes what they see
while picking, not who gets the aircraft.
</Callout>
