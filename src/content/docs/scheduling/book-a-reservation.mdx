## Before you start

The form can only offer what exists. Make sure the school has:

- At least one aircraft, simulator or room set up, under Aircraft or Facilities.
- The people you want to assign on the roster, under People, with the right roles.
- For a guest flight, the guest's email address. That is where their invoice goes.

## Book from the New reservation button

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Press New reservation">
    Top right of <Where app="web">Calendar</Where>. There is also a global <strong>+</strong> in
    the top bar that opens the same form.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Type a title">
    Something a dispatcher can read at a glance, such as "Pattern work in N12345".
    Titles are capped at 50 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Pick the type, then the resource">
    The type decides which resource picker you get. See
    [Which reservation type should I use?](/docs/scheduling/reservation-types)
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Assign the people">
    Instructor, students, renters, or the guest's name, email and phone.
  </Step>
  <Step n={5} title="Pick the date, start and end">
    Only times when the resource and everyone assigned are free are offered.
  </Step>
  <Step n={6} title="Press Book reservation">
    The modal closes onto the board with the new block in its lane.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Book by clicking an empty slot

Faster, and it fills three fields for you. Click empty space in a lane on the day board, a
column on the week board, or a cell in the month grid. The form opens with the date, the
hour you clicked (starting on the hour, ending an hour later) and that lane's resource
already set.

Clicking a room lane sets the type to Ground and a simulator lane sets it to Sim.

<Callout type="note">
The notice reports, it does not block. Dispatch legitimately books grounded aircraft for
maintenance and ferry flights, so the form tells you what the aircraft's record says and
leaves the decision to you. The server is stricter: a grounded aircraft is refused for
every type except Maintenance.
</Callout>

## Assign the people

Each type caps how many people fit on each side. A dual takes one instructor and up to
four students; a rental takes up to four renters; a solo takes exactly one pilot.

On a booking where several people are billed, **the order matters**. The first person
listed on a side is the leading payer: they are the one billed when your school's rules
say one person pays, and they take the odd cent on an uneven split.

<Callout type="tip">
Booking preferences that limit what members may book do not limit you. From the dispatch
form you can assign anyone to any aircraft, whether or not they are checked out on it.
</Callout>

## Pick a time when everyone is free

The Start dropdown lists 15-minute marks only, and only inside the window where the
resource and every person on the booking are simultaneously free. Picking a start fills in
an end an hour later, backing off if the free window is shorter.

That is why the list sometimes goes short: adding a fourth person to a lesson intersects
their calendar in too. Take somebody off and more times reappear.

If nothing is free that day, the dropdown is empty and a **Next available** link appears
with a date and time. Clicking it jumps the form to that slot.

<Screenshot id="time-picker-next-available" />

<Callout type="warning">
Adding a person after you have already picked a time can invalidate it. The end refits, or
both fields clear. Assign everybody first, then pick the time.
</Callout>

## Rating, repeat and notes

**Rating** is optional and records what the flight was for.

**Repeat** turns one booking into a series of real, separate bookings. See
[Set up a lesson that repeats every week](/docs/scheduling/set-up-a-repeating-booking).

**Notes** are capped at 200 characters. The web form does not count them for you, and
anything longer fails when you press Book reservation.

## What happens after you book

- The block appears on the board straight away.
- Everyone on the booking is sent an in-app notification, an email and a push, subject to their own notification preferences. You are not notified, because you are the one who booked it.
- Anyone with Google Calendar connected gets a calendar event.
- A `Booked` line is written to the booking's timeline.

## If the booking is refused

The server names what is wrong. It never quietly trims a booking down to what would fit.

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| `<name> does not meet currency requirements.` | A medical, flight review or checkout is not current. Adding a current instructor can clear it, if the currency rule allows flying with one. |
| `<name> is grounded.` | An admin has grounded that person. The reason is not shown, because it may be personal. |
| `<name> is grounded for unpaid invoices. Paying them lifts it.` | Exactly what it says. |
| `Resource is grounded.` | The aircraft is grounded. Only Maintenance can be booked on it. |
| `A solo has one pilot.` | Two people on a solo. Book a dual. |
| `The <plan> tier can book up to N days ahead.` | That member's membership plan has a booking window. |
| `This booking would keep the aircraft overnight` | Multi-day bookings are off. See [overnight trips](/docs/scheduling/overnight-and-multi-day-trips). |

Nothing can be booked more than 365 days ahead. The date picker stops you before the
server has to.

Full list, with the way past each one, in
[Why was my booking refused?](/docs/scheduling/why-was-my-booking-refused)
