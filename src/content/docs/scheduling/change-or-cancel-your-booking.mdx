## When editing stops being possible

The **Edit reservation** button disappears once any of these is true:

- The aircraft has ramped out.
- The booking's end time is in the past.
- The booking has been cancelled.

Running late and want to push the return time back? On the web you cannot edit at that
point, because the time picker cannot offer sensible slots around a start that has already
happened. Drag the trailing edge of the block on the calendar instead, or use the iOS app,
which still lets you change the end time.

## Why the edit form looks bigger than the one you booked with

Editing always opens the full dispatch form, with a Title field and every person picker,
even for a student. The form you booked with was the narrowed self-serve version.

That is also the only way to add a second person to a booking you made yourself: book it,
then edit it.

## You can only take yourself off a booking

Saving an edit replaces the crew with whoever is in the pickers. Students and renters may
add or remove **themselves**, and no other student or renter. Trying to remove a classmate
is refused:

> You can only take yourself off this booking. Ask an instructor or the front desk to remove someone else.

The instructor is not covered by that rule. You can still change which instructor you are
flying with on your own booking, which is what Step 3 above is for.

Instructors on a booking can change anyone's seat on it, and so can staff.

## Cancel a booking

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open the booking and press Cancel reservation">
    It is in the panel footer, and in the block's <strong>&hellip;</strong> menu on the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Pick a reason type">
    A fixed list: Weather, Aircraft maintenance, Aircraft unavailable, Instructor
    unavailable, No-show, Student or renter unavailable, Rescheduled to another time,
    Booked in error.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Say what happened">
    The note is required, up to 200 characters. Your school's cancellation report counts
    the reason types and reads the notes.
    <Screenshot id="cancel-reservation-dialog" />
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Press Cancel reservation">
    The booking comes off the board and everyone on it is notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A booking that has already ramped out cannot be cancelled. It has flown, so it needs
closing out instead.

## If there is no Cancel button

Cancelling is allowed for staff, for technicians, and for whoever created the booking.

The web console cannot see who created a booking, so it only shows the button to staff,
technicians and the instructor on the flight. If you booked yourself in and there is no
button:

- Cancel it from the AerScheduler iOS app, which does know you created it.
- Or ask the front desk.

<Callout type="warning">
If you are the instructor on a booking that somebody else created, the web and the app
both offer you a Cancel button, and the request comes back as
`You are unauthorized to make this request.` Ask an admin, a dispatcher or the person who
booked it to cancel it instead.
</Callout>

## Cancelling one of a repeating series

Cancelling any booking in a series adds a choice: **This booking only**,
**This and all later bookings**, or **All bookings in the series**.

Bookings that have already started are never cancelled, whichever option you pick.

## Drag to reschedule on the calendar

You can move a booking you are on by dragging it on the school calendar, the same as
dispatch does. Hovering a block first tells you whether it can be moved. See
[Move a booking to a new time or a different aircraft](/docs/scheduling/reschedule-a-booking).
