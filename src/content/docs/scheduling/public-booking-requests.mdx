<Summary>
Turn on public booking under Settings → Booking offerings, share the link for an
active offering, and approve guest requests from Calendar → Booking requests.
Guests confirm by email first. Members still book with Solo, Dual, Ground and the
rest; offerings do not replace reservation types.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers</Roles>

## What guests get

A public page at `/book/{your-slug}/{offering-slug}`. The guest picks an open time,
enters name and email, and submits a **request**. AerScheduler emails them a
confirmation link. After they confirm, the request appears in the same queue as
member approval requests.

This is not instant booking. The front desk still approves or declines it. Until
you approve, nothing is on the board. If the offering lets the guest pick an
aircraft, that tail is held until the request expires, is declined, or you
approve it. Without pick-aircraft, the desk assigns the plane at approval and
the time is not held.

<Callout type="note">
Students and renters who already have accounts keep using **Book** as they do
today. Public links are for people who are not members yet, typically a
discovery or intro flight.
</Callout>

## Turn it on

<Where app="web">Settings → Booking offerings</Where>

1. Create an offering if you do not have one (name, slug, reservation type such as
   Guest / discovery, eligible aircraft, and a fixed length if you want one).
2. Flip **Allow public requests**. If you have not set a link slug yet, AerScheduler
   fills one from the school name.
3. Copy the public link from the offering's menu (**Copy public link**). Only
   **Active** offerings get a link.

Calendar visibility for **Guests (public booking)** still decides how much of the
schedule the guest sees when picking times. Open slots only is the usual choice.

Public booking lives on the web console only. There is nothing to configure in
the mobile app.

## What the guest does

They open the link. Day view is the default: a month calendar plus start times for the
day they pick (8:00 AM, not a range). On a computer they can switch to a week calendar
or a week of start-time buttons. They can change 12-hour vs 24-hour and the time zone.

On a phone there is only day view: the month sits above the start times, with large tap
targets, and the window scrolls as one page. After they pick a time, the name and email
form appears. Copy on the page says the school must approve it. They then click
**Confirm this request** in email.

To put the same page on your website, first add your site under **Websites that may
embed** (one host per line, like `www.yourschool.com`). `www.yourschool.com` and
`yourschool.com` are different websites: list both if both serve your pages. Then use
the offering menu:
**Copy public link**, **Copy embed code** (inline iframe), or **Copy modal embed** (a
button that opens the page over your site). Leave that list empty to keep the share
link only: other websites cannot put the page in a frame. AerScheduler can still open
it in its own tab, including with `?embed=1` to preview. Do not inject your own CSS or
scripts into the frame. The embed does not set AerScheduler analytics or advertising
cookies, and it does not show a cookie banner.

If they never confirm, the request never reaches the desk. Unconfirmed requests
expire after 24 hours, or sooner if the requested time has already passed.

## Approve or decline

Same place as member requests: <Where app="web">Calendar → Booking requests</Where>.
Guest rows show the name they entered rather than a member account. **Approve**
creates a reservation (usually a guest on the flight). **Decline** emails them
and closes the request.

If the offering lets the guest pick an aircraft, that tail stays occupied (same
as a pending slot offer) until you approve, decline, or the request expires.
Without pick-aircraft, another booking can still take a plane while you decide.
Approve still runs the same conflict checks as a normal booking.

After you approve a public guest, the queue keeps them for 30 days with
**Invite as student** and **Invite as renter**. That sends a normal invitation
to the email they used. The reservation already exists.

## Package price

An offering can use **Charge a package price when booked**, the same collection
style as a staff calendar booking. Approval copies those fields onto the
reservation and Stripe invoices the package amount. It is not a percentage
deposit, and it is not a checkout that exists only on `/book`. See
[Charge a package price when booked](/docs/billing/charge-a-package-price-when-booked).

## Guest page look

Under the public booking card you can set an accent color, light/dark/system,
comfortable or compact density, and rounded or sharp corners. The school logo
is already the header. There is no custom CSS or scripts.

## What can go wrong

| What they see | What it means |
|---|---|
| This page is not available | Public requests are off, the offering is paused, or the slug in the URL is wrong. |
| No open times this week | Guest visibility hides occupied times, or org notice/horizon/buffers leave no window. Try another week, or loosen guest calendar visibility. |
| Check your email | The request is waiting on confirmation. It is not in the desk queue yet. |
| This page cannot be shown here | The page is in a frame on a site that is not listed under Websites that may embed. Add that exact host. `www` and the bare domain are not the same listing. |

The public demo sandbox cannot send guest requests. That is on purpose.
