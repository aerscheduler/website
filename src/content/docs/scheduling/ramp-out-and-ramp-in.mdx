<Summary>
Open the booking and use the Dispatch section. **Ramp out** records the departure Hobbs
and tach and takes the aircraft off the line. **Ramp in** records the return readings, puts
the aircraft back, and moves the booking to Awaiting review. These two steps are what turn
a plan into a billable flight.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Instructors, Students, Renters</Roles>

Staff can ramp any booking. Everybody else can ramp the bookings they are on, and the
maintenance they scheduled themselves.

## Ramp out

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open the booking">
    The section headed <strong>Dispatch</strong> carries a <strong>Not started</strong> badge.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Press Ramp out">
    Both readings are already filled in from the aircraft's current Hobbs and tach.
    <Screenshot id="ramp-out-modal" />
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Confirm or correct the readings">
    Both are required. Readings carry one decimal place, for example 1014.2.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Press Ramp out">
    The badge becomes <strong>In flight</strong>, the aircraft is marked off the line, and
    a Ramped out line with both readings is written to the booking's timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ramp out is refused if the aircraft is already out on another booking, if the aircraft is
grounded (maintenance is the exception), or if somebody on the booking is grounded.

## Ramp in

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open the booking when the aircraft is back">
    The section now asks for the ending readings.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Press Ramp in">
    Hobbs and tach are pre-filled from the recorded out readings.
    <Screenshot id="ramp-in-modal-hours-flown" />
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Type the ending readings">
    <strong>Hours flown</strong> is worked out and shown live as you type, so a mistyped
    digit is obvious before you save.
  </Step>
  <Step n={4} title="Press Ramp in">
    The aircraft goes back on the line and the badge becomes <strong>Awaiting review</strong>.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ending readings below the recorded out readings are refused. The difference between out and
in is what the flight is billed on, so a reading that runs backwards would produce a
negative flight.

<Callout type="note">
The aircraft's own stored Hobbs and tach are only moved forward when this is the latest
booking for that tail. Closing out a flight from last Tuesday does not wind the aircraft
back to Tuesday's numbers.
</Callout>

## Instruction time on a dual

The ramp in modal offers **Instruction time (hrs)**. On a flight it is optional extra
detail, billed at the instructor rate.

On a booking with no aircraft it is the only figure recorded, and it is required. The help
text under the field changes to match. See
[Close out a ground lesson](/docs/scheduling/close-out-a-ground-lesson).

## What locks once the aircraft is out

From the moment a booking ramps out, its start time, its aircraft and its crew are frozen.
They are part of the flight record now.

The **Edit reservation** button disappears from the web console entirely at that point.
The one thing you can still change is when the aircraft is due back, by dragging the
trailing edge of the block on the board. Hovering the block says so.

## The overnight minimum notice

If the booking spanned nights and your school charges a minimum per night away, the ramp in
modal says so live, before you save, in plain numbers: how many nights, what the minimum
is, and what the flight will bill instead of what was flown.

It is repeated at every close-out step until the invoice exists, because the person ramping
the aircraft back in is often not the person who booked it. See
[Book a trip that keeps the aircraft overnight](/docs/scheduling/overnight-and-multi-day-trips).

## A booking with no meters

Nothing ramps in a classroom. A Ground booking, or any booking with no aircraft, never sees
these two steps. Its button reads **Review times**, it asks for instruction time only, and
it goes straight from Not started to Awaiting review in one press.

This keys off the reservation type first, so a ground lesson booked sitting in an aeroplane
still behaves that way.

## If you entered the wrong reading

Open the booking and choose **Correct times**, on the reservation panel in the console or
**Update Details** in the app. On a booking with no meters the console calls it **Correct
instruction time**.

You can only correct the figures the booking already has. A flight ramped in on Hobbs alone
offers Hobbs and nothing else, and a ground lesson offers its instruction time. This is a
correction, not a way to record a flight nobody ramped, so it opens once the aircraft is
back and not before.


<Callout type="warning">
Correcting the times after somebody has already signed off **wipes every sign-off on that
booking**, and everyone has to confirm again with their PIN. Once the last pilot has signed
off, or any invoice exists, the correction is refused outright: void the invoice first.
</Callout>
