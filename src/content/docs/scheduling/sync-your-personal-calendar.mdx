<Summary>
Connect Google Calendar for a live push of your flights, or subscribe with a private link for Apple Calendar and Outlook. Both are one-way: AerScheduler writes to your calendar; changes in your calendar app do not change bookings.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Instructors, Students, Renters, Technicians</Roles>

## Google Calendar

Google pushes each of your reservations into a Google Calendar you choose when a booking is created, updated, or cancelled. Connecting also backfills your next 90 days of upcoming flights.

<Steps>
  <Step n="1" title="Open your calendar settings">
    On the web console: <Where app>Profile → Calendar</Where>.
    In the iOS or Android app: <Where app>Settings → Calendars</Where>.
  </Step>
  <Step n="2" title="Connect Google">
    Tap <strong>Connect Google Calendar</strong>, choose the Google account, and allow calendar access. You should see a Connected badge when it works.
  </Step>
  <Step n="3" title="Pick which calendar to write to">
    Use the <strong>Calendar</strong> picker that appears once you are connected. It lists every Google calendar you can write to, so flights can go on a dedicated "Flying" calendar instead of your default one. Switching moves your upcoming flights off the old calendar and onto the new one.
  </Step>
  <Step n="4" title="Disconnect later if you need to">
    Use <strong>Disconnect</strong> on the same screen. AerScheduler stops writing new events and gives up its access to your Google account. Events already on your calendar stay until you delete them in Google.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">
  Connected before the calendar picker existed? The picker asks you to reconnect first. Tap <strong>Reconnect Google Calendar</strong> and accept the extra permission that lets AerScheduler read your list of calendars. Until you do, flights keep going to your default calendar.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
  Flutter web cannot connect Google Calendar. Use the web console, or the iOS or Android app.
</Callout>

## Apple Calendar and Outlook

These apps subscribe to a private ICS feed. AerScheduler does not need your Apple or Microsoft password.

<Steps>
  <Step n="1" title="Create a subscription link">
    On the same Calendar screen, tap <strong>Create subscription link</strong>. Copy the HTTPS URL, or use <strong>Open</strong> / the webcal link when your device offers it.
  </Step>
  <Step n="2" title="Add it in your calendar app">
    Apple Calendar: File → New Calendar Subscription (Mac) or add a subscribed calendar on iPhone/iPad and paste the URL.
    Outlook: add an Internet calendar / subscribe from URL and paste the same link.
  </Step>
  <Step n="3" title="Regenerate if the link leaks">
    Anyone with the link can see your upcoming flights. Tap <strong>Regenerate link</strong>, then update the subscription in your calendar app. The old URL stops working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What syncs

Events include the aircraft (or sim/room), reservation type or title, airport when known, and who is on the booking. Cancelled flights drop off Google immediately; ICS subscribers see the change on the next refresh.

<Callout type="warning">
  Editing or deleting an event in Google, Apple Calendar, or Outlook does not change the reservation in AerScheduler. Change the booking in AerScheduler if the flight moves.
</Callout>
