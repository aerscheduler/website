<Summary>
Book the lesson as **Ground**, then set **Video call**: paste a Zoom, Meet, or Teams link, or
choose **Add Google Meet** to have one made. With a call, the Room can be **No room**. Everyone
on the booking sees a **Join** button, and the link goes into their calendars too.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Instructors, Students</Roles>

## Add a video call when you book

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Start a Ground booking">
On <Where app="web">Calendar → New reservation</Where>, or the **+** button in the app, set
**Type** to **Ground**. The **Video call** row appears under Room. Only ground lessons offer it.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Choose the call">
<div>
- **Paste a link**: any https link works, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex. A link
  copied without `https://` (for example `zoom.us/j/845...`) is fixed for you.
- **Add Google Meet**: a Meet is made on your own Google Calendar when you save. It needs
  Google Calendar connected first, see below.
- **No video call**: an ordinary classroom lesson.
</div>
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Pick a room, or none">
With a call set, **Room** offers **No room** and the location becomes optional: the lesson
happens over the call. Pick a room as well if some students are coming in person.
</Step>
<Step n={4} title="Save">
The booking shows a **Video call** row with **Join Zoom** (or Join Google Meet) on the web,
and a **Join** button under When in the app, which opens the Zoom or Meet app if it is
installed.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Connect Google to make Meet links

Add Google Meet uses the Google Calendar connection you may already have for syncing your
schedule. Connect it under <Where app="web">Profile & account → Calendar</Where> on the web,
or <Where app="mobile">Settings → Calendars</Where> in the app. No extra Google permission is
asked for. The Meet belongs to the person who booked it, so they are its host.

<Callout type="note">
Making a Zoom meeting for you, the way Add Google Meet does, is coming soon. Until then, create
the meeting in Zoom and paste its link.
</Callout>

## Change or remove the call

Edit the booking. **Video call** opens on the current link (for example "Zoom (current
link)"). Pick **Paste a link** to replace it, **Add Google Meet** to make a new one, or **No
video call** to remove it. Removing the call from a lesson with **No room** asks for a room
again, because a ground lesson has to happen somewhere.

## What else carries the link

- **Calendars.** Google Calendar sync and the Apple Calendar or Outlook subscription both put
  "Join Zoom: ..." at the top of the event, and use the link as the event's location when the
  lesson has no room. See [Sync your schedule to your calendar](/docs/scheduling/sync-your-personal-calendar).
- **The schedule.** A lesson with no room sits in the bottom lane of the dispatch board,
  labelled **Video calls** when that is all the lane holds.
- **Close-out.** Unchanged: press **Review times** and record the instruction time. See
  [Close out a ground lesson](/docs/scheduling/close-out-a-ground-lesson).

<Callout type="warning">
If a Meet cannot be made (Google is down, or your connection lapsed), the booking still saves
and you are told the call was not made. Edit the booking to try again or paste a link, so
nobody turns up to a lesson without one.
</Callout>
