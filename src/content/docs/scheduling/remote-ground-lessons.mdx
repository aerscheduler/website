<Summary>
Book the lesson as **Ground**, then set **Video call** to **Add Google Meet** or **Add Zoom
meeting**. The meeting is made on your own account when you save. With a call, the Room can be
**No room**, and everyone on the booking gets a **Join** button and the link in their calendar.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Instructors, Students</Roles>

## Add a video call when you book

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Start a Ground booking">
On <Where app="web">Calendar → New reservation</Where>, or the **+** button in the app, set
**Type** to **Ground**. The **Video call** row appears under Room. Only ground lessons offer it.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Choose Google Meet or Zoom">
Pick **Add Google Meet** or **Add Zoom meeting**. If neither you nor the lesson's instructor has
that account connected yet, the option says **Connects Google first** (or **Connects Zoom first**):
picking it opens Google's or Zoom's sign-in on top of the form, and once you approve, the call is
picked and you carry on where you were. There is no pasting a link: the call is always made
through one of these, so every link on a booking works.
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Pick a room, or none">
With a call set, **Room** offers **No room** and the location becomes optional: the lesson
happens over the call. Pick a room as well if some students are coming in person.
</Step>
<Step n={4} title="Save">
The meeting is made and the booking shows **Join Google Meet** or **Join Zoom**
on the web, and a **Join** button under When in the app that opens Meet or Zoom.
</Step>
</Steps>

If your organization approves bookings before they go on the schedule, a requested ground
lesson can have a call too. The meeting is made when the request is approved,
and the desk sees on the request that it will be a call.

## Connect your accounts

You can connect straight from the **Video call** menu (above; in the app that is Google, as Zoom connects on the web), or ahead of time:

- **Google Meet** uses the Google Calendar connection you may already have for syncing your
  schedule. Connect it under <Where app="web">Profile & account → Calendar</Where> on the web,
  or <Where app="mobile">Settings → Calendars</Where> in the app. No extra Google permission is
  asked for.
- **Zoom** connects under <Where app="web">Profile & account → Calendar → Zoom</Where> on the
  web. You approve AerScheduler on your Zoom account once.

**Who hosts the call:** the lesson's instructor, when they have that account connected, because
they are the one running it. Otherwise the person who booked it. So a student whose instructor has
connected Google can book a remote lesson without connecting anything themselves.

## Disconnect or remove Zoom

Either way works, and both remove AerScheduler's access to your Zoom account:

- **In AerScheduler:** <Where app="web">Profile & account → Calendar → Zoom</Where>, then
  **Disconnect**. AerScheduler also revokes its access at Zoom.
- **In Zoom:** sign in to the Zoom App Marketplace, open **Manage → Added Apps**, find
  **AerScheduler** and choose **Remove**.

AerScheduler then deletes the Zoom tokens, user id and email it held for you. Meetings it already
made stay on your Zoom account, and their Join links stay on the lessons they were made for. New
lessons cannot get a Zoom call from your account until you connect again.

## Change or remove the call

Edit the booking. **Video call** opens on the current call (for example "Zoom (current
call)"). Pick **Add Google Meet** or **Add Zoom meeting** to make a new one, or **No video call**
to remove it. Removing the call from a lesson with **No room** asks for a room
again, because a ground lesson has to happen somewhere.

## What else carries the link

- **Calendars.** Google Calendar sync and the Apple Calendar or Outlook subscription both put
  "Join Zoom: ..." at the top of the event, and use the link as the event's location when the
  lesson has no room. See [Sync your schedule to your calendar](/docs/scheduling/sync-your-personal-calendar).
- **The schedule.** A lesson with no room sits in the bottom lane of the dispatch board,
  labelled **Video calls** when that is all the lane holds.
- **Close-out.** Unchanged: press **Review times** and record the instruction time. See
  [Close out a ground lesson](/docs/scheduling/close-out-a-ground-lesson).

<Callout type="warning">
If a Meet cannot be made (Google is down, or your connection lapsed), the booking still saves
and you are told the call was not made. Edit the booking to try again, or choose the other service, so
nobody turns up to a lesson without one.
</Callout>
