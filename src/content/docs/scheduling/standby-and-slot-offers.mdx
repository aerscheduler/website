<Summary>
Stand by for a booking you want, or set standing preferences for the kinds of
slots you want. When a matching time opens, AerScheduler sends you a time-limited
offer to accept. It does not rebook you silently.
</Summary>

<Roles>Students, Renters, Instructors, Owners, Admins, Dispatchers</Roles>

## Three ways to ask for a slot

| Kind | Where you set it | What it matches |
|---|---|---|
| One booking | Open that reservation and press **Stand by for this booking** | Only that booking, if it cancels |
| Standing preference | <Where app="web">You → Profile → Standby</Where>, or Settings → Standby on iOS | Days, types, hours, aircraft, and instructors you choose |
| Open window | Same Standby screen | One date range you are free |

Leave a standing field blank to mean any. Pick at least one constraint before you save.

Owners and admins can turn the whole feature off under
<Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences → Offer open slots after a cancel</Where>.
It is on by default. When it is off, cancel recovery does not send offers and the
Standby / Slot offers screens are hidden.

If your school requires a checkout before you book an aircraft, the Standby aircraft list
shows only the fleet you are checked out on, the same rule as booking. Assigned instructors
sort first; you can still prefer any instructor on the school roster.

## Accept or decline an offer

When a matching slot opens, you get an offer with a hold deadline.

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Open your offers">
    On the web, go to <Where app="web">You → Schedule → Slot offers</Where>. On iOS,
    open Slot offers from the notification or from your schedule flow.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Accept or Decline">
    Accept creates the reservation on your schedule. Decline (or letting the hold
    expire) can roll the offer to the next eligible member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turn on **Slot offers & standby** under notification settings (email and/or push). Offers
expire quickly, and with both channels off you will only see them if you open the list
yourself.

## What the front desk does

On <Where app="web">Calendar</Where>, staff can:

- See who is standing by on a reservation
- Press **Offer this slot** on a canceled booking to start or resume recovery
- Open **Pending offers** to withdraw an offer that should not stay held

Withdrawing a pending recovery offer moves the chain to the next eligible member.

## Who actually gets offered

Eligibility still applies. A renter standing by on a dual booking will not receive that
dual offer. Role, checkout approvals, grounding, and schedule clashes all filter the list
before ranking.

For dual, ground, and instructor lessons, cancel recovery first asks the **instructor on
that booking** to confirm they can still teach. Only after they confirm does a claim
offer go to a student or other watcher. Desk-created offers skip that step. If the
instructor declines or the confirm expires, students are not offered that window.

Offers are not sent when the slot starts too soon (under 30 minutes by default) or when there would
not be enough time left to respond before it starts. The hold never runs past the slot
start.

Standing preferences and open windows rank behind a member who stood by on that exact
reservation, so a specific watcher still goes first.

<Callout type="warning">
Accepting an offer books a real reservation. Soft holds from pending offers block other
people from taking the same aircraft or instructor until the hold ends or is withdrawn.
</Callout>
