<Summary>
Stand by for a booking you want, or set standing preferences for the kinds of
slots you want. When a matching time opens, AerScheduler sends you a time-limited
offer to accept. It does not rebook you silently.
</Summary>

<Roles>Students, Renters, Instructors, Owners, Admins, Dispatchers</Roles>

## Three ways to ask for a slot

| Kind | Where you set it | What it matches |
|---|---|---|
| One booking | Open that reservation and press **Stand by for this booking** | Only that booking, if it cancels |
| Standing preference | <Where app="web">You → Profile → Standby</Where>, or Settings → Standby on iOS | Days, types, hours, aircraft, and instructors you choose |
| Open window | Same Standby screen | One date range you are free |

Leave a standing field blank to mean any. Pick at least one constraint before you save.

Owners and admins can turn the whole feature off under
<Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences → Offer open slots after a cancel</Where>.
It is on by default. When it is off, cancel recovery does not send offers and the
Standby / Offers screens are hidden.

If your school requires a checkout before you book an aircraft, the Standby aircraft list
shows only the fleet you are checked out on, the same rule as booking. Assigned instructors
sort first; you can still prefer any instructor on the school roster.

## Accept or decline an offer

When a matching slot opens, you get an offer with a deadline to respond. On iPhone
the push includes **Accept**, **Decline**, and **View**: you can claim or pass
without opening the app first. Tapping the notification body still opens the offer
screen.

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Respond from the notification, or open Offers">
    On iPhone, pull down the banner or open Notification Center and choose Accept,
    Decline, or View. On the web, go to
    <Where app="web">You → Schedule → Offers</Where>. On iOS you can also open
    Offers from the notification body or from your schedule flow.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Accept or Decline">
    Accept creates the reservation on your schedule. Decline (or letting the offer
    expire) can roll the offer to the next eligible member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turn on **Offers & standby** under notification settings (email and/or push). Offers
expire quickly, and with both channels off you will only see them if you open the list
yourself.

## What the front desk does

On <Where app="web">Calendar</Where>, staff can:

- See who is standing by on a reservation
- Press **Offer this slot** on a canceled booking to start or resume recovery
- Open **Pending offers** to withdraw an offer that should not stay open

While an offer is pending, the day and week boards show a dashed **Offer** / **Confirm**
block on that aircraft for the offered window. That time is not free: dragging or booking
over it is refused with a clear reason. The reservation stays reserved for the offered
member until someone accepts, declines, the offer expires, or the desk withdraws it.

Withdrawing a pending offer **stops the chain** and frees the window so the desk can book by hand.
Member decline or offer expiry still moves recovery to the next eligible person.

## Who actually gets offered

Eligibility still applies. A renter standing by on a dual booking will not receive that
dual offer. Role, checkout approvals, grounding, and schedule clashes all filter the list
before ranking.

For dual, ground, and instructor lessons, cancel recovery first asks the **instructor on
that booking** to confirm they can still teach. Only after they confirm does a claim
offer go to a student or other watcher. Desk-created offers skip that step. If the
instructor declines or the confirm expires, students are not offered that window.

Offers are not sent when the slot starts too soon (under 30 minutes by default) or when there would
not be enough time left to respond before it starts. The offer window never runs past the slot
start.

Standing preferences and open windows rank behind a member who stood by on that exact
reservation, so a specific watcher still goes first.

## School offer rules

Owners set these under
<Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences</Where> when offers are on.

### Fill idle time automatically

When this is on, AerScheduler AI looks for open aircraft time that matches standing
preferences and sends the same kind of timed offer cancel recovery uses. It is off by
default. Nobody is booked until they accept. The scanner only looks inside the school flying day
(Settings → Booking preferences, default 6am to 10pm local), skips gaps it already offered or cooled down, and respects a
daily offer budget so the board is not flooded.

### Minimum idle gap / look-ahead

When fill-idle is on, set how long a free scrap must be before it is offered (default 90
minutes) and how many days ahead the scanner looks (default 14). Shorter scraps stay free
for walk-up bookings.

### Max AI offers per day

Caps how many new AerScheduler AI offers the school can create in a local day (default 20),
on top of the max-pending offers cap.

### Quiet hours

Cancel recovery waits through this local window (default 9pm to 7am) before creating an
offer, so a late-night cancel does not reserve the aircraft overnight. The clock is the
**airport time zone**, then the school zone if the airport has none. Desk-created offers
skip quiet hours and still go out immediately.

### Decline cooldown

After someone declines or lets an offer expire, they are not offered another overlapping
window on that same aircraft for this long (default 48 hours). It is not limited to the
same cancelled booking.

### Max pending offers

Each pending offer reserves the aircraft. This cap limits how many of those offers can
be open at once for the school (default 10).

### Max pending offers per person

Separately, one member can hold only this many pending offers at a time (default 2). That
stops a broad standby preference from filling one person's inbox and soft-holding them
across many windows in a single sweep.

### Offer window when slot is within 24 hours

When the slot starts within 24 hours, the offer reserves the aircraft for a shorter time
(default 30 minutes). Keep this short so a last-minute recovery does not lock the plane for
hours. The offer window never runs past the slot start.

### Offer window when slot is further out

When the slot starts more than 24 hours from now, the offer reserves the aircraft longer
(default 2 hours) so the member has time to see the notification and respond. The offer
window never runs past the slot start.

<Callout type="warning">
Accepting an offer books a real reservation. Pending offers block other people from taking
the same aircraft or instructor until the offer ends or is withdrawn.
</Callout>
