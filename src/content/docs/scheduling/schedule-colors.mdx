Open <Where app="web">Settings → Schedule colors</Where>, click the color name beside a
reservation type, and pick a color from the menu. It saves at once, for the whole organization,
on the web console and in the app. **Undo** on the confirmation puts it back.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins</Roles>

## Pick a color

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Open Schedule colors">
In the web console, <Where app="web">Settings → Schedule colors</Where>. Each reservation type
has a row: Dual, Solo, Shared flight, Ground, Sim, Rental, Guest and Maintenance, with its
color's name on the right. The preview at the top shows all of them together, the way they look
on the schedule.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Click the color name">
A menu of twelve named colors opens. The one marked **Default** is the type's usual color.
**Custom…** opens your computer's color picker for any other color.
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Pick one">
It saves straight away and you see "Ground is now Teal". Press **Undo** if it was a mistake.
**Use default colors** at the bottom puts every type back.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Where the colors show

Everywhere a booking is drawn by its type: the dispatch board, week and month views, the type
dots in the booking form and on a booking's page, and the calendar in the iOS and Android apps (version 8.5.9 and later).
Other people pick the change up the next time they come back to the console or the app; nobody
has to sign out.

<Callout type="note">
A color has to be dark enough to read on white, because the booking's title is written in it. If
a color is too pale, it is refused and you are asked for a darker one. In dark mode every color is
lifted a little toward white so it stays visible on the dark board.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
Shared flights use the Rental color until you give them one of their own.
</Callout>
