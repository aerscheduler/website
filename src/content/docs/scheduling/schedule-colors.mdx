Open <Where app="web">Settings → Schedule colors</Where>, pick a color for each reservation type,
and press **Save**. The whole organization sees the new colors on the schedule, in the web console
and in the app.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins</Roles>

## Pick the colors

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Open Schedule colors">
In the web console, <Where app="web">Settings → Schedule colors</Where>. Each reservation type has
its own row: Dual, Solo, Shared flight, Ground, Sim, Rental, Guest and Maintenance.
</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Choose a color for a type">
Click one of the twelve swatches, or **+** to choose any color. The block on the left of the row
shows how a booking of that type will look on the schedule. **Default** puts the type back to the
product's color.
</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Save">
Nothing changes for anyone until you press **Save**. **All default** resets every type at once.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Where the colors show

Everywhere a booking is drawn by its type: the dispatch board, week and month views, the type
dots in the booking form and on a booking's page, and the calendar in the iOS and Android apps (version 8.5.9 and later).
Other people pick the change up the next time they come back to the console or the app; nobody
has to sign out.

<Callout type="note">
A color has to be dark enough to read on white, because the booking's title is written in it. If
a color is too pale, it is refused and you are asked for a darker one. In dark mode every color is
lifted a little toward white so it stays visible on the dark board.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
Shared flights use the Rental color until you give them one of their own.
</Callout>
