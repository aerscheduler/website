<Summary>
Once a flight has ramped in, every pilot on it presses **Confirm review** and types their
own four character PIN. When the last person signs off, the invoice is raised
automatically. A flight nobody signs off is a flight nobody is billed for.
</Summary>

<Roles>Instructors, Students, Renters</Roles>

## Set your PIN first

<Where app="web">You &rarr; Profile &rarr; Security</Where>, under **Confirmation PIN**.
Type a PIN and press **Set PIN**. It has to be exactly four characters.

<Screenshot id="profile-security-pin" />

On the iOS app it is <Where app="mobile">Settings &rarr; Security &rarr; Personal PIN</Where>.

<Callout type="note">
Your PIN belongs to your login, not to one school. If you fly at two schools the same PIN
works at both, and changing it changes it in both places.
</Callout>

Without a PIN you cannot close out a flight you were on, and the booking sits at Awaiting
review and never turns into an invoice.

<Callout type="important">
Typing your PIN **is** the confirmation. There is no second "are you sure" dialog. Your PIN is
your signature on the flight record.
</Callout>

## If something was wrong

A sign-off can be taken back, up until the flight is billed, by the school's staff (an admin
or the front desk) or by the instructor on that booking. They use **Reopen to make a
correction** on the reservation panel in the console, or **Reopen close-out** in the app's
actions menu. If you are the student or the renter, ask one of them. Everybody's PIN on that booking is cleared and they all confirm again once the
correction is made, which is the point: nobody's signature carries over onto numbers they have
not seen.

Correcting a reading or a rate does the same thing on its own, so you do not need to reopen
first to fix a Hobbs entry.

**Who can clear whose signature.** Fixing your own flight is yours to do: if the only PIN on
the booking is your own, correct the reading and enter your PIN again. Clearing somebody
else's signature is a different act, and it is limited to the school's staff (an admin or the
front desk) and to the instructor on that booking. So a student on a dual cannot clear their
instructor's confirmation. Ask the front desk or your instructor to reopen it.

A booking nobody signs off, a maintenance block or a discovery flight with no instructor, has
no signature to clear, so anybody who can close it out can correct it.

Once the flight has been invoiced, or charged to a member's ledger, the sign-offs and the
readings are fixed while that bill stands. Undo the bill first: void the invoice from Billing,
or open the member's Ledger tab, find the flight charge and choose **Reverse**. Either one
puts the flight back within reach, and the money is countered rather than erased, so the
statement still shows what happened.

<Callout type="warning">
  <div>
    Correcting a reading does **not** move the aircraft's own Hobbs and tach unless this
    flight is still the one they read, and it never un-grounds an aeroplane. If a mistyped
    reading tripped an inspection, the correction clears the reminder only when that
    inspection runs on hours alone. An annual or an Airworthiness Directive written as
    "every 100 hours **or** 12 months" stays flagged, because the calendar may have earned it
    and nothing records which clock fired. The app and the console both name any inspection
    left in that state so you can clear it on the Maintenance page.
  </div>
</Callout>

## Everyone on the flight has to confirm

The close-out waits on every person on the booking. A two person dual waits on both the
instructor and the student. Neither one can sign for the other, and neither can an admin:
the PIN is personal, and only people on the booking are offered the button at all.

Anybody looking at the booking who is not on it sees a line saying it is waiting for the
assigned pilots to confirm, and no button.

A maintenance booking carries nobody, so it needs no sign-off and completes on its own.

## What happens when the last person signs

The close-out completes and the invoice (or ledger charge, when How members pay is Account
ledger) is generated automatically. On a booking split between several people, that is one
bill each, not one bill with shares inside it.

**Billed** ticks only once that charge exists. If the meters did not move, the total is
under 50 cents and nothing is posted: Review is ticked and Billed stays grey until you
correct the times and post the charge.

The badge then walks on to **Paid** once an invoice is settled. A ledger charge is already
the money, so there is no separate paid badge.

## You can only sign off once

Afterwards the button is replaced with a line telling you that you have signed off and are
waiting on the other pilot's PIN. If a flight is stuck there, the other person on it has
not confirmed yet. Ask them.

<Callout type="warning">
If one person was somehow booked into two seats on the same flight, the count waits for two
confirmations and that person can only give one, so it sits at 1 of 2 forever. New bookings
are refused when one person fills two seats, but a booking made before that rule can still
be stuck this way. Ask your school to sort that booking out.
</Callout>

## If the recorded times were wrong

Say so before you sign. Once the readings are corrected, **every sign-off on that booking
is wiped** and everybody has to confirm again.

Corrections are made with **Correct times** on the booking in the console, or **Update
Details** in the app. Once the last person has signed off, or an invoice exists, they are
refused entirely and the invoice has to be voided first. See
[Ramp out and ramp in a flight](/docs/scheduling/ramp-out-and-ramp-in).

## Guests never sign off

A guest has no account and no PIN. A guest flight is finished by an admin or by the
instructor on it, with **Close out and bill guest**, and the guest is emailed a link to pay
by card. See
[Close out and bill a discovery or guest flight](/docs/scheduling/close-out-a-guest-flight).
