<Summary>
Booking preferences under <Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences</Where> change how
booking works for the whole school, including the flying day window, multi-day trips, and
opt-in cancel/edit locks, late-cancel fees, and booking caps. Currency checks at book are
always on and live under Compliance, not in this opt-in section. Three more rules live
elsewhere: the booking window on a membership plan, the overnight minimum in billing
settings, and the school's time zone.
</Summary>

## Where booking preferences live

<Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences</Where>. Settings as a whole is admin
only, so an owner or an admin can change these and nobody else can, including dispatchers.

<Screenshot id="booking-preferences-tab" />

Payment-method and multi-day toggles are **off** by default. Flying day defaults to
6:00 AM to 10:00 PM until you change it.

## Flying day

The local hours when aircraft can be booked on a normal day (default 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM).
Same-day bookings must start and finish inside this window. The calendar board uses the same
frame. AerScheduler AI idle offers only look inside it too.

An individual aircraft can override the school hours on its edit screen (for example a
night-capable tail that stays open later). Choose **24 hours** when the school truly books
around the clock.

<Callout type="note">
Multi-day trips skip the flying-day window on purpose: once the aircraft is away overnight,
it is gone until it is back, not free again at open the next morning.
</Callout>

## Allow multi-day bookings

Off, a booking has to start and end on the same local day, and anything that crosses local
midnight is refused.

On, the booking form's date field becomes **Out on** and gains **Back on** and **Back at**, so
a booking can keep the aircraft for a night or a week. A multi-day booking overrides the
flying day hours: the aircraft is simply gone until it is back, rather than free again the
next morning.

<Callout type="important">
The toggle is greyed out, not just refused on save, until your school has a time zone set. The
reason is money. A trip's bill depends on how many nights it spans, and nights can only be
counted in the airport's own zone. If the count fell back to whatever device somebody happened
to be holding, two people booking the same trip would be billed differently. Set a zone under
<Where app="web">Settings → Organization</Where> first.
</Callout>

A flight that ends exactly at midnight is not a multi-day booking. 10pm to midnight is an
ordinary evening flight. 10pm to 12:15am is a night away.

See [Book a trip that keeps the aircraft overnight](/docs/scheduling/overnight-and-multi-day-trips).

## Require payment method before self-book

On, a student or renter booking **themselves** must have a card saved before the booking is
accepted. The refusal reads "Add a payment method before booking. Open Payment methods in your
profile, then try again."

What it does not touch:

- owners, admins and dispatchers booking on somebody's behalf
- a booking where the person doing the booking is not sitting in the student or renter seat,
  for example an instructor booking a dual

<Callout type="note">
This setting does nothing at all until your school has finished Stripe onboarding and card
payments are live. Turning it on before that changes nothing and blocks nobody.
</Callout>

## Members can only book approved resources

A check-out is an explicit approval linking one member to one tail, granted from **Approve
members** on the aircraft or from **Approved aircraft** on the person's record. Students and
renters both appear. People groups are not involved.

On, **students and renters** can book only the aircraft they are approved on. A renter with
no approvals is told they are not checked out on any aircraft and asked to talk to the
school.

Four groups are not checked, on purpose:

- **Owners, admins and dispatchers.** They can still assign anyone to any aircraft from the
  dispatch board, which is what you want when somebody is being checked out in the first
  place.
- **Instructors.** A CFI is not held to the approved list, so turning this on cannot ground
  your dual bookings.
- **Guest flights**, which have no member to check.
- **Rooms.** Nobody is checked out on a classroom.

A **simulator is checked**, the same as an aircraft. A device your school treats as a
checkout item behaves like one.

The rule is enforced when the booking is saved, not by hiding tails in a picker. A student
or renter is refused any aircraft or simulator they are not checked out on, whichever
surface they book from and whatever kind of booking it is, and the refusal names the person
and the tail: "Ana Ruiz is not approved to use N12345." Editing an existing booking is
checked the same way, so a booking made on an approved tail cannot be edited onto an
unapproved one afterwards.

With the setting **off**, nothing is blocked and every member sees the whole fleet. The
approvals are still recorded, they simply do not gate anything.

<Callout type="note">
If your school has never opened Booking preferences at all, this counts as off.
</Callout>

## Instructors can override reservation prices

On, an instructor can adjust the resource rate and the instruction rate on a booking, using
**Override payment** on the reservation panel in the console or **Override Payment** on the
reservation screen in the app. The setting gates both identically.

Admins, owners and dispatchers can always override, whatever this is set to.

<Callout type="warning">
This is not limited to an instructor's own bookings. Switched on, any instructor can
override the rates on any booking in the school. An instructor's copy of the setting is read
from their session, so they see the action the next time they sign in rather than the moment
you save it.
</Callout>

## Booking windows on membership plans

If your school runs membership plans, each plan can carry a **Booking window**: how many days
ahead members on that tier may book. It is the classic club rule, where full members book the
season and associates book inside the week.

Set it on the plan under <Where app="web">Settings → Memberships</Where>. Two things to
know before you do:

- It applies to **every** reservation type, so a club that sets "associates book seven days
  out" meaning aircraft has also limited their ground school and their simulator time.
- It applies even when staff book on that member's behalf, because the rule is about whose
  membership it is, not who typed it in.

The refusal names the plan and the numbers: "The Associate tier can book up to 7 days ahead.
That booking is 12 days out."

A window only applies while the membership is **active**. A pending or lapsed membership has
no window at all, so the restriction disappears rather than tightening.

## The overnight minimum

A floor on billable hours per night the aircraft is kept away. Set the school's figure under
<Where app="web">Settings → Billing</Where>, and override it for one aircraft on that
aircraft's rates.

Without one, a member can keep an aeroplane all weekend, fly 1.5 hours, and owe you 1.5 hours.
AerScheduler discloses the minimum on the booking form and again in the ramp in modal, before
any invoice exists, in the form "This will bill 4.0 hours rather than the 1.5 flown."

A booking that comes back the same day is never affected.

## Booking and cancellation rules

All of these live under <Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences</Where>, in the
**Booking and cancellation rules** section. Every one is **off by default**. Turn on only
what your school needs.

### Cancel and edit lock

When set (for example 24 hours), members cannot cancel or edit a booking once it starts
inside that window. Front desk, instructors, and technicians can still change or cancel it.

Members who try see a clear message naming the hours and asking them to contact the front
desk.

### Late-cancel fee

Optional, and only available when the cancel and edit lock is on and Stripe is connected.
Inside the lock window, a member can still cancel if they agree to the fee. The fee is
invoiced through Stripe before the booking comes off the board. Staff cancels never charge.

If Stripe cannot bill the member, the cancel is refused and the booking stays, so you never
lose the slot without collecting the fee.

### Max upcoming bookings

Caps how many future (not yet ended, not cancelled) bookings a student or renter may hold
at once. Maintenance is not counted. The refusal names the member and the limit.

A repeating series counts every date it would create. If the school limit is 20 and the
member already holds 5 upcoming bookings, a repeat of 16 or more is refused before anything
is booked. The booking form caps “After N bookings” at the school limit and blocks an end
date that would clearly go over.

### Max reservation length

Caps how long a single booking may run. The refusal names both the school limit and the
length they tried to book.

Currency checks at book (medical, flight review, checkouts under Compliance) are separate
from these rules and stay **always on**, as they always have been. Duals can still go when
the currency allows flying with a current instructor who is current.

## Account ledger booking gates

These two fields only appear when the school is on
[Account ledger](/docs/billing/choose-invoice-or-account-ledger). Invoice-each-booking
schools never see them, and saved values do nothing if you switch back to invoices.

Both apply only to **self-book**: a student or renter booking themselves. If they put
another student or renter on a shared flight, that person's balance is checked too (close-out
will charge them). Owners, admins, and dispatchers booking on someone's behalf skip them,
and so does an instructor booking a dual. Same bypass as
[Require payment method](#require-payment-method-before-self-book).

### Minimum credit to self-book

Leave blank to turn it off. A dollar amount means their account must already hold at least
that much credit before they can book. **$0** is a real rule: they cannot book while
owing.

The refusal names the floor and the current balance, and tells them to add funds under
Billing or ask the front desk.

### Maximum owing to self-book

Leave blank to turn it off. A dollar amount is the most they may already owe and still
book themselves. **$0** means any negative balance is refused.

If both a minimum credit and a maximum owing would refuse the same booking, they see the
minimum-credit message (the tighter prepaid rule).

## How far ahead anyone can book

Nobody can book more than **365 days** ahead, whatever their role and whatever their
membership plan says. The date picker simply stops there.

Bookings also snap to 15 minute marks. There is no way to express an 09:07 departure.

## Settings a dispatcher cannot reach

Dispatchers run the board. They can create any reservation type, assign anyone, edit,
reschedule, drag, cancel, ramp out, ramp in and fill in who pays what. What they cannot do:

- open Settings at all, so booking preferences, the school's time zone, the overnight minimum
  and the cost-splitting rules are out of reach
- raise an invoice for a booking by hand
- see financial reports
- override prices, unless they are also an admin

That last one is worth planning around. The cost-splitting rules decide which fields in the
Who pays what panel matter, and the dispatcher filling that panel in cannot read them. Tell
them once.
