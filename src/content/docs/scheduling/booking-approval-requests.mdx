<Summary>
When your school turns on booking approval, members in the selected roles or groups submit a
request instead of booking instantly. Staff approve or decline from the calendar, and an
approved request becomes a real reservation on the board.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Students, Renters, Instructors</Roles>

## Turn approval on

<Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences</Where>, in **Booking and cancellation rules**.

Under **Booking approval required**, tick the roles that must request approval (student,
renter, instructor) and optionally tick **Groups requiring approval** for any people group.

Off by default. With every role and group off, everyone books the way they always have.

Staff scheduling on the dispatch board is unchanged. Owners, admins, and dispatchers still
book directly. Instructors and technicians booking on someone else's behalf also skip the
queue.

## Submit a request (members)

When approval applies to you, <Where app="web">You → Book a flight</Where> shows **Submit
request** instead of **Book**. Fill in the same aircraft, time, and crew fields. AerScheduler
checks the same calendar rules (notice, horizon, buffers, and the rest) before it accepts the
request.

Open <Where app="web">You → Schedule → Requests</Where> to see pending, approved, and
declined requests. You get an email or push when a decision is made if **Booking requests**
is on under notification settings.

Direct self-book is refused with a clear message if your school requires approval and you
try to skip the queue through the API.

## Approve or decline (front desk)

On <Where app="web">Calendar</Where>, open **Booking requests** when the badge shows pending
items. Each row shows who asked, the aircraft, the time, and the reservation type.

Press **Approve** to create the reservation on the board (same checks as a desk booking).
Press **Decline** to close the request without booking.

Approvers need owner, admin, or dispatcher access.

## Notifications

| Who | When | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Desk staff | A member submits a request | Email/push to staff with **Booking requests** on |
| Member | Request approved or declined | Email/push to the member with **Booking requests** on |

Turn the category on under <Where app="web">You → Notification settings</Where> in the
Reservations section.
