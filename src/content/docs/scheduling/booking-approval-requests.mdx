<Summary>
When your school turns on booking approval, members in the selected roles or groups submit a
request instead of booking instantly. Staff approve or decline from the calendar or the Home
**Needs you** list, and an approved request becomes a real reservation on the board. Requests
that are not decided before the requested time passes expire automatically.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Students, Renters, Instructors</Roles>

## Turn approval on

<Where app="web">Settings → Booking preferences</Where>, in **Booking and cancellation rules**.

Under **Booking approval required**, tick the roles that must request approval (student,
renter, instructor) and optionally tick **Groups requiring approval** for any people group.

Off by default. With every role and group off, everyone books the way they always have.

Staff scheduling on the dispatch board is unchanged. Owners, admins, and dispatchers still
book directly. Instructors and technicians booking on someone else's behalf also skip the
queue.

## Submit a request (members)

When approval applies to you, **Book a flight** shows **Submit request** instead of **Book**.
Fill in the same aircraft, time, and crew fields you would for a normal booking. On the web
this is under <Where app="web">You → Book a flight</Where>; in the mobile app, tap **Book**
from Home or the calendar.

Open **Booking requests** to see pending, approved, declined, and expired requests. On the web
that is <Where app="web">You → Schedule → Requests</Where>. In the mobile app, open **Booking
requests** from the menu or after you submit.

Direct self-book is refused with a clear message if your school requires approval and you
try to skip the queue through the API.

You get an email or push when a decision is made if **Booking requests** is on under
notification settings.

## Approve or decline (front desk)

On <Where app="web">Calendar</Where>, open **Booking requests** when the badge shows pending
items. On the mobile app, open **Booking requests** from the calendar toolbar or from
**Needs you** on Home when the count is shown.

Each row shows who asked, the aircraft, the time, and the reservation type. Press **Approve**
to create the reservation on the board (same checks as a desk booking). Press **Decline** to
close the request without booking.

Approvers need owner, admin, or dispatcher access.

<Callout type="warning">
A request does not hold the slot. Another booking can take the time while you are deciding.
If you approve too late, the same conflict checks as a normal booking still apply.
</Callout>

## When a request expires

If nobody approves or declines before the **end** of the requested time window, AerScheduler
marks the request **Expired** and notifies the member. Expired requests leave the pending
queue. They stay in the member's request history so everyone can see what happened.

Staff cannot approve an expired request. The member can submit a new request for a different
time.

## Needs you on Home

Owners, admins, and dispatchers see a **Booking requests** count on Home (and on the web
Overview **Needs attention** strip) when members are waiting. Tap the row to open the
pending queue.

## Notifications

| Who | When | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Desk staff | A member submits a request | Email/push to staff with **Booking requests** on; opens the pending queue |
| Member | Request approved, declined, or expired | Email/push to the member with **Booking requests** on |

Turn the category on under notification settings (<Where app="web">You → Notification settings</Where>
in the web console, or **Settings → Notifications** in the mobile app), in the Reservations
section.
