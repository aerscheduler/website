<Summary>
Every time on the schedule is drawn in the airport's own zone, so a 9:00 AM lesson reads
9:00 AM whether you check it from the front desk or from another state. Admins set the
school's zone under <Where app="web">Settings → Organization</Where>. You can switch your
own view to your own zone under <Where app="web">You → Profile</Where>.
</Summary>

<Roles>Owners, Admins, Dispatchers, Instructors, Students, Renters, Technicians</Roles>

## What airport time means

AerScheduler picks the zone for a booking in this order:

1. the time zone on the **location** the booking is at
2. failing that, the **organization's** time zone
3. failing both, whatever zone the device you are reading it on is in

The aircraft and the instructor are going by the clock on the wall at the field, so that is
the clock the board uses. A 9:00 AM lesson stays 9:00 AM in the day board, the week board, the
booking form and the detail panel, no matter where the person looking at it happens to be.

## Set your school's time zone

<Where app="web">Settings → Organization</Where>, then pick a zone. Owners and admins
only.

<Screenshot id="settings-org-timezone" />

Do this on day one. It is the single setting that makes everybody's schedule agree, and it is
a hard prerequisite for
[multi-day bookings](/docs/scheduling/overnight-and-multi-day-trips), which stay greyed out
until it is set.

## Set a time zone for a second airport

If the school operates from more than one field, give each location its own zone under
<Where app="web">Facilities → Locations</Where>. Edit the location and pick its **Time
zone**. A location's zone beats the organization's for bookings at that field, so a school
with a base in Denver and a satellite in Phoenix labels each booking against the right clock
instead of splitting the difference.

A location with no zone of its own carries a **No time zone** badge in that list, which is
the quickest way to find the ones still falling back to the organization's.

## Choose what you see

Every member controls their own view from the **My time zone** card on
<Where app="web">You → Profile</Where>.

<Screenshot id="profile-time-zone-card" />

Two settings on the card:

| Setting | Options | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Show the schedule in | Airport time (recommended), My time zone | Which clock the whole schedule is drawn in |
| Time zone | Automatic, or a zone you pick | What "your time" means. Automatic follows your device and moves with you |

The default is airport time, and it is the recommendation for a reason. Switching to your own
zone is for the person who genuinely works remotely and thinks in their own clock. If you fly
at the field, leave it alone.

## When a time gets a zone label

A time is labelled with its zone abbreviation, and the detail panel appends your own time, only
when the two disagree **at that instant**. When they agree there is no label, because a label
on every time would be noise.

That instant matters. Phoenix and Denver read the same for half the year and differ for the
other half, so the same pair of people see labels appear and disappear at the DST changeover
without anybody changing a setting.

## If no time zone is set anywhere

With no zone on the location and none on the organization, times fall back to each person's
own device. The schedule then genuinely looks different to different people, and it changes
under one person when they travel. That is the state that produces "the times moved" support
calls. Set the organization's zone and it stops.

<Callout type="warning">
In the Month view, a booking's chip can show a different start time from the one on the day
and week boards. If a month chip and the day board disagree, trust the day board, or open the
booking to read its time in the detail panel.
</Callout>

## Why this matters to the bill

Two places where the zone is not cosmetic:

- **Nights away.** A multi-day trip bills a minimum number of hours per night, and the nights
  are counted at the airport. This is why multi-day bookings cannot even be turned on until a
  zone exists: with the count falling back to a device, two people booking the same trip would
  be billed differently.
- **Local midnight.** A booking that ends exactly at midnight is a same-day booking. One that
  ends at 12:15am is a night away. Which side of the line you are on is decided by the
  airport's clock, not yours.
